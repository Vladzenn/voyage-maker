# Academy openings, responsibility and the ensemble

This implements the author’s handoff discussion and subsequent clarifications. Mandatory morning classes remain. Afternoons include regular preparation, partner work and announced practicals, with breaks. Clubs stay optional and introduce themselves through people and actual trial activities. Academic feedback is qualitative, with concrete consequences and ways to improve, not a new numerical resource.

The author explicitly rejected making classmates a reaction panel for the player. Each student opening introduces a small authored group with its own work and disagreements. Some people are returning students encountered on the way to class; their actual years are preserved.

The welcome and ordinary classroom arrival are setup. The opening includes an actual teacher’s question and stops before the player answers, chooses a partner or finishes the exercise. Students may inspect, argue, revise their own plans or continue working. The player can join an exchange without becoming the room’s designated spokesperson. No compulsory friendship, humiliation, rivalry or romance is authored.

Four finite native assignments follow the actual completion of the first lesson. They use native completion conditions and one set of completion effects. There is no new assessment trigger controller, grade resource or per-conversation state machine. A sincere failed attempt plus actual review can finish an assessment without falsely being called a pass. Serious misconduct or an established pattern can put standing at risk; one awkward answer does not. The existing major academic clock, thesis, familiar examination, foreign expeditions and portal anomaly are unchanged.

**Correction retained:** Find Afternoon Work and Visit the Glowhaven Guild remain in all four academy starts. Their statements are framed as optional rumors. The original completion conditions and rewards remain exact: a work lead does not employ the player, and visiting the public guild hall does not register them or accept a contract. The author explicitly asked to preserve these suggestions; do not remove them again merely because they appear in startingQuests.

The guild opening now includes Karia Morino and Renpei Sugita returning from an already-finished difficult delivery while Tetsuya Kanamori examines damaged equipment. They continue their own business around Komugi’s reception work. This pre-opening NPC job is not another commission for the player and gives the player no reward. The licensure-versus-contract distinction remains intact.

Other starts were reviewed selectively. Faculty appointments can legitimately focus on a new colleague’s subject, room and next lesson. Existing merchant, Brookmeadow, Akitsuhara, Drakenspire and Asterveil premises are retained rather than rewritten wholesale. The two new coastal/abbey openings use the same ensemble principle. Summoned starts retain the actual bond and player independence; Write Your Own and Random remain flexible.

Check that older students stay in their correct years, that four/five names do not become five simultaneous speeches, and that a conversation does not consume an entire day. Confirm the first lesson creates exactly its own assignment once and that an unsuccessful reviewed attempt is not narrated as a pass. Qualitative feedback relies on narrative continuity; no hard numerical grade guarantee is claimed.

Test a new and an already-licensed guild character. The returnees’ delivery must not become a quest, payout or invitation to join their party. The new player’s own request still determines whether they apply, browse or leave.
