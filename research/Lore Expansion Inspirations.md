## Strong candidates for Glowhaven

1. **Paid work and commissions.** Who requests repairs, escorts, retrievals, deliveries, or protection; where people find work; how clients recognize a reliable professional. This creates natural connections to outside NPCs without requiring a universal adventurer guild.
2. **Public magic and the law.** How the city treats illusion fraud, coercion, dangerous casting, property damage, authorized duels, evidence, and magical privacy. Laws should address conduct rather than automatically treating an affinity as a moral category.
3. **Creatures around Glowhaven.** A small local collection for the western woods, farmland, waterways, and coast. Give each habitat, behavior, signs, danger, and practical responses rather than only a combat label.
4. **Competence and reputation.** What residents expect from trained mages, veteran hunters, working guards, and recognized specialists; how fame differs from trust; how knowledge of a deed actually travels.
5. **Peoples, languages, and community life.** How different lifespans, family ties, migration, language, and local customs affect daily encounters. Preserve individual variation rather than assigning a personality to every member of a species.
6. **Belief and superstition.** What people think the Evertree means, how reverence differs from scholarship, and the small observances surrounding luck, death, travel, and magical work. Belief need not automatically establish an objectively confirmed deity.
7. **Hospitality, food, and gifts.** A few distinctive local habits around invitations, apologies, courtship, hosting, and sharing a meal. This gives shopkeepers, hosts, cooks, performers, and residents reasons to matter socially.
8. **Communication and travel habits.** How letters, messages, warnings, invitations, and news move between neighborhoods and nearby communities. Adapt the social function of the reference systems to Glowhaven rather than copying modern apps or another world's transport network.

Paid work and public magic law are the strongest immediate choices for defining outside NPC roles. Creature lore and everyday customs would then add reasons to explore and revisit their places.

## Export convention

World Lore entries present setting facts and meaningful consequences. Production comments such as TBD, not yet defined, reserved for development, or planned future content stay in authoring documents. In-world mysteries can remain unknown to characters without announcing the author's unfinished work.
