# Draco Malfoid: research and adaptation notes

**Prepared:** 28 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Support the requested sample character, Ryūka Marufuji, with a recognizable and behaviorally specific interpretation of Malfoid fanon. This is research for a proposed character, not an addition to the active roster.

## What can be called accurate

Malfoid is the female Draco fan interpretation the user requested, not simply the original male character with a different name. There is no single author, definitive continuity, or universally agreed first name. The most defensible target is the recurring personality in the widely circulated illustrations and short comics, while keeping the darker or more elaborate developments of particular fan stories separate. Know Your Meme documents the fan-art phenomenon; credited galleries and comic guides identify the individual works behind its familiar expressions and relationship jokes. [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/malfoid-female-draco-malfoy), [credited art collection](https://malfoid.fun/fan-art).

The useful shared foundation is an arrogant, verbally provocative, status-conscious rival who keeps trying to secure the attention of someone she insists is beneath her notice. Embarrassment, jealousy, and pride make her interest comically legible. The longer adaptations develop that tension differently. Avrana's own description emphasizes a refused offer of friendship, repeated approaches, and a gradual romance; its family-abuse and war material belongs to that particular adaptation. COCOLEMON's game explicitly identifies itself with branching romance and darker genre elements. Neither game's additional plot should be mistaken for universal Malfoid history. [Avrana's creator page](https://avrana.itch.io/malfoid), [COCOLEMON's creator page](https://ruizukun-dev.itch.io/malfoid-dating-sim).

## Evidence and adaptation decisions

These are qualitative patterns in a representative selection, not measured traits of every fan depiction. Interpretations are marked as such.

| Pattern | Evidence reviewed | Consequence for Ryūka Marufuji |
|---|---|---|
| Provocation is an opening move. | Redteneri's credited September illustrations place her in a mocking exchange; the Japanese roundup transcribes the academic and ballet taunts. | She notices what a rival is doing, approaches, and offers an insult tailored to it. She does not wait silently for someone to discover her softer side. |

## Visual identification

The recurring visual cues are long pale or platinum-blond hair, pale gray or cool-toned eyes in many versions, polished school clothing, and a haughty expression that readily becomes a pout or blush. Green and silver are familiar source colors; earrings and a wand appear in several credited designs. Exact eye color, hair arrangement, proportions, and accessories vary by artist. The galleries themselves demonstrate that variation, so one drawing cannot establish every feature. [Credited art collection](https://malfoid.fun/fan-art), [additional attributed examples](https://malfoid.com/fan-art/).

The user's subsequent reference screenshots and direction refine the sample: retain long platinum-blond hair, visible hoop earrings, a wand, academic layers, and a conventional necktie; move her to **Dragon Hoard Academy** with a **yellow tie** and yellow-and-gold school trim. Give her **prominent crimson-red eyes, dark smoky eye shadow, sharp eyeliner, a visibly prickly tsundere expression, and one hand on her hip**. The red irises are an explicit adaptation choice, not a claim that every source design has red eyes. An annoyed pout, narrowed eyes, and betrayed embarrassment should dominate the next portrayal. Keep a fully clothed adult student design and the current portrait framing and headroom requirements.

**Latest visual revision:** After the red-eyed Dragon Hoard portrait, the user requested emerald-green eyes and larger black hoop earrings. These replace the earlier crimson eyes and silver hoops in the current sample and portrait direction. The dated research and earlier generation prompts retain their historical wording.

## What must not be flattened or assumed

- **Not an emotionless aristocrat:** the expression changes, petty reactions, and obvious bids for attention are central to the selected comic interpretation.
- **Not a secretly faultless sweetheart:** this adaptation preserves actual snobbery and hurtful choices. Being interested in someone does not make every slight harmless.
- **Not hostility equals attraction:** an insult is not proof of a crush. Affection becomes plausible through repeated voluntary attention, remembered details, practical care, and reciprocal interest.
- **Not a guaranteed obsession with the player:** the source's recurring partner is a particular character. A new world needs to earn an equivalent relationship through play rather than assign it to every player automatically.
- **Not every darker fan plot at once:** an abusive parent, a war assignment, a fixed arranged marriage, severe dependency, or a particular traumatic incident is not established for the entire fandom. Those are optional developments, not compulsory depth.
- **Not instantaneous rehabilitation:** the proposed accountability arc is original connective material for this world. It is not claimed as a universal fanon redemption ending.

## Fit with the current world

- **Name:** Ryūka Marufuji gives a dragon-associated first-name echo and a loose surname echo of Malfoy while keeping the project's First name Last name convention. The spelling is a proposed Japanese-style identity, not a transliteration of Draco Malfoid.
- **Academy:** Dragon Hoard, per the user's revised direction. Its exacting inherited traditions and work with relics fit her pride in preparation and her family's interest in fine enchanted goods. This is an individual connection, not a claim that all Dragon Hoard students share her snobbery. Use its established yellow-and-gold identity and current faculty rather than the earlier Hydra Head proposal.
- **Age and standing:** Human, twenty, second year. This is an adult interpretation of a school-rival archetype, consistent with the existing adult student cast and four-year program.
- **Affinity:** Arcane, selected for controlled wand dueling, bolts, small constructs, and wards. The affinity-count draw was 11 on a uniform 1–100 roll: one affinity under the guide's 65/25/10 distribution. The school was chosen deliberately after that draw. Cold manners do not require Ice affinity.
- **Wand:** A genuine mana-bearing control catalyst, not a universal battery or source of automatic mastery. Losing it reduces her practiced precision but does not erase body casting.
- **Prejudice:** Use household status, private tutoring, expensive equipment, and acquired manners as the basis of her class bias. A hereditary magical caste or imported blood-purity doctrine is not established by current world lore.
- **Authority:** Her family's money and contacts do not grant her judicial authority, automatic admission privileges, access to sealed records, or the right to order faculty around.

## Research coverage and limits

The initial research reviewed the trend documentation, attributed illustration records, the ten-comic reading guide, Grin2702's separately indexed comic, additional creator records, and the public creator descriptions of two visual novels. The visual novels were not played through, and long fanfictions were not exhaustively read. Direct retrieval of Redteneri's two posts and Grin2702's post was blocked by X; the browser and image-preview environment also failed to initialize. Those initial external-artwork details therefore rely on credited records and descriptions, not a claim that every original panel was visually inspected. The user subsequently supplied two visible screenshots directly in chat; these were inspected for the revised visual direction above. The user's explicit red-eye and yellow-tie choices govern the adaptation.

Some aggregation pages disagree about dates and details. Historical claims are not needed for the card and were not used to reconcile those inconsistencies. The adaptation rests on convergent personality patterns rather than popularity counts, a claimed universal timeline, or one creator's private continuity.

The Marufuji household, Ryūka's specific ambitions, spell names, equipment design, local relationships, and quest incidents are **new fictional proposals**. They are not claims about Malfoid fanon or existing world canon.
