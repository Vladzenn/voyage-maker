# NPC AuthorSeeds — Research and Revamp

## What Voyage documents

The [community AuthorSeeds reference](https://wiki.unofficial.voyage/other/authorSeeds) says that one named writing-tone seed is selected at random and supplied to generateNPCDetails for strong, elite, boss and mythic tiers. Lower tiers do not receive these seeds or character archetypes. The section is a map of labels to directive strings, not a package of simultaneously applied character fields, a quest generator, a level table or a weighting system. This is documented community behavior, not a model-obedience guarantee or a live verification of this world.

Five short directive lines per entry are a documented convention. The suggested pool is 10–20 entries, not a hard quota. A key need not be an author's name. Generic dispositions and tone lenses are supported; labels do not need to quote or imitate a living author. Shared requirements belong in the applicable NPC instructions so they can also reach characters without a seed.

## Observed exports and screenshot

The previous Schools of Magic export contains one valid entry, Glowhaven Ensemble Character Craft. It is useful general guidance but cannot supply a varied random choice: each eligible selection receives the same long checklist. Because ordinary low-tier NPCs do not receive AuthorSeeds under the documented rule, that entry cannot by itself be the universal NPC-writing policy.

Both local Luxcellia exports inspected contain an older 37-entry collection: nineteen author-name presets and eighteen plot premises. The screenshot supplied in this chat instead shows ten focused labels—The Want, The Bond, The Pride, The Pleasure, The Friction, The Status, The Initiative, The Humor, The Ordinary, The Contrast. Their full current values were not supplied; this review does not claim to have inspected those ten descriptions. The structural lesson is useful: choose a focused lens. A plot-premise seed is a poor default for an ordinary person if it invents a secret arc, cosmic exception or forced relationship.

## Implemented pool

Sixteen original lenses replace the single entry:

1. What They Want Today
2. A Bond With Weight
3. Pride in the Work
4. Small Private Pleasures
5. A Flaw That Costs
6. The Face of Authority
7. Someone Who Acts First
8. Comedy of Conviction
9. An Ordinary Life
10. A Comfortable Contradiction
11. The Learned Hand
12. Courtesy With Teeth
13. Ambition Without Apology
14. Care in Particulars
15. A Different Worldview
16. Under Pressure

Each is five short directives. They describe how to reveal an individual rather than mandating an event. They do not form sixteen required components, assign a power tier, create another affinity, force romance, invent a nationality or grant an ability. The shared generateNPCDetails scope instruction tells the model that the selected lens is subordinate to the actual role, record, body, knowledge and character format. Nonhumanoid creatures keep their cognition and anatomy. Lower-tier characters still follow the shared concrete-behavior and voice rules. Existing approved cards are not rewritten.

The pattern fits this setting's bright ensemble comedy, real selfishness, capable adults, ordinary pleasures, research, work, rivalry and dangerous creatures. It allows kindness and cruelty without redeeming every flaw, and ordinary aspirations without a mandatory hidden wound. Strong-tier characters can want an ordinary evening without losing their actual power.

## Limits and tests

This update validates structure and scope, not literary quality. Test newly detailed strong NPCs across several roles and inspect whether the actual role survives the randomly selected lens. Test an average merchant separately: its useful behavior must come from shared NPC instructions, not an assumption that it received a seed. A boss animal should not acquire a human job, dialogue or romance. A previously encountered or authored NPC should keep its identity and record rather than becoming a fresh archetype. No live model samples were generated during this update.
