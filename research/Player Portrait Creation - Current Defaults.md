# Player Portrait Creation — Current Defaults

For portraits generated through Voyage character creation, the Crime and Creation update supersedes the earlier rigid camera/headroom recipe. The manual NPC portrait workflow remains a separate authoring process.

Default to a premium fantasy RPG anime character illustration: fine confident linework, expressive individual anime eyes, layered painted shading with soft gradients, detailed illustrated hair and clothing, luminous directional light and atmospheric depth. Give the player the visual care of a leading cast member without changing their body, age, status, equipment or power. Keep the whole head and a waist-up or hip-up view. Use a pose and comfortable space around the head that suit the person rather than a fixed headroom percentage, compulsory standing pose or identical light rig.

The player may optionally choose a style, crop, camera angle and backdrop. Missing choices use a fitting default; they do not require extra clarification. Compatible written appearance takes precedence. The usual waist/hip-up and natural viewpoint choices appear under Default. Explicit bust, close-up, full-body or dramatic viewpoint selections override that default and should visibly affect the image. A deliberate close-up may trim outer hair or hat edges while keeping the face legible. Backgrounds style the picture and never change location, property, affiliation or travel. Clothes and optional Accessories & Outer Layers do not grant actual armor or equipment. Preserve the body, skin, hair, eyes and real carried gear. A selected bald scalp has no invented head hair regardless of hair-color selections.

Beastfolk are mostly human in face and build with selected animal features. More animal-like bodies are rare explicit exceptions. Fae form and adult height vary; a short adult retains adult facial structure and proportions. Do not infer universal wings, flight or shrinking.

Avoid sculpted or beveled 3D facial edges, plastic highlights, realistic pores, photographic hair and ray-traced material treatment. Dramatic lighting and rich detail should still read as a drawing. No text, logos, watermark, interface, border or character sheet.

Runtime owners are imagePromptConfiguration.npcs and the two Appearance-related leaves under generateCharacterBackground. Local guides, file-save rules and authoring references are not copied into public descriptions or the final picture.
