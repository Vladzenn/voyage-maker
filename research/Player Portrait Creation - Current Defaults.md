# Player Portrait Creation — Current Defaults

For portraits generated through Voyage character creation, the Crime and Creation update supersedes the earlier rigid camera/headroom recipe. The manual NPC portrait workflow remains a separate authoring process.

Default to a detailed 2D fantasy-anime character illustration with the presence of a main-character key visual. Use expressive drawn eyes, simplified anime nose and mouth, drawn facial contours and clean colored shadow shapes with selective soft transitions. Put intricate detail into the hair, costume and setting; avoid sculpted facial planes and airbrushed realistic skin. Preserve the actual body, age, status, equipment and power. The unselected framing default is hip-up: show the complete head, torso, waist, both hips and the upper thighs below the belt. Pull the camera back enough to include that lower outfit; a crop at the waist is waist-up. Use a fitting pose and comfortable headroom without a fixed percentage, compulsory standing pose or identical light rig.

The player may optionally choose a style, crop, camera angle and backdrop. Missing choices use the default without extra clarification. Compatible written appearance takes precedence. Waist-up remains an explicit tighter option alongside Hip-up in Default. Explicit bust, close-up, full-body or dramatic viewpoint selections override the default and should visibly affect the image. A deliberate close-up may trim outer hair or hat edges while keeping the face legible. Backgrounds style the picture and never change location, property, affiliation or travel. Clothes and optional Accessories & Outer Layers do not grant actual armor or equipment. Preserve the body, skin, hair, eyes and real carried gear. A selected bald scalp has no invented head hair regardless of hair-color selections.

Beastfolk are mostly human in face and build with selected animal features. More animal-like bodies are rare explicit exceptions. Fae form and adult height vary; a short adult retains adult facial structure and proportions. Do not infer universal wings, flight or shrinking.

Avoid sculpted or beveled 3D facial edges, plastic highlights, realistic pores, photographic hair and ray-traced material treatment. Dramatic lighting and rich detail should still read as a drawing. No text, logos, watermark, interface, border or character sheet.

Runtime owners are imagePromptConfiguration.npcs and the two Appearance-related leaves under generateCharacterBackground. Local guides, file-save rules and authoring references are not copied into public descriptions or the final picture.
