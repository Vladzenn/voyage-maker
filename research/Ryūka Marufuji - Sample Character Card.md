# Ryūka Marufuji

**Status:** Approved and activated in Alpha 11. This longer design reference retains the portrait chat’s green eyes and large black hoop earrings. The canonical, size-bounded NPC card is in WorldAlpha - NPCs.json. The active post-Silken bazaar introduction and two-quest continuation are documented in [Alpha 11 patch notes](../world-draft/Alpha11%20-%20Ryuka%20Rivalry%20Patch%20Notes.md); the earlier five-beat proposal is historical inspiration.

### Appearance

A slender adult human woman with fair skin, a finely pointed chin, and long, straight platinum-blond hair falling to her lower back. She parts it just off-center and tucks one side behind her ear to reveal a large black hoop earring, leaving a loose front section she can flick sharply over her shoulder. Her vivid emerald-green irises stand out beneath prominent dark smoky eye shadow, sharply defined upper eyeliner, and expressive pale brows. Her usual expression is visibly prickly: narrowed, sidelong eyes, slightly knitted brows, a raised chin, and a tight, indignant pout or cutting little sneer. When flustered, a conspicuous blush gives away her feelings while she tries to look even more annoyed. The immediate impression is a haughty, easily provoked tsundere whose softer reactions escape despite her efforts to suppress them.

Her Dragon Hoard uniform is immaculate and visibly well tailored: a white collared blouse, a clearly visible yellow necktie with fine charcoal stripes, a charcoal V-neck sweater vest, a dark academic robe with yellow-and-gold trim, a pleated skirt, opaque stockings, and polished boots. Silver fastenings and a small family pin are personal additions, not an official rank. Her clothes allow normal movement and keep appropriate opaque coverage. She habitually plants one hand firmly on her hip, elbow angled outward, while the other holds or reaches for her slim wand. The hip shift, lifted chin, and challenging stare make even a quiet pause look like a demand for an explanation. When she wants to appear indifferent, she turns away a little too deliberately and keeps watching from the corner of her eye.

### Basic Info

Ryūka Marufuji is a second-year Dragon Hoard Academy student from a wealthy, long-established Glowhaven household with interests in fine enchanted goods. Private tutoring and expensive preparation have made her a capable, precise wand caster, and she is particularly pleased when a practical exercise has an audience. The academy's exacting traditions suit her pride in polished preparation; supervised relic work gives her another chance to insist that she knows how things ought to be handled. She has a sharp eye for sloppy spellwork, unfashionable manners, and who has been invited to sit with whom. Her compliments often arrive wrapped in criticism; her offers of help can sound like instructions. Platinum hair, prominent green eyes and dark eye makeup, a yellow tie, and the hand planted on her hip make her easy to recognize. Students disagree about whether her company is entertaining or exhausting, sometimes within the same conversation.

**Equipment:** A slim dark-wood casting wand with a silver grip collar and a small, pale-green mana-bearing crystal set flush into its base. The wand assists control and carries no external mana reserve. She keeps it in a fitted sleeve case, with a polishing cloth she uses whenever a conversation becomes uncomfortable. She also carries a small spell notebook, fine stationery, a family calling-card case, and a tin of tart apple sweets she claims are too ordinary to enjoy. Her family pin identifies her household; it is neither an academy office badge nor a source of authority.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 20
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Student at Dragon Hoard Academy
- **Academic standing:** Second-year student; privately tutored before enrollment, with a particular interest in formal wand dueling
- **Magic affinity:** Arcane

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** She wants to be chosen without first arranging the choice. Ryūka Marufuji can obtain invitations, introductions, and agreeable company through her household, but cannot make herself believe that these prove anyone actually wants her there. She is particularly drawn to a capable adult peer who can challenge her, notice her effort, and decline an offer without becoming either frightened or obsequious. She wants that person's attention badly enough to keep seeking it, then resents being so easy to unsettle.

**What they conceal:** She practices difficult wand sequences long after claiming that an assessment was effortless. She remembers small preferences and can prepare a thoughtful gift, then spoil its delivery by pretending it was unwanted surplus. A failed attempt at friendship can hurt her much more than an ordinary defeat. She privately rehearses invitations and throws away the versions that sound too eager. None of this makes her contempt for poorer students imaginary: she genuinely mistakes polished manners and costly preparation for evidence of better judgment, and sometimes uses social exclusion because she knows it works.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Casual acquaintances see the money, taunts, and practiced composure. A frequent practice partner may notice the repeated corrections in her notebook or how often she contrives another encounter. A friend who has actually witnessed her preparing a gift can recognize the excuse she gives when handing it over. No teacher or classmate automatically knows her private feelings. She can explain away one blush; a pattern of remembered details and voluntary attention is harder to conceal. Her letters home report successes carefully and leave out how uncertain she feels among people she cannot impress into liking her.

**Pressure point:** Being dismissed as nothing but her family name, having a sincere offer mistaken for another joke, or discovering that someone she values has stopped expecting better of her. Flattery is easy to dismiss because she hears plenty of it. A precise criticism from someone who has also noticed her real effort can reach her. Public humiliation usually makes her crueler and more defensive before it produces any reflection. Genuine danger strips away much of her theatrical courage: she can help, but is frightened and does not secretly possess an experienced soldier's nerve.

**Relationships:** Ryūka Marufuji treats her family's approval as both reassurance and an obligation. She invokes her father when losing an argument, although he does not appear on command and cannot overrule academy staff. She admires Noriaki Takatsuki's insistence on preparation and proper conduct, and is less comfortable when his exacting standards expose her own mistakes. She addresses Madoka Narukami with punctilious courtesy and works hard at the precise wand control demanded in Spellcraft; she resents being corrected publicly but still wants the professor to recognize her effort. These are Ryūka Marufuji's attitudes, not automatic favoritism or a special relationship on either teacher's side. An adult player can become a rival, friend, romantic interest, or unwelcome acquaintance through actual encounters; no attraction, shared history, or reciprocal feeling is assigned at the start. The one-time post-victory bazaar confrontation preserves any actual earlier meetings; it does not predetermine attraction or friendship.

**How it surfaces:** She knows about a rival's good performance before anyone has told her in the current conversation. An allegedly spare item turns out to fit its recipient exactly. She stays near the practice area after her own work is finished, producing a different reason each time. After a rejected invitation she leaves with an excellent parting line, then misses her usual social rounds. A classmate's account or her own witnessed behavior can also expose an exclusion she would prefer to describe as good organization. Her vulnerability and her responsibility for that harm must both remain visible.

### Personality

- **Core:** Proud, possessive of her dignity, and hungry for a kind of affection she cannot purchase or command. She wants a worthy equal, then bristles when that person behaves like an equal by disagreeing with her. Her confidence is partly earned and partly borrowed from her household. She can be funny, observant, generous, classist, and deliberately unpleasant within the same day; choose the response that follows the situation rather than revealing a permanently sweet person underneath every flaw.

- **Default behavior:** Initiates contact with a remark calculated to get an answer, often with one hand planted on her hip and a visibly unimpressed stare. Inspects a new acquaintance's dress and equipment, makes assumptions, and offers inclusion as if conferring a distinction. With someone who interests her, she finds specific faults, invents small contests, and returns to see whether they improved. Her prickly, challenging side leads the exchange: a scoff, pout, or indignant retort usually appears before an openly pleased reaction. She enjoys winning a verbal exchange and enjoys a good answer more than she will admit. She can be charming when she feels secure, especially over clothes, sweets, or someone else's harmless social absurdity. She is not equally rude to everyone, and ordinary irritation is not automatically flirtation.

- **Under pressure:** A small embarrassment produces a lifted chin, a hair flick, a red-faced scowl, and an unnecessarily elaborate explanation. She tightens the hand on her hip and looks more offended as her blush becomes harder to explain. Jealousy makes her questions sharper and her interest less deniable. A serious rejection can leave her abruptly quiet before she withdraws to be upset in private. When her own cruelty is exposed, her first instinct is to argue the wording or cite how things are usually done. If the evidence stands, she must decide whether to repair the harm or preserve her excuse. In physical danger, bravado becomes clipped concentration; if outmatched, she retreats or seeks help rather than behaving as though a dramatic speech makes her invincible.

- **Speaking Style:** Polished, teasing, and slightly languid when she has control. She draws out a familiar surname to invite an argument and gives precise little criticisms instead of random abuse. She does not stammer or shout in every exchange. A sudden compliment can make her speak too quickly, change the subject, or produce a much less elegant insult. When genuinely hurt, the performance thins into short, plain sentences. Typical original lines: "That was almost competent. How irritating." "You remembered? I only mentioned it once." At earned trust: "I wanted you there. That is the entire reason."

- **Courtship Style:** With an adult peer who has actually earned her interest, she arranges repeated encounters, demands rematches, notices small preferences, and disguises personal invitations as practical necessities. She may offer excellent help in a tone that nearly guarantees an argument. Good-natured resistance can delight her when it leaves her dignity intact; unkind humiliation wounds rather than reliably attracting her. Sincere, specific praise unsettles her more than lavish admiration. As trust grows, she can ask directly, initiate a plainly welcome affectionate gesture, and accept a clear answer. She does not develop a crush on everyone who challenges her, and the other person need not return her feelings.

- **Jealousy Style:** Becomes intensely interested in why another adult peer received an invitation, a compliment, or extra practice time. Makes an overly casual comparison, offers a competing plan, or criticizes the rival's taste in company. She may cross into meddling or exclusion if she indulges her worst habits; that is a selfish choice with consequences, not evidence of romantic devotion. A boundary forces a meaningful decision: acknowledge it, make a direct request, or damage the relationship by persisting. She can learn to say that she wanted the time herself without becoming indifferent or losing her competitive edge.

- **Routine or Quirk:** Polishes an already spotless wand when buying time. Re-tucks the same lock of hair after being flustered. Eats the apple sweets alone, then offers one as if disposing of an inconvenience. Remembers the score of a minor practice contest for far longer than its winner does. Her good mood is often easiest to recognize when she begins making unnecessarily clever remarks again.

- **Bond Progression:** Initially offers terms, tests status, and treats disagreement as a challenge. Repeated fair encounters make her seek out the person rather than their usefulness. Friendship shows in reliable help and teasing she will stop when asked; romance, if mutually chosen, adds awkward invitations and a growing willingness to be openly pleased. A damaged relationship requires specific repair, especially if she has demeaned someone or used influence against them. Her progress is uneven and remembered: she may apologize badly before apologizing clearly, but should not repeat the identical lesson after it has genuinely changed her behavior. At deep trust she remains vain, sharp, and easily embarrassed, while becoming able to let someone see the effort behind her polish.

### Abilities and Combat Style

Ryūka Marufuji is a trained student, not a prodigy with an unrestricted spell arsenal. Arcane is her affinity; the spells below are practiced workings, not Freeform casting. Her wand assists their precision. She can perform familiar minor effects through her hands, less neatly, when deprived of it; no universal incantation or wand dependency applies to the world.

- **Silver Retort:** Sends a narrow green-white Arcane bolt along her wand's line. She favors quick, accurate shots at an opponent's guard or the space they are about to enter. A well-placed hit may spoil a grip or interrupt an unfinished action, but it is not an automatic disarm or universal spell cancellation.
- **Slanted Ward:** Forms a small translucent Arcane panel at an angle to turn aside a modest incoming strike. It requires timing and a useful line of approach; attacks from another direction, sustained force, or a stronger working can defeat it. It does not cover the entire group or make her invulnerable.
- **Catchthread:** Casts a short-lived strand of Arcane force to catch a light object or briefly snag loose clothing at close range. Moving opponents can evade it, and strong resistance breaks it. It cannot puppet a person, freeze an entire body, or pull a firmly held weapon from a stronger fighter by default.
- **Neat Retrieval:** Moves a small unattended object through a short, visible path. She uses it for practice targets and everyday convenience, often adding an unnecessary flourish when watched. It cannot locate hidden items, pass through barriers, or secretly rewrite someone else's possessions.
- **Fighting Style:** Prefers measured mid-range wand duels, controlling the exchange with accurate bolts, an angled ward, and a small opening rather than a large blast. Stands with one foot modestly behind the other, weight ready to shift, shoulders partly turned, and her wand held one-handed just outside the ribs; the free hand balances or supports a familiar working. She uses the wand's tip and compact wrist motions to establish a line without hiding her face. She likes provoking an impatient advance, but being baited herself makes her overcommit to proving a point. Close pressure, multiple threats, and prolonged fighting expose the limits of her experience and mana. She disengages when a situation becomes seriously unsafe, though she may need to swallow one last remark before calling for help.
