# Ability Batch 1 — Arcane and Guarded Combat

Implemented in Alpha17. Eighteen Arcane spells and eight physical guarded techniques; Arcane Stitch is the retained existing entry. These are skill thresholds rather than character levels. Traits grant four designated starters; the other records are eligible choices, not automatically owned.

### Mana Knock

Cheap close pressure; a concrete first attack and interaction tool.

Stamp a compact rune of force into the air and send it into one visible point within 6 metres. The impact can strike a creature or knock a small loose object along the chosen line. A successful forceful hit may spoil footing; the result determines how much the target actually yields. A short, economical release suited to close fighting and practical interference. Costs 3 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Halfmoon Pane

A quick directional ward with a limited surface.

Unfold a curved, translucent pane about an arm’s length across within 2 metres. Angle it against one declared incoming line, or use it as momentary cover for a nearby person. It lasts for the current exchange; pressure cracks and turns its bright edge before breaking it. Its small surface rewards choosing the right angle rather than enclosing the whole group. Costs 4 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Arcane Stitch

Existing narrow tether; retain the current authored record.

Shape a narrow force tether to catch a reachable loose object or attempt a brief restraint against one target. Resolve one Arcane Magic check with its bonus. Resistance, line of effect, available anchoring and the actual result limit the tether; it is not an unavoidable capture. Costs 6 Mana.

### Glasshand Cradle

Handling fragile, hot or awkward small objects without another familiar.

Shape two cupped force surfaces around one loose object weighing up to roughly 3 kilograms within 5 metres. Carry, turn or steady it with the care of an extra pair of hands while you concentrate, for up to a minute outside combat or two exchanges in danger. Fine handling is possible at a deliberate pace; a held object or resisting creature needs a contested method rather than this unattended-object working. Costs 3 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Prism Javelin

A deliberate precise shot rather than close impulse or homing damage.

Draw a long faceted spear of compressed mana along your forearm, then release it down a clear line at one visible target within 15 metres. The narrow profile concentrates the impact at one point and suits exposed gaps or small targets. The facets are force geometry, not a Light spell. Cover, aim and the actual attack result still decide what it strikes. Costs 6 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Borrowed Stair

Exploration and rescue through temporary physical support.

Set up to three broad force footholds along a route you can see within 8 metres. They support one person at a time for a short crossing, up to a minute or two combat exchanges while you maintain them. Their spacing can bridge a gap, reach a ledge or give a stranded ally a way down. Establish the actual route and footing; the steps are temporary supports, not flight or a jump between locations. Costs 8 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Rebound Seal

Redirect a physical shot instead of absorbing it behind ordinary cover.

Place a slanted seal in the path of one visible, tangible incoming projectile within 6 metres. On a successful interception, the seal kicks it toward an unoccupied line or harmless surface you can identify. Redirecting a shot is the whole action: sending it into another creature would require its own supported attack, and a sustained beam or broad wave cannot be caught as one little missile. Costs 8 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Counterweight Cuff

Anchored load assistance; distinct from Gravity levitation.

Fasten a broad force cuff around a willing person or reachable load, then tether it to an actual stable support within 8 metres. The tether can arrest a short fall, steady a hanging burden or share the strain of moving something heavy for two exchanges. Support strength and the real load matter. The working transmits force along a tether; it does not change the object’s weight or levitate an untethered burden. Costs 8 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Lattice Snare

A prepared passage hazard instead of an unavoidable ranged hold.

Stretch a visible lattice of force across a chosen opening up to 3 metres wide within 10 metres. Set its catches to obstruct a particular route for two exchanges. A creature that presses through must contend with the threads or find an actual way around; bracing points and the opposing result determine whether it is checked, entangled or breaks the web. The bright pattern warns an attentive foe where the danger lies. Costs 12 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Hollow Knight

One articulated working frame for obstruction, labor or a directed strike.

Raise a human-sized, hollow knight-shaped frame of force within 6 metres and direct it for up to three exchanges while concentrating. It can brace one opening, carry a nearby burden within its supported strength, or deliver one directed blow as your action. It is a controlled spell structure with no independent turn, personality or summoned person. Losing control collapses the empty plates into sparks. Costs 14 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Orbit of Needles

An attack with chosen curved geometry and a single damage commitment.

Gather three bright force needles into a tight orbit around your casting hand, then commit them along converging curves at one target within 12 metres. Choose the approach before release: a wide split can threaten the sides of a guard, while a close helix concentrates its pressure. Resolve the committed volley as one attack with this bonus, not three automatic hits or three separate full damage awards. Solid cover can still intercept the chosen curves. Costs 14 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Hammerfall Diagram

A telegraphed compact area strike that rewards positioning.

Sketch a broad descending sigil over a visible patch of ground within 15 metres. After a clear downward sweep of your hand, compressed force crashes through a circle roughly 3 metres across. The forming diagram and your gesture telegraph the strike; fast opponents can contest or leave its reach. Resolve the creatures actually caught by the area through their supplied outcomes, rather than assigning every nearby body an automatic hit. Costs 24 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Glassless Citadel

Prepared group cover and lane design.

Plant three linked force panels around a chosen position within 8 metres, enclosing at most a small cluster of people behind waist-to-head-high cover for three exchanges. Doors, gaps and panel angles are selected when you set it. Concentrated pressure can break a panel, and enemies can use real routes over or around the arrangement. It is stationary shelter whose strength lies in its layout, not a moving bubble of invulnerability. Costs 30 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Twelvefold Procession

A coordinated construction/rescue working with a visible twelve-joint identity.

Assemble four elegant force frames, each with three luminous joints, in a cluster within 10 metres. For three exchanges of concentration, direct one purposeful operation each turn: lift a beam together, carry someone clear, brace a failing structure or commit their coordinated strength to one strike. The twelve joints share your attention and action; they do not create four summoned allies or four independent attacks. Broken supports and heavy loads remain real problems to resolve. Costs 30 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Pressure Psalm

Directional area pressure with visible escape routes.

Release a rolling procession of compressed force along a corridor or open lane up to 20 metres long and 3 metres wide. Its visible pulses press exposed targets toward a chosen end of that lane, with the actual attack and resistance deciding injury and displacement. Heavy anchors, side cover and openings disrupt the pressure. The measured rhythm is force control, not Sound casting; there is one committed area working rather than a fresh full hit for every pulse. Costs 40 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Severance Diagram

A controlled breach of a particular structural point.

Lay an exact force diagram against one visible physical barrier, support or force construct within 12 metres, then drive its pressure into a chosen joint. The technique concentrates a breach at that point so allies can exploit a real opening. Material strength, reinforcement and the opposing working determine success. It attacks a structure through force and precision rather than universally dispelling magic or deleting every obstacle on a route. Costs 40 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Aegis of the Last Bell

A brief, costly group defense against a specified major threat.

Ring a great silent arc of runes around yourself and up to three nearby allies within 4 metres. For the current exchange, its inward-curving surfaces meet one declared direction of assault and spread the pressure across the formation. It is a demanding chance to weather a major attack or make a withdrawal possible; the actual contest can still crack it. Attacks from an unguarded direction, enemies already inside the line and unsupported consequences are not erased. Costs 55 Mana on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Quiet Meridian

Very modest master-level active uptake; one action buys five Mana, at most once per ten turns.

Settle into a deliberate, uninterrupted recovery action and draw a small additional flow from the ambient mana. On a genuinely completed working, recover exactly 5 Mana once, capped by your actual maximum, in addition to the ordinary passive uptake. It cannot be combined with attacking, another spell, demanding exertion or active hostile interference during that exchange; interruption loses the recovery. Costs 0 Mana and 0 Stamina. Its ten-turn recovery interval also applies to cosmetic restatements of this same working.

### Doorholder

Make a narrow line meaningfully contestable; starter guard technique.

Set your trained guard across one narrow approach within your physical reach, using the actual weapon, shield, body position and terrain available. Contest someone trying to force that line during the current exchange; a successful hold checks their entry or makes them choose a real route around you. This is a deliberate positional action. An ordinary guard remains possible without spending Stamina, but does not receive this technique’s bonus. Costs 4 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Measured Reprisal

A cautious physical counter-attack after an actual opening; starter technique.

Answer a nearby opponent’s observed overextension with one compact guarded strike. Keep your line closed as you attack the opening their actual movement left. Use a trained weapon or unarmed method within its real reach. It is your committed attack, not a free reaction added to somebody else’s turn; without a genuine opening, choose a different action. Costs 4 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Iron Hinge

Redirect one close blow while holding position.

Turn a shield or suitably braced weapon around a tight pivot to shed one reachable close strike away from your center. Choose where the force can safely go and contest the actual attack. A successful turn can preserve your position and expose its committed line, but it supplies no automatic follow-up hit. You need the real equipment and room for the pivot. Costs 6 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Crossbar Catch

Briefly contest a weapon line rather than merely block impact.

Trap a reachable weapon momentarily between your trained guard and an actual shield rim, weapon crosspiece or safe bracing surface. Contest control of that line for the current exchange, buying an opening to disengage or change position. The opponent can contest or release their weapon. Reach, leverage and an available catching surface matter; the catch is not a disarm against every weapon. Costs 6 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Shoulder Lantern

A physical escort/rescue maneuver for one nearby ally.

Bring one reachable willing ally, or someone who can actually be supported, behind your physical cover and guide them a few paces toward a chosen safe position. Keep your guard between them and one visible threat. Resolve the actual movement, burden and interception; the technique does not teleport the ally or negate a hit already resolved. The lantern is the sheltering role, not a magical light. Costs 8 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Low Roof Advance

Guarded movement against a chosen firing lane.

Advance a short distance under a compact shield, braced weapon guard or actual overhead cover, keeping one identified projectile lane screened. Stay low and commit to the route instead of sprinting blindly into it. The move contests that approach while carrying you toward the next piece of cover; flanking angles, unsupported protection and an open field remain dangerous. Costs 8 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Breakwater Turn

Change a contested close line through leverage and footwork.

Meet a close opponent’s committed pressure, turn your braced guard and guide the exchange toward an open side you can actually occupy. On a successful contest, use their momentum to change the line or move them off the immediate approach. It is one physical defensive maneuver; the result decides displacement, and an anchored foe may refuse the turn. No Water casting or automatic knockdown is involved. Costs 10 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

### Shelter Behind Me

A costly physical formation covering a small group from one approach.

Build a tight protective formation with up to two allies who can stay close behind your actual reach and equipment. Spend the current exchange covering one declared approach and contest an interception that would expose them. You give ground, brace or take the pressure as the resolved situation requires. Allies can act normally, but your formation is not an all-round shield and gives you no automatic defense roll against every attack in the room. Costs 14 Stamina on commitment, including a committed miss.

## Live test focus

Start an Arcane Studies character and a Guarded Training character; inspect the two free moves for each without awarding the whole school. Try basic physical attacks and routine guard at zero Stamina. Check Halfmoon Pane and Rebound Seal timing, Borrowed Stair support, Hollow Knight/Twelvefold Procession action use and actual area outcomes. At mastery, Quiet Meridian should restore only 5 Mana once, obey its cooldown and not duplicate passive uptake. Costs, targeting and tactical effects are AI-interpreted; local eligibility checks do not establish live balance.
