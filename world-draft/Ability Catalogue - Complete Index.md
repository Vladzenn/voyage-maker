# Complete Ability Catalogue — Alpha18

480 entries: 360 spells, 64 martial/tactical techniques, 40 mixed techniques and 16 Magitech techniques. The original 35 entries are preserved exactly. Thresholds below are **skill ranks**, not character levels. Eligibility is not automatic ownership.

Each of the 20 public disciplines has 18 spells: 5 below skill 20, 6 at 20–39, 3 at 40–59, 2 at 60–79 and 2 at 80–100. Space also requires Gravity Magic 25 for every entry. Time has no public spells.
