# Ability Catalogue — Current Implementation

Alpha18 completes the expanded catalogue: 480 authored entries (360 spells, 64 martial/tactical techniques, 40 mixed and 16 Magitech). The original 35 are preserved. See Ability Catalogue - Complete Index.md for rank/cost tables and Abilities - Readable.md for every complete description.

Each of the twenty public disciplines has eighteen independent spells. The distribution is five below skill 20, six at 20–39, three at 40–59, two at 60–79 and two at 80–100. Skill thresholds are eligibility requirements, not character levels or automatic ownership. Time has no public batch. Space remains advanced and requires Gravity Magic 25.

The nineteen current magical studies and three combat-training styles each grant two eligible introductory techniques. Full section replacement files are WorldAlpha - Abilities.json and WorldAlpha - Traits.json. Only import the full world if the whole synchronized draft is intended. New grants apply when traits are applied; no old-save migration is promised.

The content pass is implemented. The next necessary work is live selection, combat and balance testing, rather than generating hundreds more records. Costs, targeting, healing, summoning and spatial travel are described for the AI; native prerequisites, bonus and cooldown values are authored directly. Skill acquisition and the future one-to-three affinity creation interface remain unchanged.
