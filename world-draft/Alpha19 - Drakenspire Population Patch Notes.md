# Alpha19 Drakenspire Population

Added **52 authored NPCs**: **18 major and 34 minor**, bringing the whole roster to **193**. Fifty are humanoids, of whom thirty-eight are dragonkin; the other two are Rovakthuun, the Gray Guardian, and Orrum, a unique yeti. All fifty-two have complete cards, behavior cues, relevant equipment, bounded abilities and concealment/knowledge guidance. Weighted affinity draws were actually made and recorded: 34 single, 12 double and 6 triple. Major status is portrait/authoring metadata, not a new native NPC field or automatic public fame.

The very old Jarl Daizen Iwatsugu was an adventurer against his family’s wishes, then returned to clan responsibility and later elected rule in honor of his deceased father. The three prominent clans remain Iwatsugu, Yukinose and Arashimine. Each now has several major characters, real household differences and stakes in a fragile peace. Their historical economic ties remain influences rather than career castes. Clans are classified as major factions; assembly, guard and Keepers have distinct responsibilities and several recurring people.

Thirty-nine existing locations have named contacts at exact areas, including shops, food, lodging, homes, public work, religious care, performances, fighting, mines and research. Several places have pairs. Some shared sites and remote landscapes remain unstaffed. New scoped lore covers succession, households, work and the two unique beings. Contacts are local rather than a global cast lecture. Ordinary giant generation remains; the generic Yeti type is retired in favor of Orrum’s individual record.

Five narrow supported hooks handle rare observation chances and continuity for the off-stage dragon and yeti. Native placement, a stored cooldown and explicit random chance prevent map-marker encounters or immediate rerolls. Their details and honest AI-dependent limits are in Drakenspire - Rare Encounter Notes.md. No per-NPC quest trigger, mandatory political plot, invented lair or schedule schema was added.

The latest user roster was merged losslessly. Its new Kharvess and Orrhask hosted portraits are retained; all existing NPCs and their order remain. Maps, coordinates, area sets, location images, portal mechanics, the 480-ability catalogue, all traits and resources, curriculum, skill acquisition and unrelated quests/triggers are unchanged. Faction, lore, local scene and carefully scoped AI additions support this population. Naming guidance now permits original nonhumanoid personal names rather than forced Japanese surnames.

The reusable Population Creation Guide is linked from Character Creation Format. Its coverage, relationship, institution, placement and portrait process adapts the supplied Kato guide without replacing the existing template or inventing character sections.
