# Alpha25 — First Days and Appearance

Use the complete **GlowhavenAlpha25 - First Days and Appearance.json** export. It is identical to WorldAlpha - World.json and preserves all existing hosted portraits, location/area images, map coordinates, 205 NPC identities and 480 abilities. This update builds on Alpha24, including the quest cleanup and stronger relationship consequences.

## Character creation

Removed **Space Affinity** from player creation. Space and Time are absent from creation choices; their configured advanced skills and existing ability catalogue remain. The other nineteen affinity options still describe learning ease; separate Starting Magical Study choices grant the actual starting skills and two techniques. This does not revert the previously approved affinity/study distinction.

Added one optional **Appearance Builder**, using the grouped visual-trait pattern observed in the user's LuxcelliaUpdated.json. Its **71 choices** cover Hair Color, Hair Style, Eye Color, Skin Tone, Build, Outfit and Portrait Background. The category permits up to seven selections, recommending at most one per group. Voyage does not document a separate selection cap per subcategory; the quantity guidance and conflict handling do not constitute a hard per-group UI limit. Choosing nothing remains valid. Explicit written appearance takes precedence over conflicting cosmetics.

All cosmetic traits have empty stat, skill, resource, item and ability grants. An outfit look supplies no armor or equipment, and a backdrop choice does not move the character or create a home. The portrait keeps one standing, centered hip-up figure, vertical 2:3, complete head/hair/horns, 12.5–15% headroom (17% maximum), crisp 2D anime treatment, no semi-realism and no text. This is a backdrop-prompt selector, not a newly invented image-upload control. No new portraits are generated by this update.

## Student first days

Replaced the four arrival openings with **Phoenix Wing: First Day**, **Gryffin Tail: First Day**, **Dragon Hoard: First Day** and **Hydra Head: First Day**. These are fuller authored scenes with a busy student body, an ordinary registrar timetable desk, a headmaster's welcome in the appropriate existing hall, and a concrete first Magic Theory lesson. The player begins already enrolled as a first-year; returning named students keep their actual academic years. The opening's short routine arrival and welcome are established setup, not a forced player answer or completed lesson.

Required lessons occupy the morning block, from the first bell to midday. Each year rotates the academy's three established core subjects on its timetable. Afternoons are ordinarily free unless an announced practical, examination or other specific duty needs extra time. No whole-school weekly scheduling simulation or bell-clock trigger network was added. The timetable is a natural information item. All four headmasters normally occupy Faculty Offices; their welcome is a bounded first-day event. Kiyoko Fujinami's saved placement was corrected from Confluence Pavilion to Faculty Offices.

The four old Enrollment quests become **First Morning at [academy]**, with a single meaningful objective: attend and participate in the first Magic Theory lesson. A genuine failed answer or beginner attempt still counts; a plan to attend does not. They use Voyage's native completionCondition rather than separate check-in and finish triggers. Four game-start setup rules retain the existing gh_enrolled flags and place only the small relevant teaching/classmate cast. Twelve older enrollment/opening-placement triggers were replaced by four setup rules: **308 → 300 triggers**. Existing portal, dungeon, trophy/rivalry, prison, guild and paced academic-event mechanics remain unchanged.

## Local homes and geography

Kept new-region generation disabled and preserved the existing geography. Clarified the relevant narration/location tasks: an ordinary visit to a friend's home can elaborate a modest interior under a suitable existing residential location. Use a new saved local area only when the runtime task actually supports that operation; otherwise keep the room as local scene detail. A house interior is not a new nation, realm or map destination. Geographic restrictions stay behind the scenes, rather than becoming NPC dialogue. This is instruction-level guidance, not a new hard navigation mechanic; the home-visit case still needs testing.
