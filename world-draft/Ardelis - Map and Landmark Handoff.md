## Landmark interpretation and interiors

Visible map features informed the archive complex, aqueduct, reservoir, western amphitheatre and exercise field, northeastern observatory and crystal works, southwestern mills, eastern locks and small harbor tower. Their functions and ownership are authored choices. Markets, lodging, trades, homes and public services fill existing city fabric rather than requiring giant new map buildings.

The palace descent remains connected areas inside Caelrath Palace. The separate sewer site is in its current supplied region and never creates a free palace shortcut. The portal room has a separate area establishing shot. Thaurivex and other named actors are not permanently painted into static location images.

Current placement source: `tools/ardelis-followup/placements.json` and `placements.csv`. Initial `tools/ardelis/` pin records remain historical.
