# 👑 Ardelis: Beneath the Crown

Beyond Glowhaven lies a capital that has spent centuries making an impression.

White towers rise above the water, gold ornaments circle the royal skyline, and bridges carry busy streets between mountain terraces. Welcome to **Caelrath, capital of the Empire of Ardelis**—a city of noble households, expensive tastes, working neighborhoods and some very carefully closed doors.

### 🏙️ A Capital to Make a Life In

Explore markets, boutiques, gardens, baths, inns, workshops, waterfronts and public institutions across the city. Browse jewelry and perfume beneath the glass of Opaline Arcade, find a performance at the Velvet Lyre Hall, or look for a room and useful work among Southbank’s households.

The Adventurer’s Guild has a local branch, with familiar badges and work ranging from ordinary beginner jobs to considerably less comfortable contracts. Caelrath’s own magical institute offers a different place to study, experiment and meet people with projects of their own.

### 🤝 New Faces, Old Obligations

Meet a broad new cast of residents, adventurers, artisans, performers and officials, alongside the royal family and several influential noble houses.

- An aging king has ambitions he does not intend to leave unfinished.
- A formidable queen and three very different princesses have their own interests, loyalties and private troubles.
- Named paladin leaders disagree about what their oaths require.
- Guild regulars, neighbors and working households have lives beyond the next request they might make of you.

The entire new cast receives portraits, from the royal household to working residents and guild regulars. The capital also gains location artwork inspired by its distinctive map.

### 🕯️ What People Ask—and What They Hide

New quests involve disputed goods, unpaid wages, patronage, missing people, noble contracts, performances and the practical work of keeping a magnificent city running.

Some jobs are honest and straightforward. Others leave you deciding whose account you believe and what a useful compromise will cost. An independent criminal organization and a private religious splinter circle offer very different kinds of trouble.

Public opportunities can be investigated or ignored. Caelrath’s guild board offers one relevant notice at a time, while deliberately chosen harder work remains available.

### ✨ Rebirth Continues

The receiving-station evidence now leads beyond Glowhaven’s jurisdiction. Secure permission to travel, follow the local records and discover what the imperial project has become.

Vladimir can help arrange a passage, but the independent court route remains open. Unresolved imperial business can bring occasional retrieval attempts after your mana trail is genuinely identified. Defeating a team may buy time without ending the operation behind it. His appearance has also been refreshed—with a very large hat and a glass staff he would strongly prefer you did not touch.

### ⚔️ Beneath the Beautiful Streets

An optional midgame descent waits under the palace, through receiving chambers, calibration galleries and cooling works toward an experimental vessel that was never meant to become a public concern.

Elsewhere, maintenance stairs lead beneath Eastwater into the sewers: raised inspection walks, old sluices and abandoned branches with smaller, less ceremonial inhabitants. Preparation, sensible retreat and practical solutions matter in both places.

### 🌟 More Ways to Begin

New Ardelis beginnings let you arrive at the guild counter, settle among Southbank’s neighbors, take a place at the institute or attend an appointment with a noble household.

New equipment, techniques and encounter types accompany the expansion. Lore now reaches further into the empire’s faith, politics, households, shared history with independent Glowhaven and ordinary life—while the journey itself stays focused on its capital.

The gates are impressive. What happens after you enter is another matter. 👑
