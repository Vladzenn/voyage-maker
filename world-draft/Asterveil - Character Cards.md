### Orivelle

### Appearance

A great four-legged sky beast with an elongated deerlike face, deep turquoise eyes and pearl-white fur that lengthens into a flowing mane. Two broad, feathered wings grow from its shoulders, washed with pale opal and gold; a clean notch breaks the trailing edge of the left wing. Branching, translucent gold horns sweep backward above its ears. Its legs end in dark cloven hooves, and a long flexible tail carries a fan of fine silver filaments. It is larger than a horse, graceful rather than heavily built, and plainly an animal rather than a humanoid wearing a costume.

### Basic Info

Orivelle is the individual chimehart sometimes seen crossing Asterveil’s open sky. Its ringing calls travel farther than its pale silhouette, and experienced observers recognize the notch in its left wing. It feeds and rests along a circuit through all four regions, avoiding crowded settlements. It is intelligent, territorial and curious about repeated visitors, but is neither a public mount nor a guardian obliged to rescue anyone. Local accounts include real observations and plenty of confident invention.

It carries no manufactured equipment. A few weathered seed burrs catch in its mane after it moves through flowering woodland; these are ordinary traces of travel, not guaranteed loot.

- **Gender:** Unknown
- **Age:** Unknown; a mature individual
- **Species:** Unique mana beast
- **Role in faction:** Free-ranging mythical chimehart
- **Magic affinity:** Wind, Light, Sound

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Keep access to its feeding and resting grounds without being crowded, trapped or pursued from one stop to the next.

**What they conceal:** Its apparent fascination with bells is selective: it answers the intervals in an old three-note call, not every loud noise. This is learned recognition, not a password that commands obedience.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Hotone Mizuhane has recorded part of the pattern but has not tested a claim of control. Akito Kazamori once heard the complete reply during a genuine close encounter and conceals how frightened he was. Neither owns the creature or knows all its movements.

**Relationships:** Hotone Mizuhane is a familiar distant observer, not its keeper. Akito Kazamori is a recognized source of a former disturbance. No human or fae has an automatic bond, party claim or riding permission.

**How it surfaces:** It may pause for a familiar interval, angle its damaged wing away from a watcher or leave before an approaching group reaches its resting shelf.

- **Core:** A living, self-directed beast whose curiosity can coexist with a firm refusal to be approached.
- **Default behavior:** Watches from a defensible perch, samples the air and gives a low resonant call before deciding whether to stay.

- **Courtship Style:** No humanoid romance; animal courtship is outside the player relationship system.

- **Routine or Quirk:** Drops one clear descending note before moving from a feeding patch, sometimes leaving listeners unsure whether they heard a bell.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Opal Flare:** Opens a brief Light fan across its wings to dazzle an approaching threat; it is resisted and does not permanently blind onlookers.
- **Hollow Chord:** Directs a short Sound concussion to interrupt pursuit and create space. The call does not enthrall every listener.
- **Fighting Style:** A powerful aerial skirmisher that uses height, wing beats and short Light or Sound bursts to break pursuit. It kicks or drives its horns at close range only when pressed. The left wing notch is an identifying old injury, not a guaranteed crippling weakness; death or capture persists and does not spawn a replacement Orivelle.
