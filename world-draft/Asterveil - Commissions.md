# Asterveil — Local Commissions

Six finite authored commissions provide initial play without a premade NPC roster. Each giver is a real duty-holder generated as needed and then retained. The offer, acceptance, field result and received report are separate. Fixed typed rewards pay once on completion.

| Commission | Offered at | Recommended | Field destination | XP / marks per player |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Cuts Among the Pollen | Bellwind Conservatory — Specimen Porch | 10+ | Bellwind Conservatory | 900 / 70 |
| Grounded Thunder | Asterveil Canopy — Field Registry | 25+ | Thunderwake Crags | 1500 / 130 |
| Where the Water Lies | Asterveil Canopy — Field Registry | 30+ | Mirrorwater Basin | 2000 / 180 |
| The Borrowed Shape | Asterveil Canopy — Field Registry | 40+ | Prismbloom Basin | 2600 / 220 |
| A Quiet Instrument | Asterveil Canopy — Field Registry | 45+ | Stillstar Sanctum | 3200 / 280 |
| Pressure Beneath the Crown | Heatherwatch Shelf — Common Room | 50+ | The Inverted Crown | 4200 / 360 |

Reading a generic board does not accept every job. Offers require engaging with that particular commission, at its actual offer area and recommended level. Native UI acceptance remains usable; explicit spoken acceptance is also supported by a gated action trigger. None of these jobs blocks ordinary travel or creates a general right to collect protected material.

Field completion records the evidence flag and objective, then exposes the report objective. Returning to a hub does not pay by itself. The actual duty-holder must receive the real result and any agreed physical material. A permitted non-destructive alternative needs actual evidence. The typed Mark payout and displayed funds are one holding.

A local commission does not automatically complete the existing authorized academy expedition. That separate quest can still recognize appropriately authorized fieldwork in any of the four Asterveil regions, followed by its own debrief. The one-time portal anomaly and postponed field test are unchanged.

## Individual design briefs

### Cuts Among the Pollen

A finite local fieldwork commission, recommended for prepared level-10+ teams. Offer it only when the player actually seeks this specific work; neither reading the whole board nor arriving accepts it. Establish the duty-holder as a persistent generated person and agree the real method, collection limit, team, transport and report. No premade giver or boss is added. Field target: Bellwind Conservatory. Required evidence: The party has actually compared damaged and intact pollen screens, observed the Threadwing Grazer feeding evidence, and carried out a sound response such as protected replacement screens, changed placement or a documented non-destructive trial. A suspicion, proposed treatment or mere arrival is insufficient.
The player may solve the task by its stated alternative rather than a mandatory kill. Use the site’s exact NPC types and bands, telegraph real hazards and permit withdrawal. Record the actual creature and outcome; do not reroll a defeated guardian or create a substitute named villain. A finding that extraction is unsafe needs real supporting evidence, not an automatic free success. Report to the duty-holder at Bellwind Conservatory. The fee is 70 marks and 900 XP per participating player, paid only by onCompleteEffects. Do not pay again through narration or item generation. Actual sample transfers are handled once by normal inventory updates; no imaginary sample is awarded merely because the type exists. This local commission does not itself complete the separate court-authorized expedition.
