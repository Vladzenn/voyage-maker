## The small social circles

Kohane Kazahana and Kureha Kazahana are sisters: argumentative, competitive and devoted to each other. Their appearances follow actual independent draws, with family resemblance retained in their copper hair and moth wings.

Honoka Hazukawa and Rento Tobari are married business owners. Yuzuri Takase is a paying guest and a friend, not their employee or an automatically available bodyguard.

Kureha Kazahana and Jianyu Wen are research collaborators with a genuine disagreement about evidence and credit. The visitor’s old dismissive paper is his private embarrassment, not a new international conspiracy.

Shūgo Shibane and Mio Sasahara have an old close friendship. He understands her discomfort over open sky and she knows what his sentimental keepsakes mean. Their relationship is not automatically romantic.

Hotone Mizuhane dislikes Akito Kazamori’s invented beast stories, but they both produce useful real field knowledge. Sakuya Tomonari handles public agreements without becoming everyone’s ruler or knowing everyone’s secrets.

## Coverage and deliberate gaps

The Canopy’s lodging, meals, registry, hearings and repairs have recognizable recurring people. Cloudbloom has a pilot; Bellwind a cultivator; Broken Halo a visiting researcher; Mythwing a keeper. Rimeglass Refuge, Reedglass Anchorage and Heatherwatch Shelf each have one competent resident contact. Most caves, open wilderness, ruins and dangerous habitats remain unstaffed. Ordinary monsters continue to use npcTypes.

Existing expedition commissions now reference this cast. Sakuya Tomonari may relay an agreement or accept its report, while technical findings retain a real specialist. Original locations, objective gates, payouts and chains remain. No new compulsory questline, portal authorization or automatic recruitment was added.

## Fae variation

The recorded draw produced five short adult fae, three around human height and one 219 cm satyr. Nine fae are not a census of the whole species. Eight have wings and one is wingless; two have rounded ears. Wooden scale patches and a short deer tail add individual variation. Short stature retains mature adult features and proportions. The reusable appearance guide includes bird wings and other combinations even when this particular draw did not select them.

The circuit uses the native day counter and native NPC placement. It does not advance the clock. Encounter, combat, death and party guards are mechanical; actual captivity or a disabling injury sets a pause flag through a scoped story query, and real release/escape/recovery clears it. No guaranteed sighting, journal reveal, player teleport, resurrection, tame companion or repeat monster is granted. If play never advances the day, the beast stays on that day’s circuit stop.
