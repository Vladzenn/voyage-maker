# Asterveil — Realm Lore

## Asterveil Sky Archipelago

Asterveil is a broad archipelago of floating islands in the same physical world as Glowhaven. Four mapped regions surround a small fae settlement: Cloudbloom Reaches in the northwest, Rimeglass Heights in the northeast, Mirrorwater Marches in the southwest and Amethyst Crown in the southeast. The realm is predominantly wilderness, with scattered field shelters rather than four towns. Cloudbloom Landing receives the authorized Evertree portal route; Asterveil Canopy is the settlement near it.

Fae live alongside a smaller number of other residents. Flower terraces, craft, fragrance cultivation, weather study and crossing work support daily life, while expeditions investigate plants, creatures, materials and the remains of earlier survey work. Residents are people with varied goals, not universal guides, miniature mascots or custodians of every secret. Some individuals can change size; their actual record determines their forms.

This is a midgame expedition realm. Normal regional bands progress through 10–25, 20–35, 30–45 and 40–55. Safe hubs have lower local bands. The Inverted Crown is an exceptional level-55–60 field site, not an endgame zone. A young visiting player is not barred by an invisible level wall, and a powerful visiting NPC keeps their actual level. Risk is communicated through local evidence and available preparation.

## Asterveil Geography and Buoyancy

The four regions form a contiguous two-by-two map. Cloudbloom is northwest, Rimeglass northeast, Mirrorwater southwest and Amethyst southeast. Each holds several large and medium islands, with small fragments between them. The settlement occupies a protected satellite near Cloudbloom’s southeastern side; the wilderness landmass around it is much larger than the inhabited footprint.

Mana-bearing rootstone supports the islands’ buoyancy. Its large formations are stable on ordinary travel timescales; their existence does not make every loose rock float, power a free personal flight spell or provide an inexhaustible generator. Short-term measurements concern wind, load, erosion and local vibration, not an inevitable collapse of all Asterveil. The origin of the oldest suspended foundations remains only partially understood.

The northeastern plateaus rise into colder air; southwestern basins trap water and mist; southeastern mineral ridges carry wind and violet heath. Waterfalls leave real island edges. Lakes and streams are not secretly continuous across sky gaps. No additional realm or unmapped continent is created beneath a fall.

## Asterveil Crossings and Field Safety

Only the Cloudbloom Landing Portal Room connects to Glowhaven. Court-issued Asterveil keys retain their actual permissions; local crossing tickets do not replace them. The existing fixed return works without a second key or new court approval. Local movement uses paths on continuous land, short maintained spans where actually present, working boats on water and attended carrier crossings over wider sky gaps.

The Asterveil Crossing Cooperative maintains routes from Cloudbloom toward Tetherroot Chain and Rimeglass Refuge. Tetherroot connects south to Reedglass Anchorage; both the anchorage and refuge have routes toward Heatherwatch Shelf. A light sail carrier uses a real hull, harness, trained crew and controlled lift; it is not an invented free teleport or an unlimited autonomous flying item. Nonflying travelers can buy a seat or join an agreed expedition charter. Short local seats are commonly 12–20 marks, longer regional seats 25–40; cargo, weather and rescue work require a clear actual quote. These are route anchors, not charges applied without a transaction.

A booking includes its actual destination, landing, load and return arrangement. Flights and boats can be unavailable for a real reason without every journey becoming a permit quest. Individual flight still needs a real bodily or learned ability, range, load capacity and safe landing. Small fae wings are not proof of the endurance to carry an adult companion. Strong wind, cold, water and falls use supported action outcomes and resource rules rather than a new stamina meter or scripted instant death.

## Asterveil Civic Life

Asterveil Canopy is a small independent community linked to Glowhaven by a practical treaty of transit and exchange. Its household assembly appoints rotating stewards for water, crossings and habitat agreements. Open hearings resolve specific disputes; the stewards do not administer every wild nest or command foreign academies. Foreign visitors obey local rules while academy members keep their existing affiliation.

The settlement groups homes, a market, guesthouse, kitchen gardens, menders and a field registry within a compact neighborhood. Goods can be traded in marks at agreed prices. Food comes from cultivated beds, mushrooms, managed pollination, lake produce and imports. Freight returns with fragrances, seeds, fibers, optical shell and carefully documented materials. Stable supplies and quiet ordinary lives exist alongside adventurous work.

Household traditions mark first blooms, returning carriers and remembered ancestors. Some residents treat island buoyancy as sacred, others study it; there is no compulsory deity or universal fae worldview. Language follows the speaker’s actual knowledge. Local personal names follow the established First name Last name preference; Elvish remains represented as French.

## Asterveil Fieldwork and Permissions

The field registry in Asterveil Canopy matches visitors with finite work: ecology counts, weather surveys, route assessment, equipment recovery, material procurement, predator problems and investigation of unusual readings. Bellwind Conservatory receives research results; crossing crews handle transport and safety work. A duty-holder is a role for a generated persistent person, not a new named NPC invented in every conversation.

Local permission states a site, purpose, collection limit and protected conditions. The court’s travel key does not confer ownership of a nest, specimen or ruin. Ordinary observation from public approaches needs no elaborate bureaucracy. Destructive sampling, taking cultivated stock or entering a protected working site needs an actual agreement. Most wild materials are not individual private property, but conservation limits and existing claims still matter.

Any academy can undertake an appropriate approved assignment. Magical combat, engineering, herbology, negotiation, physical fieldcraft and careful record work can all contribute. The four regions do not correspond to four mandatory affinities. A local contract only counts toward the separate authorized academy expedition when its actual agreed court scope, team, field result and debrief satisfy that expedition’s own conditions. Tourism, automatic arrival and an unrelated kill do not confer educational credit.

## Asterveil Ecology and Progression

Cloudbloom’s woods and meadows support grazers, small mana beasts and ambush predators. Rimeglass adds cold, altitude, charged mineral perches and concealed conifer hunters. Mirrorwater requires attention to depth, channels, footing and reflective lures. Amethyst’s broken ridges and acoustic terrain favor creatures that hide their outline, redirect sound or exploit open approaches. Bodies and methods vary within the local band; every dangerous creature need not be enormous.

Use the actual authored NPC types for species. A reusable type is a category, not a unique individual, an automatic spawn or a fixed level. On encounter, create a coherent persistent individual with the location’s band and bounded attacks. Ordinary wildlife, neutral creatures and sapient communities can coexist with actual hostile monsters. Even the innermost frontier is midgame country; its exceptional apex site caps generated creatures at level 60.

Signs should help decisions: missing notes, cut reeds, a repeated wake, feeding scars, wing dust or a charging vane reveal real behavior. Once a player knowingly enters occupied hunting ground or seeks an actual fight, deliver an appropriate encounter rather than tracks forever. Cleared threats stay resolved; a different animal or later return requires a real cause, not a reset loop. Retreat, a habitat remedy, permitted collection and an actual kill can be different valid outcomes.

## Asterveil Materials and Procurement

Materials have provenance, condition and limited uses. Threadwing scales suit pollen screens; discarded Petalglass shell supports optical studies; discharged Stormglass plates can be examined for charge control; mature glassreed suits filters and fine tubing; Prismfold membrane reveals refractive structure; old calibration components support precision research. These are examples of field value, not automatic recipes or free permanent bonuses.

Obtain an actual sample before inventory changes. Record quantity, condition and owner; a description of a colony or catalogue entry is not a grant. Shed material, a traded specimen, a permitted cutting and recovered remains have different costs. Active charge, toxins, fragile tissue and storage can matter through real supported effects. Merely being magical does not make a material a health or mana potion.

A report can include a control comparison, a negative result or a reason extraction should not occur. Contract terms determine payment. The configured Mark item and displayed money are one holding, and a trigger-paid fee is paid once. Selling the same specimen removes it; a copied field record is information rather than duplicated physical material.

## Asterveil Survey Ruins

Broken Halo Ruins, Drowned Orrery and Stillstar Sanctum preserve different parts of an old practical survey tradition. They measured wind, light, water cycles and instrument alignment; they were not one omnipotent mechanism controlling every island. Dates, affiliations and losses must be established from actual evidence. Similar design does not prove a single war, fallen dynasty or secret villain.

Most failures have local causes: a damaged counterweight, a reflection produced by a living animal, a blocked channel or a resonator deliberately isolated after harming nearby habitat. A party can distinguish those causes through real comparison and inspection. Findings persist and can be reported without creating an automatic grand conspiracy.

Old constructs and rare materials may be dangerous. Permission, stabilization, copying a record and taking an artifact are separate acts. An intact part is not guaranteed after demolition. Preserve a genuine decision to leave an unsafe or important object in place as a possible successful outcome when the commission allows it.

## Asterveil Wilderness Expeditions

Expedition scenes begin with a concrete purpose and method: survey a channel, recover a damaged instrument, protect a carrier, collect a bounded sample, identify a predator or investigate a specific anomaly. Use a small manageable cast and existing places. A contract can be exciting without recruiting every acquaintance or inventing another village beyond the map.

Prepare at Cloudbloom, the Rimeglass Refuge, Reedglass Anchorage or Heatherwatch Shelf. Actual kit, permission, field knowledge and companions create different approaches. Bad preparation has specific visible consequences; it does not make every missing signature a new quest. The shortest safe answer may be to withdraw and return better equipped. A successful finite job is allowed to end.

The six authored local commissions provide examples across the regions. Generated contracts may use other existing sites and species, with a clear objective, actual payment terms, a completion event and a persistent outcome. Do not generate new locations to satisfy a monster clue when an appropriate authored area exists. Quiet camps and settlement scenes remain possible between fieldwork.
