# 🏆 Champions of the Crucible

The Eternal Crucible is opening its gates to a new generation of challengers!

### ⚔️ Enter the Arena

- Join optional individual championships with changing fields, named cups and opponents suited to your experience.
- Fight through different arena layouts, from pillars and overgrown cover to frozen platforms and broken stairs.
- Meet new competitors in the lounge: rivals, nervous newcomers, veteran fighters and people with lives beyond the ring.
- Take a break between bouts, visit the medics or head straight into your next match. Ordinary sparring remains available between championships.
- Win prizes and a championship trophy. Later editions become available after a proper break.

### 🌿 Beyond the Applause

- Discover a new midgame dungeon of haunted glasshouses, memorial gardens and plant-bodied undead.
- Explore its guarded wings, uncover what its caretaker is preserving, and choose how to end the danger.
- Added **160 abilities** across magical disciplines, martial techniques, mixed fighting styles and Magitech.

### 🕯️ Less Honest Work

- New contacts and questionable jobs in Glowhaven and Drakenspire, with room to negotiate, refuse or pursue another solution.
- Expanded Oni lore, laying the cultural groundwork for a future journey.

Championship brackets currently support a solo player with NPC companions watching from outside the ring. Team tournaments are still ahead.

**A title is earned in the arena. What you do afterward is up to you.**
