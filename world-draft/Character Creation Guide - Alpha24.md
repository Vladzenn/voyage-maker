# Character Creation — Beta Preparation

The full Alpha24 import includes the traits, categories, guidance and supporting lore together. Character Creation - Alpha24.json is a partial root fragment for comparison/merging, not a second full world. Standalone Traits, Trait Categories and NPC Updates files are also supplied.

## The choices

1. Species & Ancestry: Human, Elf, Dwarf, Dragonkin, Fae, Beastfolk, Oni or Mixed Ancestry. Each contributes one listed +1 skill. Bird-featured people are Beastfolk. Mixed Ancestry can represent a half-elf without stacking two ancestry packages. Describe the actual adult body in ordinary language.
2. Origin & Upbringing: one of twelve formative backgrounds. This describes where you grew up or what surrounded you, not a present job or automatic acquaintance.
3. Primary Aptitude: one +4 choice from Force, Finesse, Logic, Passion, Empathy and Intuition. Each supplies shared foundational skills, including Spellcraft.
4. Secondary Aptitude: a different +2 choice. The matching primary blocks the identical secondary through the existing earlier-category gate.
5. Natural Magical Affinity: choose one to three of twenty public disciplines. These describe learning ease; they grant no learned skill or spells by themselves. Space affinity does not grant Space casting. Time is unavailable.
6. Starting Magical Study: up to two actual introductory studies, each giving its school at level 3 and two named techniques. Study can be outside your affinities. Choose none for a martial or untrained beginning. Space and Time are not introductory studies.
7. Practical Training: one physical combat approach or scholarly practice. Its listed effects apply; it does not force a weapon or job.
8. Profession & Useful Skills: one prior practical specialty, giving its listed +2 utility skill. It grants neither a current appointment nor a guild license.
9. Starting Equipment: choose one of the eleven available casting, martial or practical options. Hand-Casting Practice supplies no extra item. Tools and kits are actual listed starting possessions, supplied once.
10. Temperament: optional, up to two. Narrative preferences only; no personality grants a skill bonus.
11. First Impression: optional visible presentation, not a guaranteed reaction.
12. Personal Motivation: optional interest, not a compulsory quest or invented past.
13. Personal Complication: optional difficulty, not a forced penalty, trauma or action.

The maximum combined selection count is seventeen. Category maxSelections limits are native settings; the minimum choices requested in the text are guidance, not an invented minSelections field. The ordinary appearance/background input remains available. No custom multi-screen UI, separate species mechanic or automatic acquisition system was invented.

## Example directions

- A Phoenix Wing spellblade can pair Force and Passion, choose Lightning affinity and study Lightning and Arcane, take Force Training and a blade. Innate affinity and learned repertoire need not be identical.
- A research-minded noncombatant can pair Logic and Intuition, take an appropriate affinity, one introductory study, Scholarly Practice and a survey kit. The kit does not supply a court key or field authorization.
- A martial traveler can pair Force and Finesse, choose no learned magical study, take practiced combat training and handwraps or a blade. Foundational Spellcraft knowledge does not turn them into a trained caster.

The selected Story Start establishes actual admission, accepted work or independent status. A Guild Household origin does not replace the licensure exam. A Merchant Apprentice profession does not create stock, property or a shop.

## Live creation checks

Create a new character in Voyage and check category order, empty optional choices, different aptitudes, three affinities, two studies and named moves, martial/no-study play, exactly one kit, short adult fae, humanlike oni, bird-featured beastfolk and mixed ancestry. Check that Time and Space Studies are absent and no omitted choice is silently filled. Confirm appearance is present in both biography and image-facing description. Existing characters are not migrated or retroactively granted these creation choices.
