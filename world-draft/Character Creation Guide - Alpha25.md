# Character Creation — Alpha25

Use the full Alpha25 world for a coherent import. Character Creation - Alpha25.json is a partial merge fragment, not a standalone world. All prior ancestry, aptitude, profession, study, equipment and optional personality choices remain, except Space Affinity has been removed. Nineteen ordinary affinities remain separate from the nineteen starting studies; each study grants its actual skill and starter pair. Neither Space nor Time is selectable at creation.

The optional Appearance Builder is the final category. Its seven subcategories offer hair color (14), hairstyle (12), eye color (12), skin tone (10), build (7), outfit (8), and portrait background (8). Choose up to one per group, up to seven overall, or skip. These are recommendations per group; there is no undocumented hard subcategory limit. They grant no items, skills, stats or powers. Your explicit written appearance overrides a conflicting selection. A background changes the illustration rather than the map position. A clothing look does not provide armor or change enrollment or employment.

Portraits keep the existing standing, centered hip-up composition, vertical 2:3 format, full hair/head silhouette and 12.5–15% top margin (17% maximum), with crisp 2D anime treatment and no realism, semi-realism or text. No separate realism/style or full-body framing menu was added.

Academy First Day starts establish an enrolled first-year, a timetable, a headmaster welcome and the first mandatory Magic Theory lesson. Faculty work starts establish accepted instructor appointments with a subject, classroom and teaching tomorrow, leaving today for preparation.
