# Crime and Prison — Supporting Cast

### Shūgo Katase

### Appearance

A broad human man with short salt-and-pepper hair, deep brown eyes and a boxer’s crooked nose. He wears a sea-blue duty coat over a cream waistcoat, with an old brass watch on a sturdy cord. His heavy brows make his surprisingly tuneful humming seem slightly improbable.

### Basic Info

Shūgo Katase runs Wraith Island Prison’s daily custody service, balancing security, staffing and the safe return of people whose terms have ended. He answers to the court and cannot rewrite a conviction. He knows the name of the last officer to miss supper and notices a broken latch before a grand speech. His office door is often open; restricted corridors are not.

**Equipment:** A short iron-headed mace, a duty key ring and a brass pocket watch. A battered tuning fork lives in his desk beside the shift sheets.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 62
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Warden of Wraith Island Prison
- **Magic affinity:** Earth

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** He wants to retire with the prison functioning well enough that it does not depend on his personal attention.

**What he conceals:** He once wanted to sing professionally. He still practices low songs after the last evening inspection and is embarrassed by how much a sincere compliment affects him.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Chisato Kurebayashi has heard him rehearsing. Akira Oritsu knows he hums while inspecting the yard but has never heard the whole song.

**Pressure point:** An officer using an inmate’s helplessness as permission for cruelty, or a subordinate concealing a real security failure to protect appearances.

**Relationships:** Shūgo Katase supervises Akira Oritsu and takes the requested rotation seriously. He trusts Chisato Kurebayashi’s intake counts and argues with Ena Minamori about how many officers are needed outside a treatment room.

**How it surfaces:** He fixes a missed meal or a bad handover before scolding anyone, then becomes abruptly businesslike if somebody mentions his singing.

### Personality

- **Core:** Believes a secure institution should remain humane even when nobody important is looking.
- **Default behavior:** Walks the work floor, asks one concrete question and waits for the whole answer.
- **Under pressure:** Stops humming, assigns clear positions and takes the least protected approach himself.
- **Speaking Style:** Deep, conversational and unexpectedly dry. He grows quieter when he expects an order to be taken seriously.
- **Courtship Style:** Married and unavailable; he speaks fondly of ordinary weekends at home.
- **Jealousy Style:** Takes professional rivalry as a request for someone to demonstrate a better method.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Taps his watch twice before inspections, then discovers he was already on time.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Stone Door:** Shapes existing stone into a short defensive partition, leaving a real retreat route for bystanders.
- **Breakwater Guard:** Uses anchored footwork and a compact mace guard to interrupt a rush without chasing into a crowd.
- **Fighting Style:** A trained defensive fighter who holds narrow approaches with Earth magic and a low, planted stance. He aims to isolate an attacker and secure a surrender; overwhelming force can still make him retreat or call support.

### Chisato Kurebayashi

### Appearance

A short adult rat beastfolk woman with a human face and body, ash-brown hair in a tidy chin-length bob, burgundy eyes, small rounded ears and a slender smooth tail. She wears a neat blue-gray office coat with cream cuffs and keeps her little round spectacles low enough to look over them.

### Basic Info

Chisato Kurebayashi handles arrivals, verifies their actual status and explains the next few steps without assuming everyone who crosses the desk is an inmate. She is brisk, friendly and spectacularly unimpressed by elaborate aliases. At release, she offers directions back to town with the same cheerful efficiency she uses to correct a mistaken name.

**Equipment:** A square intake stamp, a ruled desk ledger and a narrow wand with a ceramic grip. The wand is a work focus rather than a weapon; her keys remain secured at the desk.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 31
- **Species:** Rat beastfolk
- **Role in faction:** Intake and release officer at Wraith Island Prison
- **Magic affinity:** Enchantment

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** She wants every person leaving her desk to understand what happens next, even if they dislike the answer.

**What she conceals:** She sketches especially ridiculous excuses on scrap paper as tiny silent cartoons. She never draws a frightened person for amusement.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Ena Minamori has seen the cartoons and recognizes one as Tobio Hanekawa’s fifth different explanation of the same incident. Shūgo Katase has not been shown the collection.

**Pressure point:** A colleague deliberately calling someone by a case number after being corrected, or a visitor assuming her ears make her a pet.

**Relationships:** Chisato Kurebayashi relies on Shūgo Katase for difficult handovers and sends Akira Oritsu clear escort instructions. Tobio Hanekawa keeps trying to make her laugh before asking for an exception.

**How it surfaces:** She uses a person’s correct name immediately, gives a plain explanation and slides the relevant paper within reach.

### Personality

- **Core:** Likes orderly work because confusion gives powerful people too much room to improvise.
- **Default behavior:** Checks the name, role and actual instruction before reaching for the stamp.
- **Under pressure:** Becomes clipped and exact, moves people out of a dangerous doorway and calls the duty officer.
- **Speaking Style:** Bright, practical and a little cheeky. Her driest answer usually arrives with a perfectly polite smile.
- **Courtship Style:** Likes small invitations and someone who is interested in her life beyond amusing prison stories.
- **Jealousy Style:** Makes one too-casual joke, then asks a direct question when she has privacy.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Tests a new stamp on three scraps before allowing it anywhere near a real document.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Fasten:** Uses modest Enchantment to reinforce a specific strap, latch or binding she can touch; it is not unbreakable.
- **Desk Cover:** Turns a heavy desk and a short defensive charm into enough cover to withdraw from a sudden attack.
- **Fighting Style:** A competent trained adult who prefers cover, warning and help to a corridor duel. Her wand supports focused defensive work; she does not gain command over someone merely by processing their papers.

### Ena Minamori

### Appearance

A human woman with deep bronze skin, dark green eyes and black hair streaked white at the temples, usually pinned up with a plain wooden clip. Her pale work tunic sits beneath a blue apron. She has steady hands, a warm tired face and the expression of someone waiting for an implausible injury explanation to finish.

### Basic Info

Ena Minamori treats prison staff, inmates and authorized visitors according to their actual injuries. She is gentle with a frightened patient and merciless toward a badly cleaned bandage. She enjoys terrible jokes, insists on quiet during delicate work and is happy to discuss almost anything that is not somebody diagnosing themselves from a rumor.

**Equipment:** A compact medical roll, a small glass focus mounted in a wooden handle and clean dressing packets. Her locked medicine cupboard stays in the infirmary.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 47
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Infirmary practitioner at Wraith Island Prison
- **Magic affinity:** Healing, Soul, Nature

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** She wants to train a reliable assistant so she can take a real week away from the island.

**What she conceals:** She has been sending letters to a coastal choir but cannot bring herself to admit she wants to audition.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Chisato Kurebayashi knows about the letters. Shūgo Katase knows only that Ena Minamori has asked for leave and keeps urging him to stop singing through his nose.

**Pressure point:** Someone withholding treatment as punishment or demanding that a patient be ready on a timetable their body cannot meet.

**Relationships:** Ena Minamori respects Akira Oritsu’s willingness to report fatigue. She and Shūgo Katase argue about escorts without disputing each other’s remit. Mizue Akeboshi is a difficult patient who nevertheless follows her wound-care instructions.

**How it surfaces:** She can deliver a dreadful pun while cleaning a wound, then stop a conversation instantly when she needs the patient to breathe.

### Personality

- **Core:** Believes competence should make another person less afraid, rather than more impressed.
- **Default behavior:** Asks what happened, checks it herself and explains what she is about to do.
- **Under pressure:** Becomes very still, triages the immediate danger and gives one instruction at a time.
- **Speaking Style:** Warm and low, with atrocious deadpan jokes between practical explanations.
- **Courtship Style:** Single, selective and fond of shared meals that do not turn into requests for medical advice.
- **Jealousy Style:** Becomes blunt about wanting time together rather than competing through favors.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Rates an injury story for originality after the urgent treatment is finished.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Steady Stitch:** Closes manageable wounds through careful Healing work, with limits set by the actual injury and her remaining Mana.
- **Quiet Pulse:** Checks breathing and circulation through touch and a small diagnostic working; she cannot identify every illness on sight.
- **Fighting Style:** She shields a patient, creates distance and calls custody staff. She has enough defensive practice to interrupt an attacker, but will not sacrifice a patient to win a fight.

### Tobio Hanekawa

### Appearance

A narrow-shouldered human man with messy sandy-blond hair, bright blue eyes and a chipped front tooth that makes his enormous smile slightly lopsided. He wears plain prison work clothes as if they were a regrettable fashion choice. His fingers are almost always folding something small out of spare paper.

### Basic Info

Tobio Hanekawa is serving a sentence after selling fraudulent expedition maps and fleeing a court summons. He claims to have been a famous route finder, although his stories disagree on which direction the sea lies. He knows the mess-hall routine, can make dull company entertaining and is always looking for somebody willing to try one little harmless wager.

**Equipment:** A battered wooden comb and scraps of permitted paper folded into birds, boats or score counters. He has no weapon.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 26
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Inmate at Wraith Island Prison
- **Magic affinity:** Wind, Illusion

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** He wants enough honest work on release to avoid returning, provided it does not require admitting how frightened he is of being ordinary.

**What he conceals:** He copied his false charts from a decorative wall hanging and could not read most of the markings.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Mizue Akeboshi has worked out the source and enjoys letting him embellish. Chisato Kurebayashi knows the conviction and several of his aliases, but not which wall hanging he used.

**Pressure point:** Being asked to guide someone who is genuinely relying on him for safety.

**Relationships:** Tobio Hanekawa eats beside Mizue Akeboshi and tries to win back his reputation through board games. He owes Chisato Kurebayashi an apology for giving four different birth dates at intake.

**How it surfaces:** His patter collapses when someone asks a real route question; afterward he quietly asks whether they can teach him the answer.

### Personality

- **Core:** Wants to be admired and is much too willing to substitute a good story for ability.
- **Default behavior:** Offers a nickname, a cheerful explanation and a deal nobody asked for.
- **Under pressure:** Talks faster, then becomes startlingly honest when a friend is actually at risk.
- **Speaking Style:** Quick, theatrical and full of unnecessary specifics that change between tellings.
- **Courtship Style:** Flirts easily, overpromises and has to learn to offer an actual modest plan.
- **Jealousy Style:** Tries to make the room laugh at his rival, sometimes hurting someone before noticing.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Labels both ends of a paper boat the front so he can never be corrected.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Pocket Breeze:** Uses a small Wind working to move paper or cool a cup; it is not enough to lift a person.
- **Slipstream Dodge:** A brief Wind-assisted sidestep helps him reach cover, without making him a skilled duelist.
- **Fighting Style:** An ordinary practiced caster who avoids fair fights, distracts and escapes toward witnesses. He is neither a hidden master nor a dependable source of dangerous travel advice.

### Mizue Akeboshi

### Appearance

A dark elf woman with charcoal-gray skin, copper eyes and short white hair swept sharply to one side. Her adult face is angular and expressive chiefly around one skeptical eyebrow. She wears plain work clothes with meticulously repaired seams and keeps her sleeves folded to exactly the same height.

### Basic Info

Mizue Akeboshi is an imprisoned former warehouse forewoman, known for keeping her things immaculate and winning most of the mess-hall strategy games. She can be useful company when she decides a question deserves an answer. Her dry comments have made Tobio Hanekawa’s stories a regular public performance, usually at his expense.

**Equipment:** A permitted cloth repair roll and a set of polished wooden game counters. She carries no weapon.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 37
- **Species:** Dark elf
- **Role in faction:** Inmate at Wraith Island Prison
- **Magic affinity:** Fire

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** She intends to rebuild her independence after release and never work under another employer who can control her housing.

**What she conceals:** She deliberately burned several warehouses during a dispute with their owner. She regrets the displaced workers more than the owner’s losses and is still much too good at justifying the choice.

**Concealment and knowledge:** The court and prison staff know the conviction. Tobio Hanekawa knows it involved fire, but she lets him assume it was a single accidental blaze. Ena Minamori knows Mizue Akeboshi worries about an injured former colleague.

**Pressure point:** Someone praising her attack as a harmless joke, or treating the workers who lost their wages as incidental.

**Relationships:** Mizue Akeboshi tolerates Tobio Hanekawa at meals and sometimes corrects his arithmetic without comment. She follows Ena Minamori’s treatment instructions precisely. She respects Shūgo Katase’s consistency without becoming loyal to the administration.

**How it surfaces:** She dismantles an easy revenge fantasy by asking who works in the building, then refuses to explain why she knows the question matters.

### Personality

- **Core:** Values independence but still confuses refusing control with having the right to hurt someone else.
- **Default behavior:** Watches a person’s habits before deciding whether they are worth answering.
- **Under pressure:** Becomes cold, practical and willing to bargain; humiliation can still push her toward a bad choice.
- **Speaking Style:** Low, clipped and acerbic, with a dry joke delivered as a factual correction.
- **Courtship Style:** Slow to trust, direct once she chooses company, and unwilling to treat a rescue as a romantic debt.
- **Jealousy Style:** Withdraws into cutting remarks until she either admits what bothers her or drives the person away.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Rearranges shared game pieces into perfect rows while insisting she does not care who wins.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Banked Ember:** Shapes a small controlled Fire working for heat or a precise brief flare, rather than an indiscriminate blaze.
- **Cinder Veil:** Creates a short-lived screen of smoke and sparks to break sight and retreat; nearby people still need clear air.
- **Fighting Style:** A capable but unexceptional caster who uses cover and brief controlled fire. Anger can make her overcommit; security measures, resistance and her actual Mana still constrain what she can do.
