## ⛓️ Crime & Character Creation Update

Glowhaven has a few new consequences—and a lot more ways to make your character your own!

### 🚨 Crime & Consequences
- Serious, deliberate crimes can now lead to wanted posters, guard encounters and arrest.
- Wraith Island Prison gains **five new characters**, from its warden and staff to inmates with stories of their own.
- Spend your term talking, working or exploring prison life—or choose to serve the uneventful remainder quietly.
- Automatic wanted and custody mechanics currently support solo play.

### 🎨 More Character Customization
- Added optional **Beastfolk forms** and **Fae appearance and stature** choices.
- Expanded outfit and portrait-background options.
- Added optional portrait styles, waist-up or hip-up framing, and camera angles.
- Refined the default toward crisp, colorful **2D anime illustrations**, with more freedom in posing and composition.

### ✨ New Beginnings
- Added **four summoned-as-a-familiar starts**, one for each academy. Wake in a spell circle and meet the student responsible!
- Familiar bonds preserve your independence, though your summoner’s suffering may tug at your heart.
- Added **Your First Spell**, an optional quest to invent a small personal spell through Spellcraft.
- Refreshed world tips, added emojis to start titles, and eased the repeated emphasis on free afternoons.

**A little more trouble, a little more magic, and a lot more personality.** ✨
