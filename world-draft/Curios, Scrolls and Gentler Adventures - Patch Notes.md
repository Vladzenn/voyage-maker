## 🎒 Curios, Scrolls & Gentler Adventures

More useful gear, strange discoveries, and room to grow into adventuring!

- Added **300 items**, from affordable weapons and practical supplies to whimsical curios, dangerous enchanted gear, and rare treasures.
- Added **30 teaching scrolls with new spells**. Successful study teaches the formula and consumes the scroll; difficult discoveries reward careful research.
- Expanded suitable shop stock and exploration finds, with equipment bonuses, reference prices, and meaningful trade-offs.
- Rebalanced beginner guild encounters and softened progression across early and midgame field areas.
- Routine guild offers now better match the party’s experience. Harder work remains available when deliberately requested.

Pack sensibly. Some bargains have teeth.
