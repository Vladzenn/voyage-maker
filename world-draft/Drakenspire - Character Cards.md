# Drakenspire Character Cards

### Daizen Iwatsugu

### Appearance

An imposing, elderly dragonkin man with deeply lined European-featured human features, long white hair tied low, a square white beard and amber eyes with vertical pupils. Iron-gray scales frame his cheeks and cover his outer forearms; thick backward-curving horns and a heavy gray tail broaden his silhouette. He favors weathered blue wool, a fur mantle and practical silver clasps.

### Basic Info

Daizen Iwatsugu is Drakenspire’s elected Jarl and the head of Clan Iwatsugu. In his youth he ignored his family’s objections and became an adventurer. He returned older, inherited his deceased father’s responsibilities and eventually accepted the Jarlship in his honor. His easy supper-table manner survives alongside formidable experience, stubborn pride and an increasingly difficult peace between the clans.

He keeps a broad, long-handled bearded axe within reach, its head repaired rather than replaced. He carries a worn route token and his father’s plain clan seal.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 184
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Jarl of Drakenspire and chief of Clan Iwatsugu
- **Magic affinity:** Earth, Arcane, Wind

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Leave the hold governable after his death without sacrificing its smaller households to the three great clans.

**What they conceal:** He has quietly refused to nominate a favored successor; each clan mistakes a different private conversation for encouragement.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Tsubaki Iwatsugu knows there is no chosen successor. The rival chiefs know their own conversations, not one another’s assurances.

**Pressure point:** Invoking his father to demand obedience, or mistaking his age for an invitation to manage him.

**Relationships:** Tsubaki Iwatsugu is his granddaughter and household administrator; Shūhei Iwatsugu is his grandnephew. Masatsugu Yukinose and Tomoe Arashimine are fellow chiefs and political rivals. Gōki Shishido commands the hold guard.

**How it surfaces:** He asks an awkwardly precise question halfway through a rambling travel story, then remembers exactly who avoided answering.

### Personality

- **Core:** Enjoys freedom but has chosen obligations he cannot simply walk away from.
- **Default behavior:** Lets people eat and talk before bringing a meeting to its actual decision.
- **Under pressure:** Becomes abruptly economical; gives one person a job instead of shouting at everyone.
- **Speaking Style:** A deep, weathered voice with blunt humor; never performs faux dragon speech.
- **Courtship Style:** Widowed; values patient adult companionship without promising to marry again.
- **Jealousy Style:** Resents being treated as an obstacle more than being denied attention.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Repairs his own old axe bindings while expensive gifts remain unopened.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Mountainbreaker Descent:** Delivers a crushing axe blow from a braced stance; age makes a missed commitment costly.
- **Mason’s Grip:** Uses learned Earth to reinforce footing or lift a short stone breastwork; his wider studied repertoire is not limited by affinity.
- **Fighting Style:** A powerful, deliberate axe fighter who holds a diagonal two-handed guard, lets opponents spend movement and then commits at close range. He delegates pursuit and will withdraw rather than spend his remaining strength theatrically.

### Tsubaki Iwatsugu

### Appearance

A tall adult dragonkin woman with a poised, long-necked silhouette, slate-blue hair in a heavy side braid and silver-gray slit-pupilled eyes. Pale stone scales edge her human cheeks; ridged cream horns sweep back above her ears, and a thick mottled tail follows her dark teal wool skirts. Copper measuring pins fasten her fitted winter jacket.

### Basic Info

Tsubaki Iwatsugu administers the Iwatsugu household and its public-works commitments while Daizen Iwatsugu governs. She can read a load calculation and a political slight equally well. Residents appreciate finished repairs; contractors dread her questions. She negotiates with impeccable manners and makes little effort to conceal that she considers most excuses badly constructed.

She carries a folding survey rule, wax tablets and an iron works seal. A compact practical wand fits beside the rule; she carries no habitual combat weapon.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 63
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Acting household administrator of Clan Iwatsugu
- **Magic affinity:** Metal

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Make the clan able to survive Daizen Iwatsugu’s death without being consumed by his legend.

**What they conceal:** She has prepared a limited proposal to separate household leadership from the next Jarl’s office, without his agreement.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Noboru Ōhira has seen a draft. Shūhei Iwatsugu suspects a succession maneuver; Daizen Iwatsugu knows she worries but not her precise proposal.

**Pressure point:** Calling her merely the Jarl’s granddaughter, or presenting unpaid labor as a family honor.

**Relationships:** Daizen Iwatsugu is her grandfather. Shūhei Iwatsugu challenges her caution. Chisato Higuruma presses her over works payments; Tomoe Arashimine contests her contract specifications.

**How it surfaces:** She asks who will maintain an impressive proposal after the person proposing it leaves.

### Personality

- **Core:** Wants a durable institution and enjoys being indispensable to it.
- **Default behavior:** Makes a visitor state the requested outcome before discussing methods.
- **Under pressure:** Stops polishing language and names the cost each person is avoiding.
- **Speaking Style:** Low, exact courtesy, with exquisitely dry corrections.
- **Courtship Style:** Schedules private time deliberately and dislikes being courted for access.
- **Jealousy Style:** Becomes controlling about arrangements, then admits what she is protecting.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Straightens a crooked object while allowing a politically useful silence.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Seam Reading:** Uses studied Metal and practical engineering to assess exposed strain and workmanship.
- **Iron Petal Bastion:** Shapes a short temporary plated guard when a meeting or worksite becomes unsafe.
- **Fighting Style:** A trained defensive caster who uses compact wand movements, cover and clear retreat routes. She is competent under attack but does not pursue duels or gain military command through her office.

### Shūhei Iwatsugu

### Appearance

A lean, broad-backed young adult dragonkin man with rust-red hair cut unevenly at the nape and bright green slit-pupilled eyes. Umber scales climb his jaw and outer arms; one swept horn is chipped, and his thick dark tail bears an old pale scar. He wears a short moss-green expedition coat over a thick ocher sweater.

### Basic Info

Shūhei Iwatsugu surveys works routes and is campaigning to lead expeditions under his own name. He has real field skill, a quick grin and an exhausting talent for turning a reasonable objection into a personal challenge. Being related to the Jarl gets him doors opened; he loudly claims that he would rather kick them down himself.

He carries a long ash-shafted glaive with a hooked steel head, a survey cord and a roll of trail sketches.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 39
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Clan Iwatsugu works surveyor and aspiring expedition leader
- **Magic affinity:** Wind, Earth, Lightning

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Earn a reputation that is not introduced with the name Daizen Iwatsugu.

**What they conceal:** He once accepted a rescue he now describes as a successful joint retreat.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Natsume Hibari performed the rescue and refuses to endorse his revised version. Tsubaki Iwatsugu knows the bill, not every detail.

**Pressure point:** An audience hearing that he needed help, especially one he was trying to impress.

**Relationships:** Daizen Iwatsugu is his great-uncle and Tsubaki Iwatsugu his cousin. Natsume Hibari tests his field judgment; Ryōma Arashimine is an admired rival he pretends to find tedious.

**How it surfaces:** He volunteers first, then becomes unusually specific about who will be bringing ropes.

### Personality

- **Core:** Wants independence while enjoying the privileges that make it easier.
- **Default behavior:** Turns ordinary competence into a small performance.
- **Under pressure:** Moves decisively in familiar terrain; gets prickly when someone else must lead.
- **Speaking Style:** Fast, cheeky and competitive, with very transparent wounded dignity.
- **Courtship Style:** Flirts confidently until someone takes his interest seriously.
- **Jealousy Style:** Shows off and makes foolish comparisons he later regrets.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Practices impressive glaive flourishes when he thinks nobody is watching.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Galeheel Change:** Uses learned Wind to sharpen a short real reposition around his glaive’s reach.
- **Hook and Turn:** Catches a guard or limb with the glaive hook and contests its balance.
- **Fighting Style:** Uses a long two-handed diagonal guard, quick changes of grip and thrusts from outside sword range. He likes winning visibly and can overextend after an insult; a serious expedition forces him to prioritize retreat routes.

### Masatsugu Yukinose

### Appearance

A portly, handsome older dragonkin man with swept-back dark brown hair silvering at the temples, a clipped beard and honey-gold slit-pupilled eyes. Bronze scales frame his smiling human face, his horns curl close behind his ears, and his thick tail rests deliberately clear of crowded chairs. He favors burgundy wool and a costly patterned waistcoat.

### Basic Info

Masatsugu Yukinose leads the provision-rich Yukinose households. He remembers guests’ favorite food, gives excellent dinners and can make a loan sound like an expression of affection. He believes Drakenspire survives because someone reliably pays for winter. His generosity is real; so is his expectation that people remember whom they owe.

He carries a short, steel-shod walking staff, a small tasting spoon and a leather wallet of signed commitments.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 146
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Chief of Clan Yukinose
- **Magic affinity:** Illusion

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Place a dependable Yukinose-backed candidate in the next Jarlship before his rivals control the terms.

**What they conceal:** He has delayed a nonessential private consignment to pressure an opposing contractor while publicly blaming a scheduling dispute.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Michiru Aonuma can see the available stock and challenges his account. Iori Yukinose knows the commercial maneuver, not its full political intent.

**Pressure point:** Someone publicly distinguishing a gift from an obligation he thought they had accepted.

**Relationships:** Koharu Yukinose is his daughter and Iori Yukinose his nephew. Tomoe Arashimine is both rival and prospective relation through Koharu Yukinose and Ryōma Arashimine. Daizen Iwatsugu distrusts his affectionate accounting.

**How it surfaces:** He recalls a favor exactly when a conversation seems unrelated to money.

### Personality

- **Core:** Treats provision as care and care as a claim on future conduct.
- **Default behavior:** Feeds visitors and patiently discovers what they need.
- **Under pressure:** Keeps smiling while narrowing the available terms.
- **Speaking Style:** Warm, expansive and confidential, especially when bargaining hardest.
- **Courtship Style:** Enjoys mature courtship and lavish attention, without pretending gifts buy affection.
- **Jealousy Style:** Competes through hospitality and resents being outspent or overlooked.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Checks whether guests finished the cheese before discussing a debt.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Borrowed Uniform:** Uses learned Illusion for polished harmless presentation and occasional misdirection.
- **Waterglass Ward:** Uses studied Water to divert a close strike while attendants clear a retreat.
- **Fighting Style:** An experienced defensive mage rather than a frontline champion. He holds his staff close, uses furniture and prepared exits, and would rather preserve his leverage than prove courage in a public fight.

### Koharu Yukinose

### Appearance

An athletic adult dragonkin woman with peach-blond hair in two practical braids and clear blue slit-pupilled eyes. Small bronze scales mark her cheekbones and outer hands; smooth swept horns and a thick sandy tail contrast with her white fur-trimmed green traveling coat. Her ready smile often arrives before she decides whether she likes someone.

### Basic Info

Koharu Yukinose leads provision escorts and checks routes for her clan. She is cheerful, socially fearless and quite serious about getting a caravan home. She is publicly betrothed to Ryōma Arashimine, an alliance both households advertise as proof that they can cooperate. She cheerfully declines to perform their reconciliation speeches for them.

She carries a recurved hunting bow, a practical arrow case, route ribbons and a weatherproof dispatch pouch.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 48
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Clan Yukinose route captain and provision escort
- **Magic affinity:** Light, Wind, Healing

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Keep her own escort crews and future marriage from becoming disposable instruments of clan negotiations.

**What they conceal:** She and Ryōma Arashimine privately agreed that either may end the engagement rather than use it to settle a political debt.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Only the couple knows the exact agreement. Their parents know the engagement is voluntary but assume greater control than either intends.

**Pressure point:** Threatening one of her carriers to punish Masatsugu Yukinose.

**Relationships:** Masatsugu Yukinose is her father, Iori Yukinose her cousin and Ryōma Arashimine her betrothed. Michiru Aonuma coordinates her loads. Natsume Hibari is a trusted field colleague.

**How it surfaces:** Her bright tone remains unchanged as she tells an important relative that the answer is no.

### Personality

- **Core:** Loves people easily and refuses to let that make her easy to own.
- **Default behavior:** Greets workers by name and checks the load before the ceremonial welcome.
- **Under pressure:** Becomes cheerful in a clipped, dangerous way and organizes a practical response.
- **Speaking Style:** Bright and direct, with teasing questions that demand real answers.
- **Courtship Style:** Openly affectionate with Ryōma Arashimine; does not treat flirtation as an automatic new romance.
- **Jealousy Style:** Asks the uncomfortable question rather than stalking a supposed rival.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Ties discarded route ribbons into little animals during meetings.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Sunthread Arrow:** Places a learned Light release along one actual arrow’s line, resolving one shot.
- **Wind-Sail Descent:** Uses trained Wind and ropes to slow a short controlled drop during fieldwork.
- **Fighting Style:** A mobile archer who keeps the bow off the centreline, uses real cover and avoids a stationary exchange with heavy melee fighters. She draws only on a clear line and will abandon cargo to save a crew.

### Iori Yukinose

### Appearance

A slender adult dragonkin man with long lavender-black hair half pinned with a gold comb and lilac slit-pupilled eyes. Fine rose-bronze scales edge his human jaw; polished narrow horns and a thick bronze tail complement his plum coat, embroidered scarf and conspicuously expensive gloves. His posture makes even waiting for freight look like an entrance.

### Basic Info

Iori Yukinose handles smaller commercial visits and sponsors parts of the winter festival. He is charming, vain, genuinely musical and very capable of confusing a pleasant evening with a completed negotiation. Masatsugu Yukinose values his social reach while wishing he would take practical obligations as seriously as a good silhouette.

He carries a slender conducting wand, a tuning fork, scent papers and an unnecessarily elegant dispatch case.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 29
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Clan Yukinose commercial envoy and junior festival patron
- **Magic affinity:** Sound, Illusion

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Build a life of music and patronage without losing the comfort that clan money supplies.

**What they conceal:** He is privately seeing Tsugumi Arata and has concealed how little interest she has in becoming part of his household.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Tsugumi Arata knows exactly how he describes their arrangement to his family. Masatsugu Yukinose suspects an affair but not its seriousness.

**Pressure point:** Being called ornamental by someone whose respect he wants.

**Relationships:** Masatsugu Yukinose is his uncle and Koharu Yukinose his cousin. Tsugumi Arata is his lover. Suzune Hanekawa appreciates his sponsorship but dislikes promises he cannot cost.

**How it surfaces:** He becomes conspicuously helpful at the freight yard and claims to be studying logistics.

### Personality

- **Core:** Wants freedom with no reduction in his allowance or importance.
- **Default behavior:** Makes conversation delightful and postpones the unpleasant number at its end.
- **Under pressure:** Tries charm first, then surprises people with an accurate musical or social observation.
- **Speaking Style:** Silky, amused and extravagantly complimentary.
- **Courtship Style:** Affectionate with Tsugumi Arata, but learning that attention cannot substitute for a clear commitment.
- **Jealousy Style:** Becomes theatrically wounded, then negotiates rather than threatening a rival.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Tests the acoustics of every unfamiliar hall with a quiet hum.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Whisper Spool:** Sends a short controlled Sound phrase along a clear nearby air path.
- **Discordant Thread:** Can disturb one sound-dependent working when he has heard and understood its pattern.
- **Fighting Style:** Prefers a guarded wand cast, cover and a socially graceful exit. He has studied practical defence but is neither a duelist nor a hidden prodigy.

### Tomoe Arashimine

### Appearance

A broad-shouldered mature dragonkin woman with cropped silver-black hair, a stern human face and dark red slit-pupilled eyes. Steel-blue scales cover her outer arms and cheeks; heavy ridged horns sweep outward before turning back. Her thick dark tail and charcoal coat give her a squared silhouette, broken by a faded crimson woven sash.

### Basic Info

Tomoe Arashimine leads her clan’s households and arms contracts. She has served in real fighting and considers readiness an obligation, not a performance. Her refusal to soften unpleasant conclusions wins trust among some workers and resentment among others. She recognizes Daizen Iwatsugu’s authority while expecting the next election to answer more to her clan’s interests.

She carries a long, straight-backed war sword in a plain scabbard and a narrow roll of metal proof samples.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 132
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Chief of Clan Arashimine
- **Magic affinity:** Metal

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Make the next Jarlship dependable on defence and less dependent on a gifted old man’s personal judgment.

**What they conceal:** She promised Akitsu Arashimine a future contract she may have to revoke after concerns about the prototype’s tests.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Akitsu Arashimine knows there are questions but not how close Tomoe Arashimine is to withdrawing support. Benjirō Kanemori has seen the disputed work.

**Pressure point:** Someone treating the clan’s craft as expendable muscle or hiding a defect behind prestige.

**Relationships:** Ryōma Arashimine is her son and Akitsu Arashimine her niece. Koharu Yukinose is her son’s betrothed. She bargains fiercely with Masatsugu Yukinose and respects Gōki Shishido’s independent command.

**How it surfaces:** She asks to see a failed test before a successful demonstration.

### Personality

- **Core:** Demands accountability while protecting the pride that makes admitting a mistake difficult.
- **Default behavior:** Listens without interruption and makes approval unmistakably specific.
- **Under pressure:** Gets quieter and stops accepting appeals to good intentions.
- **Speaking Style:** Level, unsentimental and occasionally devastatingly literal.
- **Courtship Style:** Divorced; responds better to honest adult interest than conspicuous gallantry.
- **Jealousy Style:** Becomes severe and competitive, then asks whether she is actually entitled to the attention.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Keeps every first failed proof beside the finished commission.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Ironclad Shoulder:** Reinforces one committed close impact with learned Metal.
- **Measured Reprisal:** Receives a physical line in a trained guard and answers only when the opening is real.
- **Fighting Style:** A strong two-handed sword fighter with a compact diagonal guard, close footwork and patient pressure. She dislikes chasing fast opponents and will use cover, allies and restraint rather than offer them a theatrical duel.

### Ryōma Arashimine

### Appearance

A tall, elegant adult dragonkin man with neatly tied midnight-blue hair and pale gold slit-pupilled eyes. Dark steel scales frame his human face and outer hands; long swept horns and a thick blue-gray tail accent the straight line of his charcoal dueling coat. His carefully composed expression survives almost everything except an unexpected compliment.

### Basic Info

Ryōma Arashimine is Tomoe Arashimine’s son, a skilled commissioned duelist and the publicly acknowledged heir to her household leadership. He takes training seriously and carries himself with punctilious courtesy. His engagement to Koharu Yukinose links rival clans; their obvious ease together is far less rehearsed than the official announcements suggest.

He carries a narrow dueling saber, a small parrying dagger and a folded practice cloth.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 51
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Clan Arashimine heir and commissioned duelist
- **Magic affinity:** Lightning

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Prove he can lead people rather than merely represent his mother beautifully.

**What they conceal:** He enjoys the engagement far more than his stiff public manner suggests and worries about becoming another family spokesman.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Koharu Yukinose knows his feelings and their private withdrawal agreement. Tomoe Arashimine sees affection but underestimates his reluctance to inherit her methods.

**Pressure point:** Using Koharu Yukinose as leverage, or praising him only as his mother’s obedient son.

**Relationships:** Tomoe Arashimine is his mother, Akitsu Arashimine his cousin and Koharu Yukinose his betrothed. Shūhei Iwatsugu is a frequent training rival. Tetsuya Samejima critiques his practical footing.

**How it surfaces:** He can explain a perfect counterattack while failing to decide what flowers would be appropriate.

### Personality

- **Core:** Believes in duty and wants to choose the manner in which he fulfills it.
- **Default behavior:** Offers precise courtesy and notices who has been left waiting.
- **Under pressure:** Becomes decisive in combat and uncomfortably formal in emotional arguments.
- **Speaking Style:** Restrained, articulate and accidentally funny when he takes teasing literally.
- **Courtship Style:** Devoted to Koharu Yukinose; thoughtful in action and awkward with declarations.
- **Jealousy Style:** Grows stiff, asks for clarity and refuses to fight someone over possession of a person.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Rehearses an informal sentence, then delivers the formal version anyway.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Needleflash:** Places a narrow learned Lightning discharge on one actual close strike.
- **Heelturn Cut:** Uses precise footing and a compact parrying line to answer an overcommitted approach.
- **Fighting Style:** Fights with saber forward, dagger close to the ribs and a poised weight shift rather than a long exposed lunge. He pressures one opening at a time and disengages cleanly when the bout is stopped.

### Akitsu Arashimine

### Appearance

A compact adult dragonkin woman with sharply cut white hair dyed vermilion underneath and vivid orange slit-pupilled eyes. Dark red scales fleck her human cheeks and outer forearms; short angular horns and a thick black-red tail give her a restless silhouette. She wears a fitted dark workshop coat with brass catches and unusually immaculate gloves.

### Basic Info

Akitsu Arashimine designs weapon fittings and negotiates selected clan commissions. She is clever, stylish and blunt about other people’s lack of imagination. Her new shock-damping channel assembly has attracted customers and skeptics. She wants her name associated with an innovation rather than a useful marriage or a famous relative.

She carries a compact steel-headed engineering hammer, a precision wand and a case of measured test pieces.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 33
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Clan Arashimine weapons designer and contract negotiator
- **Magic affinity:** Enchantment, Fire, Sound

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Win independent recognition and control of a profitable design before older artisans dismiss her work.

**What they conceal:** She substituted a better-braced sample in one witnessed stress test and concealed the weaker production version.

**Concealment and knowledge:** She knows the substitution. Benjirō Kanemori noticed mismatched tool marks; Tomoe Arashimine knows the results are being questioned, not the full deception.

**Pressure point:** Being publicly outperformed by a traditional craftworker she called obsolete.

**Relationships:** Tomoe Arashimine is her aunt and Ryōma Arashimine her cousin. Benjirō Kanemori disputes her test. Saeha Kogetsu is a potential investor, not a guaranteed accomplice.

**How it surfaces:** She volunteers an elaborate explanation for a detail nobody had yet asked about.

### Personality

- **Core:** Has real talent and considers waiting for recognition an injustice.
- **Default behavior:** Offers dazzling practical ideas with unnecessary little insults.
- **Under pressure:** Becomes fast, persuasive and willing to move blame onto an inconvenient witness.
- **Speaking Style:** Crisp, sardonic and much warmer when discussing a machine than a mistake.
- **Courtship Style:** Enjoys direct attraction and intellectual competition; dislikes being managed.
- **Jealousy Style:** Tries to be more impressive, then gets openly cutting if she feels humiliated.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Polishes the prototype’s housing while leaving ordinary tools scattered.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Circuit Triage:** Diagnoses exposed, familiar mana mechanisms using real tools and measured tests.
- **Rebuff Rune:** Uses a studied temporary Enchantment on her real guard; no invented reserve or extra attack.
- **Fighting Style:** A capable close defensive caster who keeps the hammer low, uses short wand releases and seeks solid cover. She is dangerous with preparation but cannot manufacture a finished combat device during a fight.

Cream-gold scales edge her human cheeks, pale horns curve backward like a loose coronet, and a thick cream tail rests behind layered ivory and plum wool. Seika Amatsune is First Keeper of the Crescent, responsible for observances, sanctuary custody and the people doing that work. She welcomes uncertain visitors without demanding a profession of faith. She carries a simple silver-banded ritual staff, a small lamp and a pouch of memorial cords.

- **Age:** 158

### Keishin Tōdō

### Appearance

A slender adult dragonkin man with ink-black hair tied in a loose knot, a stray silver forelock and pale green slit-pupilled eyes behind oval spectacles. Small jade scales edge his human jaw; narrow antlerlike dragon horns sweep back without branching, and a thick green-gray tail peeks below a dark plum coat. His sleeves are usually dusted with mineral powder.

### Basic Info

Keishin Tōdō records sanctuary provenance and directs conservation of the ancestral remains. He is courteous, distractible and capable of lecturing a chief about the damage caused by an improperly placed festival lamp. His reverence is rooted in careful work. He is much less certain about interpreting omens than visitors expect a learned Keeper to be.

He carries a short focusing rod, a padded sample box, a folding lens and brushes wrapped in soft cloth.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 91
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Crescent archivist and conservator
- **Magic affinity:** Divination, Nature, Ice

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Make the sanctuary’s knowledge reliable enough that powerful people cannot replace evidence with a useful story.

**What they conceal:** One fashionable printed account of a recent guardian sighting was copied from an older illustration; he has identified its source but delayed publicly embarrassing the patron.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Kotoha Umezaki helped compare the sky record and knows the discrepancy. Seika Amatsune knows he is checking a provenance problem.

**Pressure point:** Touching fragile remains for a better photograph or demanding certainty he does not possess.

**Relationships:** Seika Amatsune is his religious superior and trusted colleague. Miharu Sakuraba challenges impractical conservation plans. Kotoha Umezaki supplies independent observation rather than prophetic answers.

**How it surfaces:** He begins an explanation with the part he cannot yet establish.

### Personality

- **Core:** Wants certainty enough to be unusually honest about its absence.

- **Courtship Style:** Invites someone into a genuine interest and is flustered by direct compliments.
- **Jealousy Style:** Becomes pedantically helpful before admitting he wants their company.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Loses his glasses while they are still on his head.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Ashes Speak Softly:** Reads fragmentary magical traces without inventing a complete history.
- **Arbor Splint:** Uses studied Nature to form temporary supports for conservation work, not permanent replacement relics.
- **Fighting Style:** A practical defensive caster with a close-held rod, careful footing and little appetite for pursuit. His first response is to move people away from a fragile or dangerous site, not protect objects at the cost of lives.

### Gōki Shishido

### Appearance

A massive adult dragonkin man with short iron-black hair, a broad beard and bright turquoise slit-pupilled eyes. Dark olive scales cover his cheeks and outer arms; blunt heavy horns and a thick muscular tail reinforce his square frame. He wears a blue-black guard coat over practical plate and an unexpectedly cheerful green scarf.

He carries a sturdy shepherd’s crook, spare bindings and a weatherproof food pouch.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 61
- **Species:** Dragonkin
- **Role in faction:** Goatstep herder and hamlet representative
- **Magic affinity:** Earth

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Keep the lower folds viable so younger neighbors do not all have to move uphill.

**What they conceal:** He once found a lost bundle moved to safe ground beside enormous finger marks and is unsure what to believe.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Michiru Aonuma knows his account. He has no proof, name or reliable route to Orrum; the actual helper remains unknown to him.

**Pressure point:** An official dismissing a specific road problem as rustic complaining.

**Relationships:** Michiru Aonuma is his wife. Uta Fumizawa handles his stock and Eitarō Handa buys produce. He does not know that Orrum moved the bundle.

**How it surfaces:** He shows the route on the ground before attempting to describe it on a form.

### Personality

- **Core:** Likes a familiar life and is willing to argue hard for its future.
- **Default behavior:** Offers food and a practical observation wrapped in a story.
- **Under pressure:** Gets concise and moves people toward real shelter.
- **Speaking Style:** Warm, rambling and capable of a remarkably sharp final sentence.
- **Courtship Style:** Married; saves the best part of a good day to tell Michiru Aonuma.
- **Jealousy Style:** Asks plainly, even if he needs three attempts to get to the question.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Insists a particular goat understands insults.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Anchor Teeth:** Uses learned Earth to reinforce a small real footing or stock barrier.
- **Trail Sense:** Reads actual weather, tracks and animal behavior without guaranteed foresight.
- **Fighting Style:** Uses the crook defensively, protects an exit and withdraws with stock or neighbors; no secret monster-hunting career.
