# Drakenspire - Map Art Preview

A fresh view of Drakenspire places its stone-built town high on a rocky mountain, with the ancestral dragon's crescent and fortress above denser inhabited terraces. Lower roads lead into snowy valleys, grazing folds, a small hamlet and the wider highland country.

A separate landmark guide accompanies the artwork. This is an art preview; it has not replaced the playable world's map.
