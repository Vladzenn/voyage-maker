# Drakenspire map artwork preview

A new Drakenspire map follows the revised layout with the mountain city and fortress on the right, the frozen basin on the left, and the hamlet and approach below. The stone town is much denser, with the original map's bright snowy colors, intricate buildings and warm inhabited details.

The artwork includes city services, craft and freight districts, and exterior cues for surrounding highland destinations. This is an artwork proposal for review; it has not been installed as the live world map.
