## Coverage

Thirty-nine of Drakenspire’s fifty-nine locations have one or more new authored contacts at exact named areas. Some broader institutions share the people working in their separately mapped halls and offices. Ordinary incidental staff can still exist without new permanent records. Shops, households, public work, craft, food, lodging, entertainment and fieldwork all have anchors. Wilderness, ruins, the processional street on an ordinary day and some shared sites remain deliberately without a permanent authored resident.
