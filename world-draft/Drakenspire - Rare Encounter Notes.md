# Drakenspire Rare Encounters

Rovakthuun and Orrum begin off-stage with blank location and area. The documented native npc-placement effect supports moveHere for the existing record and moveAway to remove it from the scene while keeping it. No public lair, hidden-NPC property or new map marker is invented.

At Dragonwatch Balcony, a genuinely completed extended observation can make one guardian roll: one in ten. A failed or successful eligible attempt prevents another roll for 24 game turns. At the White Silence, a genuinely completed careful field search has one chance in five, with 16 turns between eligible attempts. These are test calibrations, not claims that each turn equals a fixed hour. No player-level match or mandatory quest guarantees either being.

Arriving, map clicking, asking where the being is, a quick glance, a purchased rumor, prayer, an invented success or repeated demand does not qualify. A native location check is exact; interpreting completed observation/search uses an AI semantic condition. Chance and attempt timing are then handled by JavaScript storage and Math.random. This makes the random roll and cooldown explicit, but the quality of the semantic judgment still requires playtesting.

On a successful roll the exact authored NPC moves to the actual party area and becomes known. No copy, free gift, quest completion, hostility, attraction or recruitment is added. Existing Health, relationship and state remain. A defeated individual cannot be respawned by the watch trigger. An actual party member or creature already in combat is not dragged into a new scene.

Two departure hooks remove the visitor only when the completed story actually confirms a living departure. A simple state continuity hook also moves a living, noncombat, nonparty visitor off-stage after the party leaves. Remaining in an actual conversation does not erase the speaker. A corpse, restrained combatant or actual companion is not silently moved away.

Local tests cover chance boundaries, failed-attempt timing, unqualified actions, wrong locations, combat, death, repeated attempts, same-record placement, state preservation and departure. They supply semantic decisions and simulate native effects; no live Voyage import or playtest has been performed. Check in Voyage that natural observation wording qualifies only when intended, a successful placement appears in the current scene, and actual departure removes the creature without resetting it.

Reference: community NPC and trigger documentation at https://wiki.unofficial.voyage/world/npcs and https://wiki.unofficial.voyage/mechanics/triggerDefinitions.
