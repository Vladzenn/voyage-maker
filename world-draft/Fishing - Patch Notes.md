# 🐹 Minor Patch 4

### 🎣 Added Fishing

Sometimes an adventure needs a quieter afternoon. Bring a rod, find a good stretch of water and see what bites!

**Fishing is now a practical skill of its own**, available wherever you find suitable, reachable water. Cast in rivers, lakes, harbors, estuaries, rocky shores and offshore waters. Beginners can learn through actual practice, while experienced fishers have stranger specimens to look for.

- Ordinary catches resolve quickly. Rare fish can put up a more interesting struggle, giving you another decision about how to bring them in—or whether to let them go.
- Choose between reusable lures and consumable bait. Different waters, weather and bait choices influence what you might hook.
- Find rods, floats, spinners, line, creels and other useful tackle. An optional Fishing Starter Kit is also available during character creation.
- Discover different fish in Glowhaven, Drakenspire, Asterveil and Akitsuhara, from mountain char and cloudscale carp to elusive koi. Unfamiliar waters can hold their own surprises too.
- Cast your rod or lower your hook to start fishing. When two party members use bait on the same turn, a miss and a catch can resolve independently.
- Improved Studio import compatibility for the fishing update.

### 🌦️ Watch the Water

Glowhaven now has changing inland and coastal weather. Clear spells, cloud cover, rain, fog and wind can favor different catches, and conditions hold for a while instead of changing with every cast.

A passing squall is a good reason to wait before fishing exposed offshore water. Sheltered places and ordinary local knowledge still have their uses.

### 🐟 Something Worth Bringing Home

Catch familiar supper fish, unusual specimens and a few particularly elusive finds. Keep an actual catch for cooking, sell it to a willing buyer, help with a specimen request or release it.

- Fishing records remember discovered species and the traveling party’s best measured catches.
- Meet Makiho for practical advice and ordinary tackle.
- Take on small optional commissions involving lessons, fresh fish and curious natural colors, with objectives tracked in the quest journal.

No grand calling required. Just a line, a little patience, and perhaps one more cast.
