# 🌊 Glowhaven Expanded

There is more to Glowhaven than the view from the academy gates.

The countryside and eastern waters now have more people to meet, work to take and places worth taking your time with—from a busy windmill and a riverside abbey to reef landings, old stonework and a ship whose crew rarely agrees quietly.

### ⛵ Meet the Gilded Albatross

Captain Reina Kazehara and her dragonkin first mate, Mio Tatsunami, bring an independent salvage company to the coast. Arrange passage, ask about work or get to know the people keeping the ship afloat. The cook, quartermaster, diver and deck crew have opinions of their own, and a promising recovery can become complicated long before anyone draws a weapon.

New beginnings let you start aboard the ship or at Starlark Abbey, where faith, hospitality and the demands of a working hospice share the same household.

### 🐚 A Coast Worth Exploring

- Take rural jobs, investigate tide pools, help with lighthouse problems and explore the surviving marks of an island ruin.
- Recover wreck cargo and decide what to do when finding something does not settle who owns it.
- Meet unfamiliar wildlife, mischievous small creatures and more dangerous inhabitants of the outer approaches.
- Study a sleeping blue serpent, help a peaceful giant turtle, or deliberately prepare for the kraken that moves through the eastern deepwater.

Nerathyl has separate sleeping and awake portraits, so disturbing its rest brings a visible change as well as a dangerous one.

The new work includes modest beginner tasks, more demanding field assignments and dangerous optional hunts. Quiet places remain available for a walk, a conversation or a little peace.

### 🏫 A First Day with Something Ahead

The academy openings now introduce classmates in the middle of their own preparations, arguments and ambitions. Join their conversations, find a working partner or get on with your own approach. Their lives do not stop at the classroom door.

Phoenix Wing prepares for a defensive practical; Gryffin Tail asks for a claim worth testing; Dragon Hoard puts field judgment under scrutiny; Hydra Head faces competing needs in a civic exercise. Required lessons and afternoon preparation carry expectations, with clear feedback and room to improve. Clubs remain optional, and the familiar town-work and guild leads remain suggestions to follow when you choose.

The guild opening also gains a glimpse of adventurers coming home from difficult work—with the equipment repairs and disagreements that follow them back.

### 🎨 Portraits and Finishing Touches

Fourteen new location illustrations bring the coast and countryside into view, from the Albatross and Starlark Abbey to the reefs, windmill and quieter paths beyond the city.

Refined default portrait guidance favors fine anime drawing, softer layered shading, detailed outfits and expressive lighting. Academy clothing now has explicit school colors and creature crests, with school-specific uniform choices available alongside the general options.

New equipment, useful tools and unusual coastal finds accompany the expansion. Physical Foundations has its missing defensive training restored, and Past Experience includes a small Spellcraft-focused option. Existing map placements and artwork are retained as the expanded territory receives finished names and descriptions.
