# Force merge and level caps

Current full export: **GlowhavenAlpha3 - Six Attributes.json**, also saved as **WorldAlpha - World.json**.

- Removed Endurance as an independent attribute. Force now includes physical power, resilience, sustained exertion, and braced fighting.
- The six attributes are **Force, Finesse, Logic, Passion, Empathy, Intuition**.
- **Guarded Combat now uses Force**. Its existing training and techniques remain intact.
- Removed only the two obsolete Endurance aptitude choices and their category entries. Existing Force descriptions and active AI/lore references are updated. Other abilities, traits and creation design were not expanded or redesigned.
- Raised the trainable skill cap from **25 to 100**.
- Raised the separate shared check-contribution ceiling from **25 to 100**, allowing skill levels above 25 to contribute to checks. This ceiling also bounds individual attribute, ability, random and contextual contributions; it is not solely a skill-level setting.
- Kept the character level cap at **100**. XP rates, reward cadence, HP/MP, ability bonuses, cooldowns, and the existing attribute ceiling are unchanged.

The user-approved school mappings are unchanged:

| Logic | Passion | Empathy | Intuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane, Metal, Gravity, Enchantment | Fire, Lightning, Earth, Wind, Water | Healing, Nature, Light, Summoning | Divination, Void, Illusion, Ice, Sound |

All NPCs, portraits, roster order, locations, prison layout, quests, items and modes are preserved. Ordinary uses of the word endurance in character descriptions remain ordinary prose; they are not references to a removed stat.

Local JSON, reference, size-limit and preservation checks passed. No live Studio validation or gameplay test was performed. Earlier seven-attribute exports and reports remain historical snapshots. Broader abilities, traits and character creation remain for the separate design pass requested by the user.
