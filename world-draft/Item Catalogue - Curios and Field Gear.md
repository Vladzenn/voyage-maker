## Teaching and use

Scroll spells are learned by actual successful study, consuming one scroll and granting its learned formula and ability. Failed study and already known formulas leave the scroll intact. Ordinary scroll spells have no fixed school-rank requirement; difficult study can compensate for low proficiency. Space retains the Gravity 25 foundation. Casts still use the discipline, the printed Mana cost, scope and cooldown. There is no Time scroll.

The first twelve new medicines have exact typed self-use effects. Their labels state restoration and any Health cost. Other consumables use their real described physical or magical utility through the normal action system.

Availability is author guidance, not a guaranteed drop table or a free purchase. The current full-world delivery is required for teaching traits, spells, triggers, shop context and beginner balancing to work together.
