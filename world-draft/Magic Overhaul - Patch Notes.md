# 🦦 Minor Patch 5

### ✨ Magic, Reactions & Resonance

Magic has a few new ways to play off itself!

- Added **24 elemental reactions**, with combinations that can deal extra damage, disrupt aim, weaken attacks or briefly hinder movement and defense. Enemies can use them too.
- Every participating element has several partners, with distinctive effects and narration. Physical attacks and Soul damage keep their own identities.
- Added **Resonance**: build charge through elemental reactions and unleash a stronger damaging technique when it fills. Misses, healing and utility actions preserve a ready surge.
- Expanded damage-type presentation, added a distinct **Soul** damage type and standardized **Poison** naming.
- Unified teaching-scroll learning, with successful study consuming one scroll and unlocking its formula. Failed study and already-known formulas retain their copies.
- Refreshed action-result names, from **Spectacular Blunder** to **Storybook Triumph**, while keeping the existing success thresholds.
