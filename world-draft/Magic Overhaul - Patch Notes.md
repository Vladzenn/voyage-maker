# 🦦 Minor Patch 5

### ✨ Magic, Reactions & Resonance

Magic has a few new ways to play off itself!

### 🌈 Set It Up, Follow Through

Added **24 elemental reactions** across sixteen magical damage types. Land an elemental hit to leave a brief imprint, then strike the **same target with a compatible element during that turn or the next** to trigger a reaction. Either order works, and an ally can supply the follow-up!

Different combinations offer different advantages:

- **Fire + Ice → Thermal Shock:** a sudden clash of temperatures cracks frost into luminous vapor, adding damage to the follow-up hit.
- **Water + Ice → Rimebind:** clinging frost briefly hinders movement-dependent blocking and evasion, creating an opening without an extra damage bonus.
- **Lightning + Arcane → Sparkstitch:** crackling interference briefly weakens the target's next damaging spell.

Some reactions focus on damage. Others combine a smaller damage boost with disruption, or trade the bonus entirely for weakened attacks, spoiled aim or a vulnerable defense. Each participating element has **three compatible partners**, so there are combinations to discover beyond the familiar ones.

- Each target holds one elemental imprint. Repeating that element refreshes it; a different element replaces it or triggers a compatible reaction.
- A reaction consumes the imprint, with at most one reaction per target each turn. Combining two elements inside one attack does not let it react with itself.
- Debuffs are brief: they affect the next relevant action or expire after the following turn. A newer reaction debuff replaces the previous one.
- Hits must actually land. Misses, complete immunity, decorative magic and rain alone do not set up reactions. Physical damage and Soul do not participate.

**Enemies can use these combinations too.** Watch what has just hit you, and consider whether to press your attack, change tactics or deny the follow-up.

### 💫 Build Your Resonance

Finishing a genuine combat reaction earns **one Resonance**, up to **20**. The character landing the follow-up earns the charge, allowing party members to take turns setting up and finishing combinations.

At full charge, your next landed damaging spell or trained combat technique releases a surge for a **50% damage boost**. The reaction that fills the meter sets up a later attack; it does not boost itself. Misses, healing and utility actions preserve a ready surge, and earned charge carries between battles.

### 📜 More Magical Polish

- Expanded damage-type presentation, added a distinct **Soul** damage type and standardized **Poison** naming.
- Unified teaching-scroll learning, with successful study consuming one scroll and unlocking its formula. Failed study and already-known formulas retain their copies.
- Refreshed action-result names, from **Spectacular Blunder** to **Storybook Triumph**, while keeping the existing success thresholds.
