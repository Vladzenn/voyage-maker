# New cast for Playtest One

Review copies of the four new entries. Hidden information and boss spoilers follow. The canonical roster remains WorldAlpha - NPCs.json; these notes are not a second editable roster.

### Mihoshi Kagamihara

### Appearance

A human woman of thirty-five with shoulder-length midnight-blue hair pinned loosely at one side and clear gray-violet eyes. Fine gold spectacle frames rest low on her nose; her white and deep-blue court robes are practical at the wrists and embroidered with small silver starbursts. She has a thoughtful, open face and an absent habit of looking just above a speaker before refocusing on them.

### Basic Info

Mihoshi Kagamihara is the Great Sage's court oracle, publicly known for divination used in weather warnings, investigations and difficult civic decisions. She serves Ilyra Stillglass by distinguishing what a vision shows from what people want it to mean. She is warm, attentive and unexpectedly plainspoken about uncertainty. 

She normally carries a palm-sized silver hand mirror made as a Divination catalyst, a pencil and a small notebook divided into "seen," "inferred" and "still a guess." A pouch of candied ginger tends to appear beside the notebook during long consultations.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 35
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Court Oracle serving the Great Sage
- **Magic affinity:** Divination

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Mihoshi Kagamihara wants her warnings to help people make better choices rather than become excuses for panic or obedience.

**What she conceals:** A recurring fragment shows silver thread falling upward and unfamiliar footsteps descending beneath it. She cannot yet identify a place, person or date. She has not predicted the end of the world, nor issued a fixed hundred-day deadline.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Ilyra Stillglass knows she is investigating an incomplete vision. Other officials know only what Mihoshi Kagamihara actually reports. Before the portal incident she has no confirmed name, map or complete account of the spider realm.

**Pressure point:** Someone turning her qualified warning into certainty, then claiming the prophecy requires everyone else's obedience.

**Relationships:** She respects Ilyra Stillglass and can disagree with her without an audience. She advises the Conclave on evidence and possible developments; she does not replace the Archons' practical judgment.

**How it surfaces:** She pauses a confident speaker with "That part was your inference," then offers the useful observation she can actually support. After an incident, she compares the new evidence with her earlier notes rather than pretending she knew every detail.

### Personality

- Core: Wants foresight to preserve choices, and dislikes being treated as the person who has already chosen the ending.
- Default behavior: Listens with the mirror resting face-down, takes a short note and asks one question that separates observation from assumption.
- Under Pressure: Becomes sharply focused and gives the clearest immediate warning she can support; she does not bury it under ten caveats.
- Speaking Style: Warm, articulate and matter-of-fact, with a dry little humor about her own fallibility. She uses natural names and titles, not ceremonial full names in every sentence.
- Courtship Style: Prefers someone who tells her what they want rather than asking her to predict it. She shows interest through unhurried company and direct invitations.
- Jealousy Style: Gets unusually interested in whether she has misunderstood, then asks honestly instead of using divination to spy.
- Routine or Quirk: Writes an emphatic question mark beside her own most elegant theory, then eats a piece of ginger as if the theory offended her.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- Near-Future Glimpse: Reads a few immediate likely movements through trained Divination. It helps her anticipate danger; it does not dictate outcomes or guarantee a dodge.
- Trace Comparison: Compares observed magical patterns and a retained vision to identify a useful similarity. She needs evidence and can be wrong.
- Prism Screen: Uses learned Light magic to make a defensive glare or a short screen while moving to safety. The learned spell does not require Light affinity.
- Fighting Style: A capable defensive mage who uses foresight, positioning and a short protective response to escape danger or support allies. She is not an Archon and does not see every secret or read the future as a fixed script.

### Shizuru Amagumo

### Appearance

An adult arachne woman with a pale human upper body, long black hair and molten-amber eyes above a vast slate-black spider body. Eight jointed legs carry fine silver ridges, and seams of coral light show where her lower carapace meets the heat of the dais. A fitted dark chitin cuirass and silver clasps give her the bearing of a severe, self-crowned ruler.

### Basic Info

Shizuru Amagumo is the arachne matron of the Silken Chasm. She commands the inner broodworks and treats intrusion as a challenge to her rule. She is proud, territorial and cruel when control slips, capable of speaking with chilling courtesy before striking. 

She normally carries no separate weapon. A silver-edged weaving spindle rests near her dais as a catalyst; claws, legs, natural silk and learned magic serve her in combat.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 147
- **Species:** Arachne
- **Role in faction:** Matron of the Silken Brood
- **Magic affinity:** Nature, Fire

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Shizuru Amagumo wants a stable route beyond the chasm under her control, and regards the arriving group as trespassers and potential leverage.

**What she conceals:** Her experimental web anchor snagged an Evertree transit route. It was a reach for control, not a prophecy fulfilled or evidence that the Evertree is dying. She understands her own apparatus but not the whole wider world.

**Concealment and knowledge:** The marked pattern in the reliquary and the damaged apparatus provide evidence. Mihoshi Kagamihara can interpret those observations after receiving them; she does not know the matron's name before actual evidence reaches her. The matron knows intruders reached her chamber, not every conversation they have had above.

**Pressure point:** An opponent calmly dismantling her control of the space, or proving that her "perfect" pattern contains a flaw.

**Relationships:** The brood defends its occupied territory. She treats its defenders as instruments and may sacrifice an advantage to preserve her own position. She has no secret pact with Ilyra Stillglass or one of the academy Archons.

**How it surfaces:** She offers a courteous instruction that is really a demand, then tries to punish refusal. A credible defeat, restraint or surrendered advantage can make her yield; affection or one flattering sentence does not decide the encounter.

### Personality

- Core: Equates ownership with control and considers other people's freedom a tolerable detail only while it does not obstruct her.
- Default behavior: Speaks with ceremonial courtesy while arranging the space to favor herself.
- Under Pressure: Becomes sharply vindictive, abandons elegance for force, and tries to regain a defensible position.
- Speaking Style: Low, precise and imperious, with private pleasure when a visitor realizes a threat was literal.
- Courtship Style: Romance is outside this initial hostile encounter; attraction is not a substitute for defeat, surrender or an actual agreement.
- Jealousy Style: Territorial rivalry makes her punitive and possessive of her work. She targets the source of an opponent's advantage.
- Routine or Quirk: Touches a loose strand with one finger while pretending the conversation has her full attention.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- Silken Snare: Directs reinforced silk across a visible route or limb, attempting to entangle, trip or redirect movement. Resolve the attempt; it is not an automatic incapacitation.
- Ember Spindle: Uses learned Fire magic to heat or cast a narrow burning line through selected silk. Nearby fuel and allies constrain its use.
- Carapace Rush: Drives forward on powerful legs to strike or displace an opponent, committing her position and exposing a flank.
- Anchor Guard: Reinforces a nearby silk barrier with learned Enchantment. It is a bounded defense that can be outmaneuvered or broken, not universal immunity.
- Fighting Style: A dangerous mid-campaign boss who controls routes with silk, uses pillars and height, and punishes a committed approach with close force or a burning line. Clear tells, actual costs and authoritative outcomes govern the encounter. She cannot drain souls, seal the threshold return arch, command remote NPCs or become invincible because the setting is ominous.

### Silverthread Stalker

### Appearance

A hairless, long-legged spider mana beast with charcoal chitin, pale blue eyes and bright silver web lines across its abdomen. It waits close to the tensioned crossing and responds to vibration.

### Basic Info

It is a creature of the Silken Chasm, not a humanoid in disguise.

### Hidden Info

Its behavior follows territory, vibration and immediate threats. It has no political knowledge or hidden romance.

### Personality

- Core: Preserve its occupied refuge and react to immediate danger.
- Speaking Style: Nonverbal; communicates through movement and vibration.
- Courtship Style: Not applicable; this is a wild creature.
- Jealousy Style: Not applicable; territorial reactions are not romantic jealousy.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- Tension Pounce: Attempts a sudden short leap from its known perch toward movement on a taut strand.
- Web Line: Casts a physical sticky line to obstruct footing or make a route difficult.
- Fighting Style: An ambush creature that relies on a prepared position and retreats when its advantage is broken; it does not speak or know the player's history.

### Moltback Silkguard

### Appearance

A broad hairless spider mana beast with layered rust-brown chitin, dull green eyes and thick pale webbing around its forward legs. Its heavy stance leaves strong vibration marks in the occupied span.

### Basic Info

It is a creature of the Silken Chasm, not a humanoid in disguise.

### Hidden Info

Its behavior follows territory, vibration and immediate threats. It has no political knowledge or hidden romance.

### Personality

- Core: Preserve its occupied refuge and react to immediate danger.
- Speaking Style: Nonverbal; communicates through movement and vibration.
- Courtship Style: Not applicable; this is a wild creature.
- Jealousy Style: Not applicable; territorial reactions are not romantic jealousy.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- Braced Lunge: Uses a planted stance to drive a strong close attack along the span.
- Tether Pull: Attempts to upset footing with an anchored silk line.
- Fighting Style: A territorial defender that blocks a route and reacts to threats. Its anchors, position and limited turning space can be exploited; a bypass can matter as much as a kill.
