## Professions

Optional, one prior profession. No current job, licence, workshop or free work kit is implied.

### Craft and Workshop

- **Magitech Technician:** Basic mechanism inspection and tools experience, no owned workshop. Starts with +2 Magitech; +1 investigation.
- **Blacksmith Apprentice:** Prior experience with forge work, fitting iron and recognizing a poor weld. Begin with +2 Magitech and +1 athletics.
- **Armorer:** Prior experience with fitting protective layers and maintaining their fastenings. Begin with +2 Magitech and +1 investigation.
- **Carpenter:** Prior experience with measuring, joining timber and repairing ordinary structures. Begin with +2 Magitech and +1 investigation.
- **Mason:** Prior experience with stonework, mortar and checking the load on a damaged wall. Begin with +2 Magitech and +1 athletics.
- **Tailor:** Prior experience with cutting cloth, fitting garments and judging material quality. Begin with +2 Trade and +1 Perception.
- **Jeweler:** Prior experience with fine settings, stones and patient work under magnification. Begin with +2 Magitech and +1 Trade.
- **Glassblower:** Prior experience with controlled furnace work and shaping fragile glass. Begin with +2 Magitech and +1 Perception.
- **Bookbinder:** Prior experience with binding, repairing and conserving physical books. Begin with +2 investigation and +1 Magitech.

### Study, Care and Records

- **Research Assistant:** Practical research habits and evidence collection, not a degree or famous patron. Starts with +2 investigation; +1 Perception.
- **Medical Assistant:** Basic nonmagical first aid and care without professional credentials being assumed. Starts with +2 Medicine; +1 Insight.
- **Alchemist Apprentice:** Apprentice-level preparation and lab discipline, no free recipes or materials. Starts with +2 Alchemy; +1 investigation.
- **Perfumery Apprentice:** Experience blending scents and identifying common ingredients. Starts with +2 Perfumery; +1 Trade.
- **Scribe:** Prior experience with accurate copying, correspondence and readable records. Begin with +2 investigation and +1 Etiquette.
- **Archivist:** Prior experience with cataloguing, provenance and finding a misplaced reference. Begin with +2 investigation and +1 Perception.
- **Interpreter:** Prior experience with careful communication across languages actually learned. Begin with +2 persuasion and +1 Insight.
- **Tutor:** Prior experience with explaining a familiar subject and noticing misunderstandings. Begin with +2 persuasion and +1 investigation.
- **Herbalist:** Prior experience with identifying useful plants and preparing ordinary ingredients. Begin with +2 Medicine and +1 survival.

### Trade, Hospitality and Performance

- **Merchant Apprentice:** Basic experience managing prices, sales or customer expectations. Starts with +2 Trade; +1 Insight.
- **Cook:** Experience preparing meals and handling practical kitchen work. Starts with +2 Cooking; +1 Trade.
- **Performer:** Audition, stage or street performance practice, not fame. Starts with +2 performance; +1 Etiquette.
- **Accountant:** Prior experience with balancing ledgers and identifying a mismatched payment. Begin with +2 Trade and +1 investigation.
- **Appraiser:** Prior experience with examining workmanship and comparing plausible market values. Begin with +2 Trade and +1 Perception.
- **Innkeeper:** Prior experience with guest arrangements, supplies and the ordinary problems of lodging. Begin with +2 Trade and +1 persuasion.
- **Tavern Worker:** Prior experience with serving meals, remembering orders and handling a busy room. Begin with +2 Cooking and +1 Insight.
- **Baker:** Prior experience with dough, ovens and preparing dependable batches of food. Begin with +2 Cooking and +1 Trade.
- **Tea Blender:** Prior experience with judging leaf quality and preparing distinct fragrant blends. Begin with +2 Cooking and +1 Perfumery.
- **Stage Magician:** Prior experience with sleight of hand, misdirection and rehearsed stagecraft. Begin with +2 performance and +1 Deception.
- **Dancer:** Prior experience with rehearsal, balance and reading a partner or musical phrase. Begin with +2 performance and +1 acrobatics.
- **Musician:** Prior experience with practice, ensemble timing and performing a learned instrument. Begin with +2 performance and +1 Perception.

### Fieldwork and Public Service

- **Field Scout:** Tracking weather and routes, recognizing ordinary terrain hazards. Starts with +2 survival; +1 Perception.
- **Animal Caretaker:** Practical animal care without automatically owning or commanding a companion. Starts with +2 Animal Handling; +1 survival.
- **Courier:** Regular errands and delivery stamina without an automatic travel permission. Starts with +2 athletics; +1 survival.
- **Community Coordinator:** Organizing arrangements and managing ordinary official or social procedures. Starts with +2 Etiquette; +1 persuasion.
- **Farmer:** Prior experience with seasonal work, soil conditions and keeping tools serviceable. Begin with +2 survival and +1 athletics.
- **Gardener:** Prior experience with propagation, pruning and caring for cultivated plants. Begin with +2 survival and +1 Perception.
- **Fisher:** Prior experience with reading water and weather, handling lines and landing a catch. Begin with +2 survival and +1 Perception.
- **Shepherd:** Prior experience with watching a flock, recognizing distress and managing grazing. Begin with +2 Animal Handling and +1 survival.
- **Stablehand:** Prior experience with feeding, grooming and handling ordinary mounts. Begin with +2 Animal Handling and +1 athletics.
- **Monster Hunter Apprentice:** Prior experience with reading tracks and preparing a sensible hunt. Begin with +2 survival and +1 Tactics.
- **Miner:** Prior experience with working a seam, checking supports and recognizing unsafe stone. Begin with +2 athletics and +1 Perception.
- **Prospector:** Prior experience with surveying exposed minerals and judging a promising sample. Begin with +2 investigation and +1 survival.
- **Caravan Hand:** Prior experience with loading, securing cargo and supporting an overland journey. Begin with +2 athletics and +1 Trade.
- **Sailor:** Prior experience with lines, deck work and weather judgment aboard ordinary vessels. Begin with +2 athletics and +1 survival.
- **Cartographer:** Prior experience with survey notes, bearings and mapping observed terrain. Begin with +2 investigation and +1 Perception.
- **Lamplighter:** Prior experience with maintaining street lamps and following a reliable service route. Begin with +2 Magitech and +1 Perception.
- **Watch Recruit:** Prior experience with watch routines, reporting and protecting a position. Begin with +2 Guarded Combat and +1 Perception.
- **Private Investigator:** Prior experience with interviews, physical clues and testing an explanation. Begin with +2 investigation and +1 Insight.
