## ✨ Minor Patch — Character Creation & Polish

More ways to make your character feel like yours!

### 🌟 Gifts, Backgrounds & Training

- Added an optional **Special Talent** selection, combining unusual gifts, blessings and exceptional aptitudes. Stronger gifts come with meaningful trade-offs.
- Races and personality choices now have more noticeable starting bonuses.
- Expanded professions, from crafts and hospitality to fieldwork, performance and public service.
- Clarified that affinities are optional, with a physical-training alternative among starting studies.

### 🎨 Style, Gear & General Polish

- More outfits, layered accessories and portrait backgrounds.
- Added an explicit bald option. Yes, really.
- More weapon families, casting implements and starting equipment choices.
- Updated location artwork and made minor balance adjustments.
- Adjusted music selection and guild badge handling to reduce unwanted interruptions and duplicate rewards.
