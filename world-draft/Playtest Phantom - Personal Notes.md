# Personal Phantom Patch — Playtest Adjustments

Private changelog; this does not count as a numbered public minor patch.

- Added **18 Past Experiences**, covering workshop work, trade, sailing, river work, care, community service, research, archives and performance.
- Added **12 First Impressions**, including **Unbothered Bearing**, Sleepy Composure, Relaxed Charm and Playfully Cheeky. These remain optional impressions rather than forced personality or guaranteed NPC reactions.
- Added **Otherworld Rebirth** to Origin & Upbringing. It grants +1 Insight and the **Summoned one** marker, allowing compatible openings to enter the existing Rebirth storyline without replacing their starting scene.
- Made the Brookmeadow Rebirth description and background instructions explicitly describe an **isekai awakening** with a remembered previous life, rather than being born and raised in Glowhaven.
- Reduced the initial Rebirth delegation wait from **200 to 100 turns**. The existing calm-town and scene-suitability checks remain; later pursuit timing is unchanged.
- Added **Quickened Chorus**, an optional Special Talent that lowers maximum Resonance and its charging threshold from **20 to 16**. Points still require real reactions; the surge's strength is unchanged.
- Moved **Resonance directly below Stamina**, before Infamy and Renown.
- Lowered the levels of **285 eligible background civilian NPCs**, reducing their native HP and damage. Haruho and Mihana are now **level 3**, about **102 HP at neutral difficulty**. Original health multipliers are retained. Trained fighters, faculty, protected major characters and special creatures keep their existing levels. New ordinary worker generation follows the lower civilian bands.
- Added a **Fenwick** name replacement filter for future generated names.
- Raised the first level-up requirement from **400 to 550 XP**, preserving the +60 increase per subsequent level. Ability-point grants remain **one per level**; the reported extra point was a false alarm.
- Applied the full milestone-title ladder through level 150, from **Trailfoot** to **A Story Without End**.
- Renamed relationship stages while preserving their score ranges:

| Enemy | No Friend of Mine |
| Hostile | At Daggers Drawn |

| Cordial | Pleasant Enough |

| Close | Rather Dear |

| Deeply Bonded | A Place in My Heart |

Local schema and rule fixtures verify the authored changes. They do not prove live AI recognition, dialogue quality or UI behavior. Studio draft upload awaits the specific payload approval described in the implementation notes; nothing has been published.
