# Quest Catalogue — Alpha20

112 quests: 42 retained and 70 additions. Use the complete world import for the matching quest, trigger, AI and local-hook changes. All original quest keys retain their order, and additions are appended. A student follows six major milestones; alternative academy versions are not extra compulsory assignments.

## Academic sequence

Magic Theory and original design → familiar examination → inter-academy festival → an actual authorized expedition → one practical thesis capstone → closing ceremony. Enrollment and ordinary school life remain. The level-20 field test and optional Silken investigation have separate conditions.

| Milestone | Definitions |
|---|---|

| Familiar examination | Four academy versions, joint practical at Dragon Hoard |
| Festival | Four Academies, One Festival; public participation also possible |
| Expedition | Beyond Glowhaven: An Authorized Expedition; retained and expanded |
| Thesis | Four alternative versions of One Question Worth Answering; once per playthrough across schools |
| Ceremony | What We Bring to the Next Morning |

## Ongoing school life

Ordinary chosen classes, practice and substantial social/extracurricular episodes continue between milestones and after the ceremony. Major invitations wait for completed earlier work, a minimum turn gap and meaningful intervening activity. A short remark or inventory check does not count as a school day. Explicitly asking to move ahead can advance preparation toward an eligible invitation without passing unfinished work or inventing a timeskip.

Initial calibration: after theory, 12 turns plus two completed ordinary episodes; after familiar assessment, 20 plus three; after festival, 18 plus two; after recognized expedition, 24 plus three; after thesis, 10 plus one. These are pacing intervals, not a calendar or fixed duration of a lesson. The activity counter accepts one fresh substantive completed episode at most every four turns. Live pacing needs playtesting.

The four recurring guild patterns require fresh terms, a new actual instance and a new board offer after at least 20 turns from settlement. Previous work cannot pay again. Other personal and chapter quests remain finite.
