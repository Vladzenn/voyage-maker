## Phoenix Wing: A Spell of Your Own

Haruki Saitō opens the first Magic Theory lesson of your current course with a challenge: design one small spell you can actually explain and repeat.

Contact: Haruki Saitō at Phoenix Wing Academy. Field location: Phoenix Wing Academy.

Haruki Saitō sets a small moving target on the desk and asks what would make a useful spell rather than an impressive noise. Hinae Komori worries about doing everything properly; Yūma Kurobane supplies a magnificently false account of his sealed power; Takeru Akabane wants to test his design immediately. Yūma Kurobane's supposed ancient power is delusion, never a secret revelation. Haruki Saitō gives specific ordinary feedback without becoming a concealed prodigy. Use Yūma Kurobane, Hinae Komori and Takeru Akabane as a small foreground cast where actually present. Let the player choose purpose and an already trained discipline. A returning inventor develops a fresh function or substantial refinement; do not erase an existing repertoire. Spend real time on one meaningful attempt, feedback and an adjusted test if needed. Spell invention requires an actual successful Spellcraft design and a reproducible demonstration; casting later uses its discipline. The normal learned-ability generator records the resulting personal technique when supported. A cooldown delaying that card is not a failed course, and a failed design is not an ability grant. No fixed Fire/Arcane spell is compulsory.

Objectives:

1. Choose a practical function and develop its spell blueprint.
2. Demonstrate a reproducible original design.

Native completion reward: 0 marks and 400 experience per player party member. No service credit is attached to this chapter.

## Phoenix Wing: The Familiar Examination

Contact: Noriaki Takatsuki at Dragon Hoard Academy. Field location: Dragon Hoard Academy.

This is a joint practical physically held at Dragon Hoard Academy, with Noriaki Takatsuki as the actual Summoning examiner. Arrange an actual visiting assessor if needed; no absent professor is already in the room. Preparatory instruction uses normal learning and supported cooperative work. Objectives:

2. Handle the summon and conclude the examination.

Native completion reward: 0 marks and 700 experience per player party member. No service credit is attached to this chapter.

## Phoenix Wing: One Question Worth Answering

Choose one practical magical question worth pursuing. Your capstone will be judged by the work and the result you can demonstrate.

Contact: Haruki Saitō at Phoenix Wing Academy. Field location: Phoenix Wing Academy.

A useful protective or combat application is welcome, but so is another well-motivated topic. Haruki Saitō discusses the principle; Ritsu Kurogane can assess movement or combat and Ranka Kagari actual alchemy. Their expertise has limits. This thesis is once per playthrough across all academies. The player chooses a narrow meaningful project. Agree its question, accessible existing field site or laboratory and what would count as an answer. Spend the adventure on a real experiment, expedition, trial, creature interaction or device rather than forms. Make resistance tangible: an unstable working, relevant creature, difficult terrain, competing interpretation or real test condition. A well-supported negative finding can answer the question. Failed attempts allow revision within this same project, not a fresh reward-bearing thesis. Conclude with one brief practical demonstration and a conversation about the actual result. No mandatory repeated manuscript, signatures, oral-defense rounds, new resource, automatic ability or graduation. The four-year curriculum remains canon without imposing a four-year timeskip.

Objectives:

1. Agree a practical question and a meaningful test.
2. Carry out the chosen investigation or experiment.
3. Demonstrate and discuss the result.

Native completion reward: 0 marks and 1600 experience per player party member. No service credit is attached to this chapter.
