# 🌅 Schools of Magic, Rebirth!

A different body. A quiet village. An empire that has begun asking questions.

This update opens a new beginning in Glowhaven and carries the adventure beyond its borders. There are ordinary lives to settle into, an extraordinary household to visit and an imperial capital with far more going on than its beautiful skyline suggests.

Along the way, the world gains a large new cast, new discoveries, more ways to build your character and **100 new teaching scrolls**.

### 🏡 A Different Beginning

Wake in an unfamiliar room in Brookmeadow, with your old memories intact and people nearby who would quite like to know your name.

The new Rebirth opening gives you room to establish a life before its larger story begins. Find work, look for lodging, make friends, travel to the city or pursue an education. What you reveal about your previous life remains your decision.

As time passes, a foreign inquiry can reach the people and records around you. Follow its trail, protect a confidence, investigate independently or decide how much trouble you are willing to invite into your new life.

### 👑 Welcome to Ardelis

Explore **Caelrath, capital of the Empire of Ardelis**: white towers above cascading water, busy bridges between mountain terraces, violet rooftops, expensive tastes and carefully guarded doors.

Markets, boutiques, gardens, baths, workshops, inns, theaters, waterfronts and public institutions give the capital a life beyond its court. Browse perfume and jewelry in Opaline Arcade, catch a performance at Velvet Lyre Hall, or find useful work among the city's households and trades.

The local Adventurer's Guild recognizes familiar credentials and offers work from modest beginner contracts to much less comfortable assignments. Caelrath's own magical institute brings apprentices, demonstrations and research projects into the city.

Travel requires an appropriate introduction or permission. Once you have access, the portal provides a practical route between the capital and Glowhaven.

### 🤝 More Than 120 New Characters

Meet residents, investigators, adventurers, artisans, performers and unusual household company across the update.

- An aging king has ambitions he does not intend to leave unfinished.
- The queen and three very different princesses have their own interests, loyalties and private troubles.
- Noble houses compete through wealth, patronage, influence and family obligations.
- Named paladin leaders disagree about what their oaths require.
- Guild regulars and working neighbors have friendships, habits and problems beyond the next job they might offer.

The new cast receives portraits, including the capital's supporting residents and the stranger figures encountered along the investigation.

### 🕯️ A Story That Crosses Borders

The shore inquiry grows into an investigation of missing records, intermediaries, receiving stations and a project the imperial court would prefer to keep private.

Follow checked evidence through Glowhaven and into Ardelis. Vladimir can offer an introduction, but an independent court investigation remains possible. Later discoveries bring a powerful new rival and difficult choices about the people caught in the operation.

If your mana trail is genuinely identified, unresolved imperial business can also bring occasional retrieval attempts. Defeating or escaping one team may buy time without ending the operation behind it. Preparation, allies, concealment and your eventual handling of the investigation can all matter.

### 🗝️ The Wizard of Shadowspire

Shadowspire now has a household worth getting to know—and a private realm that makes the tower seem almost ordinary.

Earn Vladimir's permission, explore the tower and follow the cellar gate into **Vladimir's Elsewhere**. Copper lanterns guide guests between eight distinctive destinations:

- Moving galleries and transparent-root gardens.
- An inverted courtyard and porcelain workshops.
- A clockwork little kingdom and a tilting ringball court.
- An orchard with unusual rain and a cabinet of windows holding different evening skies.

Meet the Brass Usher, investigate curious household problems and discover which of Vladimir's hobbies he takes far too seriously. His appearance has also been refreshed, complete with an enormous wizard hat and an ornate glass staff he would strongly prefer you did not touch.

### 📜 100 New Teaching Scrolls

Discover new formulas across every public magical discipline, from useful kitchen and workshop workings to defensive techniques, unusual attacks and scarce masterworks.

Study a particular scroll, work through its difficulties and test what you have learned. Successful research teaches the formula and consumes one copy. Difficult workings reward care and preparation, while failed experiments leave room to try again.

New Ardelis equipment, techniques and story items accompany the wider adventure.

### 🐾 More Ways to Look Like Yourself

**Beastfae** join character creation as a rare ancestry with fully animal-shaped faces and upright bodies. Choose from a broad range of furred, scaled and feathered forms.

Fae and Beastfolk also gain expanded optional appearance presets. Existing stature and mixed-ancestry choices remain available, with room for your own colors, combinations and individual features.

### ⚔️ Beneath the Beautiful Streets

An optional midgame descent leads beneath the palace, through receiving chambers, calibration galleries and cooling works toward an experimental vessel that was never meant to become a public concern.

Elsewhere, maintenance stairs lead into Caelrath's sewers: inspection walks, old sluices and abandoned branches with smaller, less ceremonial inhabitants. New creatures, encounter types and practical jobs give adventurers reasons to explore both the capital's public face and its less fashionable corners.

An independent criminal organization and a religious splinter circle offer different kinds of trouble. Take their work, question their accounts, refuse an offer or find another way through a dispute.

### 🌟 More Ways to Begin, More Places to Discover

Alongside Rebirth, new Ardelis beginnings let you approach the guild counter, settle among Southbank's neighbors, take a place at the institute or attend an appointment with a noble household.

New location artwork brings the capital and Vladimir's Elsewhere to life. Map placements have been updated, lore now reaches further into the empire's faith and politics, and Glowhaven's independence and shared history with Ardelis receive more detail.

Investigation handoffs and peaceful outcomes have also been improved, helping the story continue through the choices you actually make.

There is a new life waiting. What you make of it is yours. 🌅
