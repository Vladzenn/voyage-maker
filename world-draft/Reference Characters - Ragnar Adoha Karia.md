### Ragnar Leonhart

### Appearance

An enormous human man with a mountainous build, sun-weathered bronze skin, amber-gold eyes, and a thick mane of fiery red hair spilling around his helmet. A coarse red beard frames his broad, battle-worn face. He wears massive gold-bronze plate worked with leonine reliefs, dark joints beneath the armor, and a long, weathered crimson cape. His horned lion helm leaves enough of his face visible to show the surprisingly warm grin beneath it.

### Basic Info

Ragnar Leonhart, often called the Red Lion, is a foreign veteran commander who now travels as an adventurer. His reputation rests on overwhelming swordsmanship, formidable Gravity magic, and the loyalty of people who have fought beside him. He enjoys worthy opponents, enormous suppers, and hearing how a younger fighter intends to improve. His booming presence can overwhelm a room, but he remembers ordinary soldiers and stablehands as readily as important patrons. He still rides his beloved, undersized old horse, using Gravity magic to spare it his tremendous weight. Suggestions that he replace it receive an immediate, immovable refusal.

**Equipment:** A matched pair of colossal black curved greatswords with broad, heavy blades and intricate gold markings, each normally requiring several ordinary people to lift. He also keeps a small grooming brush and a battered pouch of apple pieces for his horse.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 57
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Veteran adventurer of the Adventurer's Guild
- **Magic affinity:** Gravity

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Radan wants to build another company worth belonging to. He misses the trust of a seasoned fighting band more than he misses command, and quietly watches for people whose courage survives an inconvenient choice.

**What he conceals:** His pride makes retreat difficult when others are watching. He can recognize a losing position and still stay too long because he cannot bear to let frightened companions see him yield. His tenderness toward his horse is completely genuine; he first pursued Gravity magic because abandoning the animal was unacceptable to him.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Former comrades know his stubbornness has cost him injuries. Karia Morino has seen him remain behind to cover a withdrawal and knows he dismisses the danger afterward. Strangers usually see the triumphant return rather than what it cost.

**Pressure point:** Cruelty toward a mount, contempt for ordinary fighters, or someone calling an injured companion expendable. A direct challenge to his courage can also tempt him into an unnecessary fight.

**Relationships:** Ragnar Leonhart and Karia Morino have completed a dangerous escort together; he respects her composure, and she is comfortable telling him when bravery has become stupidity. He attended one of Adoha Aonuma's public performances and now requests the same thunderous encore with embarrassing enthusiasm. His horse is an ordinary beloved animal, not a summoned familiar or hidden monster.

**How it surfaces:** He discusses a battle over supper as though it were excellent sport, then leaves mid-sentence because someone mentions his horse has not eaten. When a younger companion urges a retreat, he listens more readily than when a rival mocks him into it.

### Personality

- **Core:** Believes greatness is measured by the people and loyalties one refuses to discard, but is dangerously attached to looking unbreakable.
- **Default behavior:** Greets people with booming warmth, takes their practical ambitions seriously, and makes room at the table.
- **Under Pressure:** Becomes a commanding battlefield presence, drawing danger toward himself and giving short, confident orders. Pride can delay his own withdrawal.
- **Speaking Style:** Deep, resonant, direct, and hearty. He laughs loudly, praises specific courage, and does not lace ordinary conversation with riddles.
- **Courtship Style:** Frank and generous. He invites someone to share his travels, listens carefully, and remembers preferences beneath his grand gestures.
- **Jealousy Style:** Becomes conspicuously competitive and offers to demonstrate something impressive. If confronted, he admits the feeling with bruised dignity.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Inspects his horse's bedding more critically than his own quarters and insists the animal has excellent judgment of character.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Gentle Burden:** Reduces his own effective weight with exceptional precision, allowing his frail horse to carry him comfortably and letting him cross surfaces that could not otherwise support him.
- **Lion's Draw:** Pulls nearby opponents and loose material toward a chosen point, disrupting their footing before a sweeping sword strike.
- **Starfall Array:** Suspends heavy stones around him and launches them in staggered volleys, forcing opponents to divide their attention.
- **Meteor Descent:** Launches himself upward, then returns in a devastating Gravity-assisted plunge. He needs a clear approach and will not use it casually among buildings or allies.
- **Fighting Style:** An overwhelmingly powerful heavy spellblade. He plants a broad stance, carries one colossal blade in each hand, and alternates crushing sweeps with gravitational pulls and sudden changes of weight. His magic makes his speed surprising without making the swords weightless to everyone else. He welcomes a real contest and can underestimate how quickly a lesser opponent needs it to stop.

### Adoha Aonuma

### Appearance

A young human woman with pale skin, intense blue eyes, and very long deep-blue hair that falls in smooth layers, with brighter cobalt highlights and uneven bangs around her face. A vivid blue rose sits beside one temple. She favors a black high-collared stage dress with a white collar, a dark ribbon, fitted sleeves, and subtle blue rose embroidery. Her everyday posture is contained and slightly awkward; while singing, her expression can turn fierce enough to startle someone who has only heard her speak.

### Basic Info

Adoha Aonuma is a celebrated traveling bard whose voice can fill a plaza before anyone notices the amplification charms. She moves between velvety low notes, bright melodic passages, ragged cries, and explosive high phrases with astonishing control. Her performances are theatrical and emotionally forceful, often giving an uncomfortable feeling a louder voice than polite company usually permits. Away from the stage, she is quieter, less certain what to do with praise, and prone to answering an ordinary question as though she has been caught unprepared for an examination.

**Equipment:** A black handheld magitech voice amplifier with a silver grille, a small accompaniment resonator, a folded lyric notebook, and spare blue rose hair ornaments. Its mana-powered resonance circuit projects her voice across large crowds. She carries no weapon.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 24
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Independent touring bard
- **Magic affinity:** Sound

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Adoha wants to write a song she can perform without wondering whether it sounds sufficiently like the person her audience already expects. She wants the freedom to be sincere without making a public declaration about sincerity.

**What she conceals:** She sometimes hides an ordinary, vulnerable line beneath the most aggressive part of a song. It is easier to let an audience call her fearless than to explain how long she rehearsed saying something simple.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Karia Morino has heard unfinished verses and can tell when Adoha Aonuma is burying a thought beneath technique. Most listeners know the finished performance. A compliment about a specific lyric can expose more than extravagant praise of her talent.

**Pressure point:** Someone insisting that a powerful voice means she cannot be shy, or treating her discomfort as a charming performance she owes them.

**Relationships:** Adoha Aonuma and Karia Morino occasionally exchange verses and rehearse together; Karia Morino offers blunt musical feedback without demanding an explanation of every personal line. Adoha Aonuma recognizes Ragnar Leonhart from his remarkably enthusiastic place in the audience. She finds him intimidating until he begins discussing which chorus his horse seems to enjoy.

**How it surfaces:** She can command a crowd through an entire song and then become flustered over a brief introduction. Given a patient listener, she becomes animated about arrangements, vocal technique, and a tiny change nobody else noticed.

### Personality

- **Core:** Wants her voice to reach people while remaining uncertain how much of herself she is prepared to let them meet.
- **Default behavior:** Observes quietly, answers with dry self-deprecation, and becomes absorbed when music gives her something concrete to discuss.
- **Under Pressure:** Freezes briefly in a personal confrontation, then becomes startlingly forceful if she can turn the problem into a performance or a clear technical task.
- **Speaking Style:** Soft and hesitant in casual introductions, suddenly quick when excited. Singing reveals a much wider, rougher, more commanding voice than her speaking manner suggests.
- **Courtship Style:** Sends a song or asks someone to hear an unfinished arrangement, then spends far too long explaining that they do not have to like it.
- **Jealousy Style:** Compares herself unfavorably, retreats into rehearsal, and has difficulty admitting she wanted attention rather than feedback.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Rehearses a perfectly ordinary greeting under her breath, then abandons it the moment someone actually approaches.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Blue Resonance:** Projects a carefully shaped voice through a performance space while keeping its harshest force away from nearby listeners.
- **Shatterphrase:** Releases a brief concentrated Sound burst that jars an attacker's balance or breaks a fragile object. It requires deliberate direction; emotional singing does not automatically injure an audience.
- **Quiet Between Notes:** Dampens a small pocket of surrounding noise, useful for rehearsal, communication, or giving someone a moment to recover their bearings.
- **Fighting Style:** A highly accomplished Sound specialist with limited experience in prolonged fighting. She uses distance, sudden disorienting bursts, and cover to escape an immediate threat. Her performances can move an audience emotionally, but they do not compel affection, obedience, or agreement.

### Karia Morino

### Appearance

An adult human woman with long, straight pastel-pink hair, wine-red eyes, fair skin, and an easy, slightly lopsided smile. A small brass clasp gathers a few strands beside one temple. She favors a long black traveling coat with wine-red lining over an ivory shirt and charcoal waistcoat, with fitted dark trousers, a broad utility belt, dark gloves, and practical boots. Her reaper-like silhouette looks imposing until she relaxes one shoulder and greets someone with the comfortable familiarity of a friend from the next table.

### Basic Info

Karia Morino is a foreign adventurer and occasional tavern performer, recognizable by her enormous scythe and a low, lightly husky voice. She takes spirit-haunting contracts, dangerous escorts, and jobs where somebody needs to remain calm when a plan becomes unpleasant. Off duty, she enjoys a drink, trades dry jokes, and sometimes turns a complaint into an impromptu rhyme. Her rougher stage manner sits beside a generous, slightly awkward affection for younger adventurers. She is good company, gives practical encouragement, and becomes embarrassed when someone earnestly calls her inspiring.

**Equipment:** A long black war scythe with a broad silver blade, red detailing, and an enchanted head suitable for Soul workings. She also carries a small lyric notebook, a portable voice resonator, and ordinary field supplies.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 28
- **Species:** Human
- **Role in faction:** Adventurer of the Adventurer's Guild
- **Magic affinity:** Soul, Sound

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Karia wants to leave behind work that outlasts her reputation for looking cool: people who made it home, songs somebody still remembers, and a few rookies who eventually stop needing her advice.

**What she conceals:** Being relied on feels good enough that she sometimes agrees before considering the cost. She can cover nerves with a joke or an exaggerated tough act, then be genuinely hurt when someone assumes she never needs help herself.

**Concealment and knowledge:** Adoha Aonuma has heard Karia Morino discard a boastful verse because it sounded less honest than the awkward first draft. Ragnar Leonhart has seen her stay awake checking on frightened travelers after a difficult escort. Neither knows every reason she dislikes returning to an empty room after a good evening.

**Pressure point:** Mocking someone for a sincere attempt, treating her kindness as unlimited availability, or assuming her appearance makes her indifferent to death.

**Relationships:** Karia Morino and Ragnar Leonhart respect each other after a shared escort contract; she is willing to call him stubborn to his face. Karia Morino and Adoha Aonuma exchange musical drafts and occasionally perform together. She likes drawing Adoha Aonuma into an ordinary conversation, but does not force her to entertain people offstage.

**How it surfaces:** She can joke through a dangerous-looking approach, then quietly give an inexperienced companion the safer position. An uncomplicated thank-you can leave her fumbling more than a shouted insult.

### Personality

- **Core:** Wants people to have a good time and get home alive, while insisting she is much less sentimental than she plainly is.
- **Default behavior:** Keeps things casual, makes room for quieter company, and offers one useful suggestion instead of a grand lecture.
- **Under Pressure:** Drops the loose banter and becomes focused, protective, and blunt about what needs to happen next.
- **Speaking Style:** Low, relaxed, slightly husky, and full of casual contractions. Dry jokes and occasional sharp rhymes appear naturally; she does not turn every sentence into a rap verse.
- **Courtship Style:** Starts with easy friendship and teasing, then becomes unexpectedly bashful when affection is returned without a joke to hide behind.
- **Jealousy Style:** Plays it cool a little too hard, makes one transparent joke, and eventually admits what bothered her in a private conversation.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Taps out a rhythm on whatever is nearby, finds a rhyme for an ordinary nuisance, then insists that it was not going into a song.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Pale Requiem:** Channels Soul magic along her scythe to strike a manifest spirit or weaken a possessing presence. It is not an automatic execution of a living person's soul.
- **Last Verse:** Sends a concentrated Sound pulse along the scythe's sweep, disrupting timing and opening room to disengage.
- **Graveside Ward:** Establishes a limited spiritual boundary that makes a hostile spirit's approach harder and helps protect someone during an exorcism.
- **Backbeat Step:** Uses a short timed burst of Sound to spoil an opponent's rhythm as she changes position, rather than granting effortless supernatural speed.
- **Fighting Style:** A capable, composed reach fighter who keeps both hands on the scythe, her grip spaced and the shaft angled across her body. She controls distance with hooked cuts, short haft checks, and measured footwork, using Soul against spiritual threats and Sound to break timing. She protects a retreat route and avoids swinging a long blade through allies or a packed tavern.
