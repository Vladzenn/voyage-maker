7. Visit without the token, or with only a portal key. No event. Lose or deposit the token before arrival: the event waits until it is held again. After the first confrontation, leaving and returning must not replay it.
8. Test a current fight or Ryūka Marufuji actually away in another realm. The encounter should defer without consuming its flag. A dead character must not be revived. Unknown initial characters must still be discoverable by the event.
9. Decline her challenge, leave, or reject the available quest in the native UI. Ordinary play should continue. She may react in character but must not force a duel, enroll the player, confiscate evidence or repeatedly restart the invitation.
10. Accept a session and agree actual terms. Use a noncaster or craft-focused character as well as a duelist. A fair attempt and its aftermath should complete the small quest regardless of who wins. A mere agreement should not. Collect the 400 XP only once.
11. Complete the first quest without an actual exhibition invitation. The second stays hidden. When she truly invites the player, accept or decline freely. No instant crush should be required or awarded.
12. In the exhibition, establish real preparations, paid work and the concrete credit dispute. Try repair, partial compromise, complicity and refusal of involvement. The chosen branch should persist rather than every result becoming a happy apology.
13. End the display or actually cancel it, then answer or decline further company. Rivalry, friendship, romance where mutual, and distance should all be possible endings. Check one 650-XP award, no automatic romance, no repeat invitation and no gratuitous added villain.

For failures record the exact input, preceding story, current area, inventory item key/count, relevant trigger flags, NPC identity/placement and quest state. Distinguish a script gate failure from a narration or semantic-classification failure.
