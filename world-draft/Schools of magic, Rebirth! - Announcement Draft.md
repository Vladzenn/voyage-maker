# Schools of magic, Rebirth!

*Upcoming major update · announcement draft*

**You wake up in a body that isn’t yours.**

You remember another life. The people who found you unconscious on the shore have brought you to Brookmeadow. Someone has put a bowl of broth beside your bed, and a neighbor outside is practicing an apology he hopes you’ll never hear. What you tell them is up to you.

- **A new beginning in Brookmeadow.** Choose Rebirth at the top of Story Starts. Make friends, learn magic and decide how much of your old life to share.
- **The Wizard of Shadowspire.** Long after your first morning in Brookmeadow, tensions between Ardelis and Glowhaven reopen an old mystery. Somewhere in the woods, a wizard recognizes the magic that brought you here.
- **Explore Shadowspire.** Earn Vladimir’s permission to enter his woodland tower, then find the portal beneath it. Beyond the gate are shifting galleries, strange gardens and a sports court where the floor can tilt.
- **Around 100 new scrolls and a wider world.** Discover new workings for everyday life and dangerous adventures. Explore expanded lore and a working port just down the road from Brookmeadow.
