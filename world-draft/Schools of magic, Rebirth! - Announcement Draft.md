# Schools of magic, Rebirth!

*Upcoming major update · announcement draft*

**You wake up in a body that isn’t yours.**

You remember another life. The people who found you unconscious on the shore brought you to Brookmeadow. There’s a bowl of broth by the bed and sand beneath the chair. Someone asks what to call you. What you tell them is up to you.

- **A new beginning in Brookmeadow.** Choose Rebirth at the top of Story Starts. Find work, learn how magic fits into ordinary life and decide whom to trust with what you remember.
- **The Wizard of Shadowspire.** Long after your first morning in Brookmeadow, tensions between Ardelis and Glowhaven reopen an old mystery. Somewhere in the woods, a wizard recognizes the magic that brought you here.
- **Explore Shadowspire.** Earn Vladimir’s permission to enter his woodland tower, then find the portal beneath it. Beyond the gate lies a shifting realm built around an archmage’s whims.
- **More ways to look Fae, Beastfolk or Beastfae.** Browse dozens of new appearance options, from woodland Fae and feather-accented Beastfolk to Beastfae with full animal faces, fur, scales or plumage.
- **Around 100 new scrolls and a wider world.** Discover new workings for everyday life and dangerous adventures. Explore expanded lore and a working port just down the road from Brookmeadow.
