# 🌅 Schools of magic, Rebirth!

You wake up in a body that isn't yours.

The room is unfamiliar. Your old memories are still there. Someone has left broth beside the bed, and the people who found you on the shore would like to know what to call you.

### 🏡 A Life in Brookmeadow

A new reincarnation opening begins in the Glowhaven countryside, among people with work to finish, promises to keep and opinions of their own. Find your footing through village life, travel, study or the city guild. What you tell people about your past is yours to decide.

As that life develops, foreign visitors begin asking questions. An investigation reaches from ordinary shore records to something much harder to explain. Your choices can protect a confidence, expose a connection or bring you into danger. The Glowhaven chapters end with a lead pointing toward Ardelis and the next part of the story.

### 🗝️ The Wizard of Shadowspire

The tower beyond the wandering paths now has an owner worth meeting and a home worth exploring. Earn Vladimir's permission to visit, climb to his study or follow the private gate beneath the stairs.

Beyond it are moving galleries, translucent gardens, impossible courtyards, porcelain workshops and some astonishingly elaborate ways to waste an afternoon. Follow the copper lanterns, meet the household and discover which of the wizard's interests he takes far too seriously.

### 📜 100 New Teaching Scrolls

Discover new formulas across every public magical discipline, from useful kitchen and workshop workings to defensive techniques, unusual attacks and scarce masterworks.

Study a particular scroll, work through its difficulties and test what you have learned. Successful research teaches its formula and consumes one copy. Difficult workings reward care and preparation, while failed experiments leave room to try again.

### 🐾 More Ways to Look Like Yourself

Beastfae join character creation as a rare ancestry with fully animal-shaped faces and upright bodies. Choose from a broad range of furred, scaled and feathered forms.

Fae and Beastfolk also gain many more optional appearance presets, alongside the existing choices for stature, clothing and individual features. These forms leave room for your own colors, combinations and character.

### ✨ People, Discoveries and Polish

- Meet new residents, investigators, visitors and peculiar household company.
- Follow linked investigations with room for refusal, practical solutions and different approaches.
- Find new creatures, household activities and small jobs beyond the main story.
- Expanded lore and a more grounded narrative direction, with warmth and absurdity where they belong.
- Continued mechanical and validation improvements behind the scenes.
