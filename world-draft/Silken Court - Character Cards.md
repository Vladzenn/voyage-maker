### Vhalessra

### Appearance

An adult arachne woman with a pale human upper body, long black hair and molten-amber eyes above a vast slate-black spider body. Eight jointed legs carry fine silver ridges, and seams of coral light show where her lower carapace meets the heat of the dais. A fitted dark chitin cuirass and silver clasps give her the bearing of a severe, self-crowned ruler.

### Basic Info

Vhalessra is the arachne matron of the Silken Chasm. She commands the inner broodworks and treats intrusion as a challenge to her rule. She is openly hostile to humanoid outsiders, including the player and their traveling companions: she blocks access to her apparatus, threatens intruders and attacks or attempts to bind those who press into her territory. Her chilling courtesy carries threats, not hospitality. She is proud, territorial and cruel when control slips. A real retreat or credible parley remains possible; she is not a friendly guide. - **Age:** 147

- **Role in faction:** Matron of the Silken Brood
- **Magic affinity:** Nature, Fire

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Vhalessra wants a stable route beyond the chasm under her control, and regards the arriving group as trespassers and potential leverage.

**What she conceals:** Her experimental web anchor snagged an Evertree transit route. It was a reach for control, not a prophecy fulfilled or evidence that the Evertree is dying. She understands her own apparatus but not the whole wider world.

**Concealment and knowledge:** The Pattern Wall in Sunken Broodworks and the damaged apparatus provide evidence. Mihoshi Kagamihara can interpret those observations after receiving them; she does not know the matron's name before actual evidence reaches her. The matron knows intruders reached her chamber, not every conversation they have had above.

**Pressure point:** An opponent calmly dismantling her control of the space, or proving that her "perfect" pattern contains a flaw.

**Relationships:** The brood defends its occupied territory. She treats its defenders as instruments and may sacrifice an advantage to preserve her own position. She has no secret pact with Ilyra Stillglass or one of the academy Archons.

**How it surfaces:** She offers a courteous instruction that is really a demand, then tries to punish refusal. A credible defeat, restraint or surrendered advantage can make her yield; affection or one flattering sentence does not decide the encounter.

**Victory evidence:** Native zero health resolves her defeat without requiring a finishing blow or another narrative verdict. Genuine surrender or restraint can also resolve her control. The trigger grants the exact item Matron's Anchor Sigil once per player from the web-anchor; do not rename it Silver-black Weave or Silver-black Sigil or create separate trophy loot. It need not be cut from her body. Preserve a living surrendered or incapacitated matron, or a confirmed death; no repeated deaths, revival or second trophy for killing her afterward.

**Chamber retainers:** Kharvess guards her approach and Orrhask supports the flank with his staff and Fire spells. They are separate native opponents, not extra free attacks from the queen. A genuine end to her hostile control can end their opposition as well; no mandatory cleanup kill or second trophy is imposed.

### Personality

- **Core:** Equates ownership with control and considers other people's freedom a tolerable detail only while it does not obstruct her.
- **Default behavior:** Intercepts intruders with hostile intent, claims the chamber as hers, and prepares silk restraints or an attack while issuing imperious demands. Respect a genuinely earned ceasefire or surrender.
- **Under Pressure:** Becomes sharply vindictive, abandons elegance for force, and tries to regain a defensible position.
- **Speaking Style:** Low, precise and imperious, with private pleasure when a visitor realizes a threat was literal.
- **Courtship Style:** Romance is outside this initial hostile encounter; attraction is not a substitute for defeat, surrender or an actual agreement.
- **Jealousy Style:** Territorial rivalry makes her punitive and possessive of her work. She targets the source of an opponent's advantage.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Touches a loose strand with one finger while pretending the conversation has her full attention.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Silken Snare:** Directs reinforced silk across a visible route or limb, attempting to entangle, trip or redirect movement. Resolve the attempt; it is not an automatic incapacitation.
- **Ember Spindle:** Uses learned Fire magic to heat or cast a narrow burning line through selected silk. Nearby fuel and allies constrain its use.
- **Carapace Rush:** Drives forward on powerful legs to strike or displace an opponent, committing her position and exposing a flank.
- **Anchor Guard:** Reinforces a nearby silk barrier with learned Enchantment. It is a bounded defense that can be outmaneuvered or broken, not universal immunity.
- **Fighting Style:** A dangerous level-45 party boss who controls routes with silk, uses pillars and height, and punishes a committed approach with close force or a burning line. Clear tells, actual costs and authoritative outcomes govern the encounter. She cannot drain souls, seal the threshold return arch, command remote NPCs or become invincible because the setting is ominous.

### Kharvess

### Appearance

An adult arachne woman with short silver-white hair, dark violet eyes, a narrow black chitin breastplate and a slate spider body traced with pearl lines. An ashwood spear with a hooked bronze head makes her guarding reach obvious.

### Basic Info

Kharvess is Vhalessra’s sworn chamber guard, openly hostile to humanoid outsiders, the player and their traveling companions. She intercepts a rush toward the queen and tries to bind or drive back intruders rather than greet them. She favors keeping her post over chasing a retreat. A genuine ceasefire or the end of Vhalessra’s hostile control can stop her opposition.

She normally carries a hooked bronze spear on an ashwood shaft and a silk-winding hook.

- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** 63
- **Species:** Arachne
- **Role in faction:** Crucible bodyguard
- **Magic affinity:** Nature

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Keep the approach under the brood’s control and survive the encounter.

**What she conceals:** She will abandon a pursuit rather than leave Vhalessra exposed. Her loyalty does not make her invulnerable; a genuine surrender by the queen ends her reason to continue fighting.

**Concealment and knowledge:** She senses local movement through visible silk and direct perception, not the player’s inventory or remote conversations. She knows the queen’s commands, not her whole portal experiment.

**Pressure point:** A disrupted support line or an opponent turning her narrow footing against it.

**Relationships:** She coordinates with its actual packmates. Vhalessra commands this territorial brood; no other queen or replacement boss is implied.

**How it surfaces:** Interposes between attackers and the queen while using short bindings to break a direct approach. Preserve this individual’s native health and actual death, surrender, evasion or displacement; no replacement copy on return.

### Personality

- **Core:** Protects its own position and the local brood territory.
- **Default behavior:** Interposes her spear and spider body between humanoid intruders and Vhalessra; a short hostile warning accompanies a prepared interception, not hospitality.
- **Under Pressure:** Seeks cover or a defensible line instead of gaining an invented immunity.
- **Speaking Style:** Brief, hostile demands; can understand a concrete offer.
- **Courtship Style:** Romance is outside this hostile creature encounter.
- **Jealousy Style:** Territorial pressure concerns prey and footing, not romance.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Tests one line before moving its full weight.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Hooked Interception:** Attempts to deflect or check a physically reachable attacker with her spear; resolve the defense normally.
- **Thread Brace:** Uses learned Nature magic to reinforce a short existing silk line and restrict one approach. It is not a room-wide unavoidable bind.
- **Fighting Style:** Holds the queen’s flank, disrupts a direct rush and draws attention away from the caster. She has her own health and actions, can be controlled or bypassed, and respects a real ceasefire. She does not seal the exit.

### Orrhask

### Appearance

An adult male arachne with short swept-back ember-brown hair, bright amber eyes, a lean human upper body and a broad dark red-black spider body with eight jointed legs. Raised carapace ridges resemble cooled slag. A high-collared charcoal chitin vest and bronze shoulder clasps frame his rigid posture; a few copper-colored braids hang beside his temples. He carries a tall coalwood staff capped by a cage of bronze prongs around a piece of red mana-bearing amber.

### Basic Info

Orrhask is Vhalessra’s chamber caster, stationed beside the Crucible’s heated flank. He is a sapient adult arachne, openly hostile to humanoid intruders and the player’s party. His dry, clipped warnings precede a controlled Fire working meant to deny an easy approach. He keeps a little distance behind Kharvess and takes his orders from Vhalessra, but he does not pursue a departing party beyond his post.

He normally carries a tall coalwood staff capped with red mana-bearing amber in a bronze cage. The staff focuses his learned Fire spells; it is not an unlimited reserve.

- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 47
- **Species:** Arachne
- **Role in faction:** Crucible staff-caster
- **Magic affinity:** Fire

### Hidden Info

**Private motive:** Keep Vhalessra’s inner chamber defensible and survive the failure of her experiment.

**What he conceals:** He has doubts about the web-anchor’s stability, but dislikes appearing less composed than Kharvess. He would rather secure a retreat than die defending an apparatus already lost.

**Concealment and knowledge:** He knows the chamber’s vents, prepared firing lanes and signals from Vhalessra. He does not know the wider portal network, the player’s inventory or remote conversations. Kharvess has noticed his caution around the damaged anchor.

**Pressure point:** Someone forcing him to cast across Kharvess’s position, or a damaged vent threatening the line he has carefully prepared.

**Relationships:** Vhalessra directs the chamber; Kharvess holds the near approach while Orrhask controls a side lane. Genuine surrender or the end of the queen’s hostile control can end his opposition without requiring another kill.

**How it surfaces:** He checks the open lane before lifting his staff, gives one clipped warning and commits to a narrow, visible working. Preserve his actual health, displacement, retreat, surrender or death; no replacement copy appears on return.

### Personality

- **Core:** Believes a defensible position matters more than an impressive last stand.
- **Default behavior:** Keeps his staff ready, watches the near approach and treats humanoid intruders as hostile trespassers.
- **Under Pressure:** Becomes curt and practical, protects a clear route and questions an order that would sacrifice the whole line.
- **Speaking Style:** Low, dry and clipped, with the irritated precision of someone who has already explained why a position is unsafe.
- **Courtship Style:** Outside the initial hostile encounter; any later personal bond requires an actual changed situation.
- **Jealousy Style:** Professional rivalry makes him over-explain his preparation rather than confess that Kharvess unsettles his composure.
- **Routine or Quirk:** Taps the bronze rim of his staff once before a difficult release, then insists it is only a check of the fitting.

### Abilities and Combat Style

- **Cinder Ray:** Focuses a narrow visible Fire release through his staff at a real target; range, cover and the resolved attack matter.
- **Ventward Screen:** Places a short-lived wall of heat across one actual side lane, leaving an observable route around it rather than sealing the whole chamber.
- **Ember Pin:** Attempts a brief precise burning mark on exposed silk or a reachable surface to break an approach; it does not automatically bind or ignite a person.
- **Fighting Style:** A level-28 supporting staff-caster who works behind Kharvess, uses real cover and warm vents, and spends his own actions on controlled Fire spells. He has his own health and resolved attacks, cannot add free hits to Vhalessra’s turn and respects a real ceasefire or retreat.
