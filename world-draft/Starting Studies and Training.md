Space remains an advanced Logic discipline with Gravity Magic 25 as its foundation, so no Space Studies option is added to creation. Time remains configured but excluded from starter choices, developer grants and the public ability catalogue. Scholarly Practice supports theory and invention rather than granting an arbitrary combat school.

The Developer Playtest Kit now includes all 480 actual entries once. Existing trait selection counts, exclusions, attributes, skill bonuses and resource grants are unchanged. The later affinity-selection redesign and teacher/book skill-acquisition policy are not activated.

Replace Abilities first, then Traits, with their respective full plain-map section files. Replacing Abilities alone does not update starter grants. New grants apply when traits are applied; do not assume existing characters or running saves receive them retroactively.
