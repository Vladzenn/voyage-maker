# Write Your Own

**Your character. Your first scene.** Describe what is happening when the story begins, who is involved and the mood you want. A few sentences are enough: a first lesson, an awkward reunion, a market dispute, a quiet day at home, or a journey already under way.

Choose the matching starting location from the list so the opening and map agree. For a particular room within that place, name it in your prompt. The list offers public places in Glowhaven, the four academy entrances, Brookmeadow, Drakenspire's market and Asterveil's landing terrace. Opening abroad can reflect a local or visiting background; it does not issue future portal permissions.

Use existing characters when you want a familiar face. Their established identity, responsibilities and willingness still matter. Describe your intended situation; your actual character setup supplies starting possessions, skills and mechanical grants.

Examples: “I'm outside the Everlight Library with an overdue book and a perfectly reasonable excuse. Begin as I reach the desk.” “I'm in Brookmeadow, helping at the mill for the first time. Start with the problem nobody warned me about.”

In multiplayer, the host writes the shared opening.



# Phoenix Wing: Arrival Court

Admitted to Phoenix Wing, you arrive among red pennants, disputed duel scores and a welcome from Hinae Komori. Check in, ask questions, or follow your curiosity.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The adult player is an admitted student at Phoenix Wing Academy, with the configured admission packet. Foreground Hinae Komori, the first-year student helping direct arrivals; use her existing record. This selected opening places her in the Arrival Court, without recruiting her. Adapt the narration's player reference to the actual character, in third-person present. Preserve the selected appearance and equipment. Render the scene below with its dialogue and immediate problem, then wait. No completed check-in, chosen class, duel or player answer is implied.]

The scrape of wooden practice blades carries across Phoenix Wing's Arrival Court. Beyond a low wall, someone calls a score; a second voice disputes it with tremendous conviction and very little success. Red pennants stir above the entrance, where two noticeboards compete for space. One advertises supervised dueling. The other announces a cooking competition in lettering every bit as aggressive.

Hinae Komori stands between them with a notebook open against her admission papers. A breeze lifts one corner. She catches it with her elbow, loses her place, and gives the newcomer a warm, slightly hurried smile.

"Are you here for check-in too? Main Hall first. I asked twice. The second time was because somebody said 'registration by the arena,' and I thought they meant we had to fight for it."

She glances toward the practice yard, where the disputed score has apparently become a third person's problem.

"We don't. I checked that as well."

The academy's admission packet gives the same route: check-in in the Main Hall, then orientation and a choice of introductory activities. A queue is beginning to form inside the open doors. To the side, a chalk arrow points toward the training yard, and the smell of something toasted drifts from farther along the building.

Hinae taps a question mark in her notebook.

"Do you already know what you want to study? I keep adding things. Apparently 'everything' isn't a timetable."

# Gryffin Tail: Arrival Steps

Begin enrollment at Gryffin Tail with a packet, an annotated campus plan and Fumito Sakurai, who knows several more interesting routes than the official one.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The adult player is admitted to Gryffin Tail, with the configured packet. Fumito Sakurai is a second-year student assisting arrivals on the Arrival Steps for this opening; he is not a party member. Use his earnest voice and existing appearance. His enthusiasm is genuine, not a secret test. Stop before the player gives a name, chooses a course or completes enrollment.]

Cyan banners hang almost motionless over Gryffin Tail's broad steps. At their foot, the admission notices are arranged in immaculate columns. Someone has corrected a misplaced accent in red ink. Someone else has corrected the correction in smaller blue ink.

Fumito Sakurai is studying both with professional concern when he notices the new arrival. His round glasses slip down his nose; he pushes them back and immediately straightens the papers under his arm.

"Welcome. The registration desk is through the Lecture Wing—sorry, the sign says 'initial academic processing,' but it means registration."

He points out the route on a campus plan, then hesitates over a handwritten addition to it.

"This shortcut works, but it passes the Research Rooms, and if there's a demonstration going on, you can lose an hour. Two, if they let you ask questions."

The last part sounds like a recommendation. A bell chimes inside. Fumito looks down at a page so densely annotated that only its printed heading remains visible, then folds it away with obvious reluctance.

Beyond the doors, students hurry past a display case of measuring instruments. A sharp voice objects to someone carrying a cup too near it. From farther down the corridor comes a delighted shout followed by a much less delighted demand for a cloth.

"Your packet should have the introductory lesson choices," Fumito says. "Do you want the straightforward directions, or the version with everything useful along the way? I can do either."

He pauses.

"The straightforward version is genuinely shorter. I've been told to say that first."

# Dragon Hoard: Arrival Gallery

Your first day at Dragon Hoard begins among expedition exhibits and strict notices. Kōsuke Shishido can explain check-in—once he finds the staff standing beside him.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The adult player is admitted to Dragon Hoard, holding the configured packet. Kōsuke Shishido is the existing second-year student present in the Arrival Gallery, not an assigned guide or companion. He has misplaced his actual staff in this gallery; this is a small scene circumstance, not a new quest, theft or equipment grant. Do not invent a named familiar or a permanent new pet for the player. Stop with his request unanswered.]

Yellow light falls through the high windows of Dragon Hoard's Arrival Gallery. Behind glass, an expedition case displays a cracked compass, a recovered stone relief and a field journal opened to a sketch of the site where both were found. The labels devote more space to safe handling and provenance than to the discovery itself.

A tall wooden staff stands beside the visitors' bench.

Kōsuke Shishido looks under the bench, behind the bench, and finally at the ceiling.

"They said tall objects are easiest to overlook because you expect them to be somewhere important," he explains to the newcomer. "I don't know if that's true, but I've already tried everywhere unimportant."

His pockets rustle with wrapped animal treats as he stands. Then his bright amber eyes settle on the admission packet.

"Oh! New student. Main doors, then the check-in desk. Don't touch the expedition seals in the Exhibition Hall. Apparently 'it looked lonely in the case' isn't a reason to open one."

He delivers this advice with the confidence of hard-won experience. Farther inside the academy, an instructor's voice demands an explanation for a diagram drawn across the wrong floor tile. Several passing students straighten their backs at once.

Kōsuke does too, a moment late.

"I'd show you the desk, but I should really find my staff before somebody takes it to lost property again."

The staff remains beside the bench, its gold-colored cap level with his shoulder.

"Have you seen one? Quite big. Very difficult to miss, usually."

# Hydra Head: Arrival Court

Arrive for enrollment at Hydra Head. Kana Fujimoto has the directions, the lesson plan, and a small enchantment mishap she would very much like to keep off both.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The adult player has been admitted to Hydra Head and has the configured packet. Kana Fujimoto, an existing junior instructor, is on welcome duty in the Arrival Court for this start. Use her ordinary professional manner and real modest Void ability; neither her affinity nor this scene makes her an intimidating master. The small wardrobe mishap belongs to background activity, not a named new NPC or emergency. Wait before the player's response or registration.]

Violet awnings shade Hydra Head's Arrival Court. On a long table, campus plans sit beneath paperweights shaped like the academy's emblem. Somewhere beyond the garden walk, a glass chime sounds three clear notes. An open door carries the scent of fresh flowers and the less romantic smell of wet ink.

Kana Fujimoto checks a line on her clipboard, looks at the arrivals, and begins with carefully rehearsed confidence.

"Welcome to Hydra Head Academy. Registration is in Confluence Hall. Please keep your admission packet with you until—"

A bright ribbon escapes from a nearby display and winds itself enthusiastically around a chair leg. Kana watches it for a moment.

"—until check-in is complete. Please also avoid accepting demonstrations of permanent enchantment before you've found your classroom."

She frees the chair with the air of someone who did not put this on the lesson plan. The ribbon settles. Her shoulders settle with it.

"There. That's better."

The noticeboard lists introductory lessons, supervised practice and open consultations. Beside it, students compare invitations to an afternoon exhibition. Some look polished enough to have arrived for a portrait sitting; others have ink on their cuffs and breakfast in their hands.

Kana gives the newcomer a more natural smile.

"Do you need directions, or is there something you'd like to ask before you go in? It's all right if the question seems obvious. I'd rather explain it now than discover someone's been attending the wrong lesson for a week."

# First Day of Work: Phoenix Wing

An accepted appointment brings you to Kōhei Sanada's office. Establish your duties in an academy that approaches induction almost as seriously as dueling.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The adult player has an accepted appointment at Phoenix Wing, represented by the configured letter. Begin in Faculty Offices with Kōhei Sanada at his actual office location. Match the job to the player's established qualifications: do not turn a groundskeeper into a professor or replace an authored employee. The headmaster's questions establish remaining duties; no signature, oath or assignment is accepted on the player's behalf. No colleagues join the party.]

The noise from Phoenix Wing's practice yard reaches the Faculty Offices in firm, regular impacts. Inside, the desks are orderly. A dueling schedule covers one wall; beside it, a cooking competition has somehow acquired a schedule almost as complicated.

Kōhei Sanada stands behind a cleared corner of his desk. His uniform is pressed, his black hair neatly combed. A plain saber rests in its scabbard within reach, neither displayed nor hidden.

"Good. You've found the office."

His gaze drops to the appointment letter, then returns to the new employee.

"Before anyone gives you six jobs because you happen to be standing here, we'll establish the one you were hired for."

He lays out a short induction sheet: responsibilities, access, who to contact when a student is hurt, and where to report damaged equipment. The emergency section is underlined once. The section about tournament volunteers is underlined twice, apparently by someone else.

A cheer rises from the yard and stops on a whistle. Kōhei listens until the ordinary rhythm resumes.

"The students will test what you allow. So will some of the faculty. Be clear with them. If you don't know something, say so and find out. I would rather hear that than watch someone improvise a safety rule."

His expression softens by a degree.

"What do you need from me before we begin?"

# First Day of Work: Gryffin Tail

Norio Shibata has your induction folder ready. Start an ordinary working life in a place where the research is extraordinary and the room numbers still need correcting.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The player has accepted a job at Gryffin Tail, with the configured appointment letter. Norio Shibata receives them in Faculty Offices, where he is already placed. Preserve the actual job and credentials; he is an administrator, not a secret prodigy or conspiratorial recruiter. The player's answer determines which induction details matter. Do not invent a signature, contracted obligation beyond the existing appointment, or access to restricted research.]

There is a place for everything in Gryffin Tail's Faculty Offices, and Norio Shibata appears to know all of them. He takes a folder from a gray tray, checks its cover, and sets it square with the desk edge.

"Good morning. New appointment?"

His neutral robes, thinning gray hair and mild expression make him seem almost restful after the cyan banners and crowded research notices outside. He indicates the visitor's chair without making a ceremony of it.

"I have your induction materials. Please check that the role on the letter is the role you accepted. It's easier to correct before someone builds a timetable around it."

Three sheets lie ready: duties, ordinary access and the contact list. A fourth has been removed from the stack and marked obsolete. Norio notices the newcomer's glance.

"Old room numbers. Somebody keeps copying the wrong master."

From the corridor comes an increasingly technical argument about a piece of apparatus. Norio waits until the voices move away, then resumes at exactly the same volume.

"If an instruction is unclear, ask for clarification. If a record is inaccurate, bring it here. If the coffee urn has stopped working, the fault has already been reported. Repeated reports don't repair it faster."

He opens his notebook to an empty line.

"Now. What would you like to check first?"

# First Day of Work: Dragon Hoard

Begin your accepted job with Miyu Asahina, a clear induction plan and several forms competing for her attention. Your role follows your actual background.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The adult player holds the configured Dragon Hoard appointment letter and has a real accepted role consistent with the selected background. Miyu Asahina receives them at Faculty Offices, her saved position. Preserve the academy's strict expedition and relic procedures, with Miyu's bright practical leadership. Her workload is scene texture, not an automatic request to do all of it. No grant of research clearance, court key or expedition command follows from choosing this start.]

Three stacks of folders move across Miyu Asahina's desk on a controlled breeze. A fourth stays firmly beneath her hand. Its cover bears a smiling seal and the words: AN EXCITING OPPORTUNITY TO SIGN THIS.

Miyu looks up with a quick, genuine smile.

"Welcome! Please tell me you're my new appointment and not another request to skip the equipment return register."

She catches herself and laughs.

"Sorry. That was the last three visitors. Yours is the folder without the red corner."

The Faculty Offices are bright with yellow trim and very serious notices about field equipment. One shelf holds completed survey reports; another holds boxes awaiting assessment, each securely tagged. Nothing resembles an abandoned treasure pile.

Miyu sets an induction plan on the clear part of the desk. It has a start time, an end time, and a break that has been circled with considerable determination.

"We'll confirm your duties, introduce the people you'll actually work with, and make sure you can find what you need. If someone tries to add a surprise expedition to that list, send them to me."

A loose slip rises from the nearest folder. She pins it back with a finger without looking away.

"And if I start explaining three procedures at once, you're allowed to stop me. What would make your first day useful?"

# First Day of Work: Hydra Head

Meet Kiyoko Fujinami for a quiet induction in the Confluence Pavilion. Tea is offered; your duties and first questions are still yours to settle.

[OPENING DIRECTION: The adult player has accepted employment at Hydra Head and carries the configured appointment letter. Begin in Confluence Pavilion, where Kiyoko Fujinami is actually located. This is a quiet induction meeting, not an assumed friendship, servant relationship or invitation to private research. Match duties to the player's background. Reika Saionji is not automatically present; references to her use the canonical full name in director records, natural address in dialogue. Wait for the player's answer.]

The Confluence Pavilion opens onto Hydra Head's garden paths. Violet shade cloth tempers the light, and a small table has been arranged with an appointment book, two cups and a tea timer that looks more exacting than some examination clocks.

Kiyoko Fujinami closes the book as the new employee arrives. Her formal uniform is immaculate; her voice is quiet enough to draw attention without asking for it.

"Please, take whichever seat suits you. The one on the left squeaks. We are negotiating."

She gives the chair a level glance, then returns to business.

"Your appointment letter should describe your post. Today we'll settle the practical details: hours, resources, responsibilities, and whom to ask when somebody has described all four incorrectly."

Through the garden, two people carry a long display frame toward the main building. They stop, turn it sideways, reconsider, and carry it back. Kiyoko watches for just long enough to establish that nobody is in danger.

"Research and public work share our rooms. That occasionally produces an unusual timetable. It need not produce an unclear one."

She turns the appointment book toward the visitor. The page has space for questions as well as instructions. The tea timer reaches its mark, and she removes the leaves with a practiced motion.

"Tea, if you would like it. Then tell me what you need to do your work properly."

# Adventurer: Guild Counter

Morning sun falls in green and honey-colored pieces across the guildhall tiles. Someone at the request board is arguing that a delivery cannot possibly count as an expedition. The person beside him points at the distance written on the slip and keeps chewing her breakfast.

At the counter, a young woman in a brick-red waistcoat is drawing something with far too many legs in the corner of her own scrap paper. Her peach-colored ponytails shift as she looks up, blue eyes bright.

“Welcome. New registration, looking for work, or here to explain why the parcel is making that noise?”

Komugi Hanabishi slides the scrap beneath a ledger with a completely unconvincing air of innocence.

“If you’re applying, we’ll start with the licensure assessment. You can use what you actually know—blade, bow, magic, whatever your method is. If you already have a genuine badge, show me and we can look at the board.”

Behind her, marked drawers hold records. Across the hall, bronze, silver, gold and pale platinum tabs divide the notices. A kettle clicks softly near the communal tables; beyond a rear doorway, a practice target swings and settles.

She rests her pen against the open ledger.

“So. What brings you to our counter?”

# Merchant: First Morning

Find your commercial footing in Four Sages Bazaar. Rinpei Komatsu has a demonstration, practical advice and a question about what business you want to do.

[OPENING DIRECTION: Begin at Four Sages Bazaar - North Arcade with the existing stallholder Rinpei Komatsu at his real position. This is the player's commercial foothold, not proof of purchased stock, ownership of a stall, a trading license or a prearranged sale. Use the actual character background and inventory to establish what business they intend. The demonstration trinket remains Rinpei Komatsu's property. No quest or transaction is accepted in the opening.]

The North Arcade is opening for business. Wooden shutters knock back into place, a cart wheel protests at the paving, and warm bread competes with lamp oil in the morning air. Near the arcade's edge, Rinpei Komatsu lifts a little brass bird from a folding display case.

It sings three beautiful notes.

Then it sneezes.

"That last part is intentional," Rinpei tells the newcomer. "The first prototype just stopped. Customers felt judged."

He sets it beside a row of household trinkets and smooths the persimmon sash at his waist. Behind him, his repair screwdriver lies on a square of cloth. A handwritten card separates working stock from demonstration pieces.

"First morning trading here? Best advice I can give you: ask what a space costs before someone starts helping you fill it. Very friendly market. Astonishingly enthusiastic about fees."

Beyond his stall, an empty patch beneath the awning offers shade, but no sign says who controls it. Porters are already unloading farther along the arcade. The fountain sounds through the gaps between shouted prices.

Rinpei studies the newcomer with open professional interest.

"Looking to sell, buy, find a supplier, or work out what people here will actually pay for? Those are four different conversations. Usually all before breakfast."

# Village Life: Brookmeadow

A quieter beginning in Brookmeadow: a mill yard, ordinary neighbors, and Haruho Taneda's mildly infuriating tally slate. Be a local, returner, worker or visitor according to your background.

[OPENING DIRECTION: Begin in Brookmeadow - Mill Lane with its authored mill-yard hand Haruho Taneda. The player can be a resident, returning local, new worker or visitor according to their chosen background; do not invent family, a house, a farm, debt or previous intimacy. Haruho Taneda knows an established local only when that history actually supports it. His immediate request is an optional small interaction, not an automatically accepted quest.]

Grain dust floats through a shaft of sunlight in Brookmeadow's Mill Lane. Beyond the working yard, water turns with a steady wooden clatter. Cottages line the quieter side of the lane; farther off, the Village Green carries the voices of people setting out for the day's work.

Haruho Taneda stands over two sacks with a tally slate in one hand and a deeply dissatisfied expression.

"Well, one of these has been marked twice, or somebody's grown a very modest extra harvest."

He brushes dust from a clean patch on the nearer sack, compares the marks, and shakes his head. His patched apron is already flour-white at the waist. A breeze stirs the corner of a covering cloth, and he secures it with his elbow.

"Morning. If you've a moment, can you tell me whether that's a three or an eight? I've been staring at it long enough to dislike both."

There is nothing alarming about the sacks. A few need moving under shelter before the weather changes, and a cart by the gate has a wheel awaiting a repair. The road toward Glowhaven remains open beyond the town, but here the immediate business is grain, neighbors, and whether the roof over the yard will keep another season's rain out.

Haruho turns the slate so its smudged figure is easy to see.

"No hurry if you're going somewhere. It'll still be an irritating number when you get back."
