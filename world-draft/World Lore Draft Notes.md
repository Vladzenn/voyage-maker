## 2026-10-26 — Archons and school leadership

- Archons are exceptionally powerful and capable figures. Their stature is part of the office: each represents their academy.
- Each Archon is the academy's public face and holds its seat on the Conclave of Archons.
- The headmaster manages the academy's school system and day-to-day institutional operation; the Archon represents the academy and serves on the Conclave.

## 2026-10-26 — Magic learning and affinities

- All schools of magic can be learned by anyone.
- Having an affinity in a type of magic makes it easier to learn spells from that affinity; it does not prevent someone from learning other types.

## 2026-10-26 — Academy names, philosophies, and civic roles

- The four academy names are Phoenix Wing Academy, Hydra Head Academy, Gryffin Tail Academy, and Dragon Hoard Academy.
- Strength, harmony, study, and wonder are provisional shorthand for the founding Archmages’ philosophies, respectively. They are not strict definitions of the academies or rules for their students.
- The academies provide broadly comparable magical education. A student's personality, beliefs, talents, and interests are not determined by their academy. Students may embody or contradict its reputation, creating natural interpersonal and comedic tension.

## 2026-10-27 — Academy civic responsibilities

- The academies are more than places of study: each will also tend to a civic duty in Glowhaven City.
- The specific civic duties have not yet been decided.

## 2026-10-26 — The Evertree

- The official and exclusive name of the city's magic-enhancing landmark is **the Evertree**. Always call it “the Evertree”; do not use a nickname, shorthand, or alternate title.

## 2026-10-26 — Glowhaven locations

- The Grand Sage's Court is the public governing palace before the Evertree; formal court business and the Great Sage's work take place there.
- Sage's Spire is the Great Sage's office tower. Stargarden Manor is the Great Sage's residence.
- Wraith Island, Wraith Island Prison, and Shadowspire are marked undiscovered and should not appear on the map until discovered. Wraith Island Prison is operational.
- Shadowspire has a forest approach. Travelers lose their way while trying to approach its tower, and the tower cannot be entered.
- The Perfumed Rose is owned by an alchemist. Perfumes are its main specialty, though it also sells other alchemical goods.
- Moonwhisker Emporium serves both summoned companions and ordinary pets. Familiars and summons are common pets in the city, while cats, dogs, and birds are also kept.

## 2026-10-26 — Elvish language

- Elvish is represented as French in story text and dialogue. When a character speaks Elvish, write the spoken words in French.
- At least one Elvish nation exists beyond Glowhaven City. Its name and further details are undecided. Élodie Vautrin is a foreign student from this nation.

## 2026-10-27 — Everyday magic and the Evertree's appeal

- Magic is commonplace in Glowhaven. Most ordinary residents use minor cantrips and find it convenient to remain with their natural affinity.
- Serious mages, including academy students, study several elements and magical theory. Learning beyond an affinity is difficult at first, but practice makes the unfamiliar element more comfortable to use.
- The Evertree thickens the currents of mana in the air and surrounding environment. These currents are easier to sense and control, almost physically tangible, making Glowhaven an unusually favorable place to learn and improve.
- The city attracts people interested in studying, using, and developing magic, including practical and commercial uses.

## 2026-10-27 — Government, nobility, and civic responsibility

- Glowhaven is substantially self-governing. The Great Sage is treated locally as a king or queen; the precise constitutional relationship with the wider kingdom remains open.
- Noble standing can involve formal titles and fiefs granted as rewards, accumulated wealth, inheritance, and strategic intermarriage.
- Many noble houses are involved in infrastructure and the development of the city and kingdom. Specific grants, titles, house portfolios, and obligations remain to be developed.
- The City Guard performs the city's policing work.
- The Great Sage's elite force has military and sensitive security responsibilities comparable to an elite military and confidential service. Its personnel may also serve as diplomats to neighboring nations.
- Exceptional power does not replace the people and expertise needed to administer and develop a society. Daily life is mostly ordinary work rather than a constant succession of major attacks.

## 2026-10-27 — Provisioning, commerce, and major events

- Extensive surrounding farmland supports Glowhaven's food supply. The harbor supplies fish and supports imports and exports.
- The city's outstanding commercial specialty is quality enchanted items and magical trinkets.
- Glowhaven has many festivals. The major colosseum tournaments at the Eternal Crucible are among its biggest public events.
- Currency, prices, detailed economic mechanics, and a formal festival or tournament calendar remain future work.

## 2026-10-27 — Schools of Magic canon and academy mandates

The supplied Schools of Magic lore sheet's accepted author decisions supersede inconsistent older descriptions. The current user instruction further establishes Dragon Hoard's exploration-and-relics focus, superseding the sheet's description of that mandate as pending. Strength, Harmony, Study and Wonder are retired shorthand, not governing academy ideals.

Phoenix Wing serves defense, readiness and protective fieldwork. Hydra Head serves diplomacy, trade, logistics and civic coordination. Gryffin Tail leads research and field investigation and alone formally specializes in magitech among the academies. Dragon Hoard emphasizes exploration, relic assessment and responsible recovery; its strict inherited traditions reflect its founder. Broad magical and combat education remain comparable, and students retain varied personalities. Existing courts, policing, property claims and offices retain their authority.

Every student must complete an expedition beyond the city, authorized by the Great Sage or court. Actual scope, supervision, participants, field outcome and debrief matter. The requirement need not involve killing monsters, and does not force a visitor to enroll or accept a particular mission.

Souls enable intentional mana circulation. Ordinary animals are soulless and may contain inert mana but cannot deliberately cast; mana beasts are distinct. Capacity has a partly inherited starting point and develops through use. Uptake varies, and genuine clear-minded rest improves recovery. The Evertree thickens local mana without becoming its universal origin.

Body casting is trainable. Catalysts use mana-bearing materials and generally assist control without a spare reservoir. Premium external reserves are costly and awkward; useful direct mana-transfusion engineering remains unsolved beyond weak prototypes. Enchantments are distinct from mechanical engineering integrated with magic. Personalized spell blueprints can use optional aids; true Freeform is rare and difficult. Intentional bodily enhancement is Enchantment and can become very powerful. Arcane body wards are possible without guaranteed immunity.

Zero usable mana leaves a caster conscious. Ordinary self-casting cannot spend the soul-sustaining remainder. A deliberately forced last spell at zero very rarely succeeds through an exceptionally difficult authoritative check; repeated forcing brings migraines. Hypothetical external extraction below the usable threshold causes unconscious soul shock, and complete extraction kills; no humanoid practitioner is witnessed. Surviving shock may require weeks or months of recovery. Extremely rare permanent harm requires abrupt, rapid shock. An attempted extraction is treated as attempted murder, subject to evidence and actual judicial process.

Healing primarily treats physical injury, while its channeling can assess and support circulation. Mana potions are expensive regulated medicine. Excess ordinary doses cause unpleasant effects and diminished returns, not fatal overdose; dangerous stronger formulations are research-only. Restored MP does not instantly cure soul shock. During live tournament duels, external stored mana and mana potions are prohibited; non-battery catalysts remain permitted and emergency treatment after an official stop is allowed.

Implementation choices are distinct from these facts: variable +1–3 MP per tick preserves the prior +2 mean; existing rest, capacity and level gains remain. A party quest ledger records one chosen authorized expedition, and a flag records official duel state. No third resource, fixed biological growth formula, generic potion dose/price, witnessed drain ability, new founder history or automatic relic ownership is established. The sheet's remaining questions stay open.

### 2026-10-28 — Drakenspire national expansion, approved author answers

Drakenspire names both the independent nation and its capital. Most of the city occupies a snowy mountain peak, with a large fortress on a higher northeastern shelf and the busy dragonkin city below. An enormous ancient protector dragon's skeleton curves around the fortress in a crescent. The protector saved the endangered founders; the remains are sacred history, not a heat or mana generator. The settlement developed mining without becoming exclusively a mining culture. Ordinary government buildings are restrained alpine fortress architecture; wealthy commercial buildings may be fancier.

The Jarl is elected through clan chiefs within a decentralized monarchy. Clans, merchants and military leaders influence decisions. Dragon and ancestor worship shapes public life. Local personal names remain Japanese-inspired in First name Last name order despite Norse/Gaelic cultural inspiration. A huge gray guardian has four legs, two wings, lightning breath and intelligent spellcasting. He is wise, generally unhurried and rarely seen except far overhead as a warning of danger. His lair is unknown. Generations of sacred dragons exist, but their number and history are not fully known.

Dragonkin are roughly human-height and may live about two centuries. Scale color is hereditary but has no inherent magical or social meaning. Drakenspire and Nahlah Qamar's desert population are regionally varied members of the same species. Human faces, horns, facial side scales, slit pupils, scaly backs of arms/hands, human palms and thick tails remain the visual definition.

The nation supplies mana crystals, amber, local creature materials and excellent metallurgy, especially spellblade equipment. Rare unfertilized dragon eggs left as gifts contain prized mana-bearing material; shed scales are also valued. Water comes from deep aquifers, with geothermal springs and one onsen. Goats, sheep, preserved supplies and trade support food and clothing. The local school is a modest ordinary building; serious magic students often go to Glowhaven. The two nations are friendly sovereign allies. Street duels are forbidden; a small fight pit provides entertainment. Lower streets accommodate dragon festivals, inns, taverns and an adult nightlife district.

Outer country contains snowy mountains, sparse pine folds, valleys, caves, working and abandoned mines, abandoned settlements, giant territories and wyvern nests. Wyverns are smaller two-legged shrieking pests, avoided rather than routinely culled; dragons dislike them. Dragon presence discourages monsters from entering the city without constant direct intervention. Avalanches and territorial beasts are real dangers. Yeti are real, rare isolated giants, while most people only speculate about them.

Authored implementation: 60 Drakenspire locations / 253 areas, a temporary 8-column grid, six supporting factions, three prominent named clans and two new creature types. See the national lore, catalogue and map brief for the full scoped details. These new site and clan names are creative implementation choices, not additional verbatim author statements. No regional NPC cast or map image was generated. Existing images, NPCs, quests and portal mechanics are preserved.

### 2026-10-28 — Disciplines, invention and physical exertion
