## Canonical realm

Drakenspire is an independent dragonkin-majority nation and the name of its snowy mountain capital. The city occupies two nearby elevations: a bustling inhabited shelf below, and Crescent Crown Fortress on a higher northeastern shelf. An enormous sacred dragon skeleton curves around the fortress in a crescent, joining stone walls and ancient bones into the country's defining landmark. The remains commemorate the dragon that protected the endangered founders; they do not power the city's heat or magic.

Practical alpine stone buildings, steep cobbled lanes, heavy shutters and wool-bright interiors characterize the lower city. The fortress government is sturdy rather than extravagant, while a few wealthy commercial buildings advertise private prosperity. Markets, homes, workshops, inns, a modest schoolhouse, festival streets, a small fight pit and one onsen support ordinary life. Dragon and ancestor worship shapes public customs without making every resident equally devout or every dragonkin a warrior.

A Jarl elected through the clan chiefs governs within a decentralized clan-and-chief order. Clans retain substantial influence, alongside merchants and military leaders. Drakenspire and Glowhaven are friendly sovereign partners: this country supplies excellent metallurgy and valuable magical raw materials, while Glowhaven provides enchanted goods, specialist education and other trade. The enclosed Drakenspire - Portal Room receives authorized travelers and connects to Evertree Base - Portal Room under the existing key rules.

Beyond the inhabited shelves lie lower pastures, sparse pine hollows, mines, abandoned workings, neighboring peaks and dangerous snowbound country. Some routes suit prepared fieldwork; others reach wyvern perches, giant territory or rarely visited basins. Sacred dragons discourage many threats from approaching the capital without constantly fighting on its streets. A vast gray, lightning-breathing guardian sometimes appears far overhead. His lair, the number of dragons and the full history of their generations remain unknown to the people.

## Canonical region

Drakenspire Highlands covers the capital's two snowy shelves and its surrounding mountain country across the mountain basin. The higher northeastern fortress and crescent of ancestral dragon bones dominate the skyline; the lower shelf holds the busy city. Near slopes support livestock, water infrastructure and maintained approaches. Farther routes reach sparse pines, a frozen basin, working mines, old settlements, caves, Frostback Mountain and isolated creature territories. Places within the fortress precinct are separate playable sites in one coherent complex, not detached castles. The region's existing boundaries contain these expeditions; travel within it does not invent additional regions. The enclosed portalward quarter remains the arrival point from Glowhaven.

## Full public location and area descriptions

### 01. Drakenspire Portals

Drakenspire's portalward quarter is the usual first stop in the snowy capital. An enclosed basalt transit hall opens into a compact market and communal hearth court; beyond them, steep lanes connect the lower city's homes and workshops. Above the roofs to the northeast, the fortress rises within the immense crescent of the ancestral dragon's bones. Travelers can arrange cargo, buy a hot meal, obtain directions and hear current road conditions before choosing where to go. This map location covers the arrival quarter; the wider city and nation also bear the name Drakenspire.

People associated with this place include: Fuyumi Kirihara — Portal arrivals clerk; Daigo Numata — Portal cargo porter. The authorized transit desk can approve a legitimate visit to Glowhaven and issue its reusable portal key locally. An approved journey is separate from merely standing at the arch.

**Drakenspire - Portal Room**

A warm vaulted basalt chamber encloses the permanent Glowhaven arch. Iron roof ribs, blue mineral veins and brass runic channels frame the crossing; frosted high windows reveal the weather. A duty desk and clear cargo floor leave passengers room to arrive. The fixed home connection returns to Evertree Base - Portal Room; its operation follows the existing route and key rules. Actual doors lead to the Cobbled Market, Hearth Court and Steep Lanes. Fuyumi Kirihara is usually based here, working as portal arrivals clerk.

**Drakenspire - Cobbled Market**

Low stone stalls shelter beneath striped wool awnings. Vendors sell travel food, crampon repairs and inexpensive gloves, while porters compare cargo slips. This is a small arrival market; the broader household trade happens at Hearthmarket Square. Displayed merchandise remains the vendors' property.

**Drakenspire - Hearth Court**

A hooded brazier warms a sheltered court with stone drains and benches cut to leave space for tails. Residents trade news beside posted local work. A traveler may eat, wait for a companion or ask directions without being drawn into an emergency.

**Drakenspire - Steep Lanes**

Steps and narrow ramps climb between dark stone houses with blue-gray roofs. Handcarts use the ramps; pedestrians yield at blind corners. The lanes lead into the lower city, while an uphill view repeatedly catches the fortress bones through gaps between chimneys.

**Drakenspire - Cargo Recess**

Iron floor rings and numbered chalk bays keep freight clear of the portal. Attendants compare declared loads with their documents before release. A torn packing label may require the owner to identify a crate; opening it or taking its contents is a separate matter. Daigo Numata is usually based here, working as portal cargo porter.

### 02. Embersteel Foundries

A connected group of working foundries turns mountain ore into dependable metal stock, tools and substantial commissioned work. Soot-dark stone, extraction chimneys and sheltered loading courts make the district feel industrial without requiring every machine to be magical. Customers arrange specifications in the Commission Hall before entering supervised work areas. Drakenspire's reputation for metallurgy rests on skilled labor, careful testing and materials knowledge. The foundries supply smaller specialist workshops as well as larger orders, so a commission may involve several businesses rather than one master who does everything.

People associated with this place include: Chisato Higuruma — Embersteel foundry forewoman.

**Embersteel Foundries - Commission Hall**

Sample bars and finished fittings line a high room separated from the furnaces. Clerks and craftspeople translate intended use into a specification and quote. Customers can discuss repairs or deliveries without wandering into active metalworking.

**Embersteel Foundries - Forge Walk**

A protected walkway overlooks furnaces, hammers and crews handling bright metal. Heat, noise and moving loads demand attention. Visitors remain in marked safe spaces unless an actual supervised task takes them onto the working floor. Chisato Higuruma is usually based here, working as embersteel foundry forewoman.

**Embersteel Foundries - Testing Court**

A walled court holds measured loads, impact blocks and quenching stations for appropriate inspections. Tests answer a specific question about a real workpiece. Watching a demonstration does not grant the observer the item or its properties.

**Embersteel Foundries - Materials Yard**

Sheltered bays separate ore, scrap, fuel and finished stock. Weight slips accompany substantial deliveries. A mismatched label or poor storage can cause an expensive problem before any metal reaches a furnace.

**Embersteel Foundries - Repair Bay**

A quieter side bay receives damaged tools and machinery fittings. Workers separate straightforward maintenance from faults needing specialist enchantment work. Repairs are assessed, agreed and carried out rather than completed by merely discussing them.

### 03. Stonefang Training Grounds

Stonefang provides supervised practice space for local fighters, guards on arranged sessions and visitors seeking instruction. It is a training ground rather than a large magical university. Its open ring, stone steps and equipment shelter support drills, sparring and practical evaluation. The ground welcomes different methods when they can be used safely in the available space; spectacular magic is not automatically suitable. Lessons and partnerships depend on the actual instructor or participant, with room for ordinary repetition and imperfect progress.

People associated with this place include: Ryōma Arashimine — Clan Arashimine heir and commissioned duelist; Tetsuya Samejima — Stonefang instructor.

**Stonefang Training Grounds - Open Ring**

A cleared ring accommodates supervised drills and agreed sparring. Marked edges help participants judge distance. Entering it does not start combat until the people involved have agreed what they are doing. Ryōma Arashimine is usually based here, working as clan arashimine heir and commissioned duelist.

**Stonefang Training Grounds - Stone Steps**

Broad steps beside the ring serve as seats and places to discuss a technique. Observers can watch without joining. A patient explanation or useful correction can be more valuable than another exhibition of strength.

**Stonefang Training Grounds - Equipment Shelter**

Maintained practice gear is stored under a deep roof. Items are issued for actual sessions and checked on return. Visitors do not own a weapon merely because they have been allowed to train with it.

**Stonefang Training Grounds - Target Lane**

A screened lane permits suitable ranged practice with clear firing and retrieval periods. Distance and backstops constrain safe techniques. Spells that exceed the facility's limits require another venue, not an implausibly indestructible wall. Tetsuya Samejima is usually based here, working as stonefang instructor.

**Stonefang Training Grounds - Balance Course**

Low beams and uneven stone blocks support controlled footwork drills. Snow and ice are cleared or deliberately accounted for. The course builds practical movement without being a lethal obstacle gauntlet.

### 04. Whitepass Caravan Lodge

A substantial lodge near the overland approach accommodates carriers, expedition parties and visitors who need more than a quick meal. Its common hearth, route desk and covered yard connect lodging with practical travel preparation. Rooms are sturdy and warm rather than luxurious, and staff understand drying gear, early departures and delayed companions. The lodge is a useful base for mountain work without making every guest a guide or every rumor a contract. Returning safely can be reason enough for an uneventful evening here.

People associated with this place include: Natsume Hibari — Licensed mountain adventurer and expedition guide; Akemi Fushida — Proprietor of Whitepass Caravan Lodge.

**Whitepass Caravan Lodge - Common Hearth**

Long tables surround a broad hearth where wet clothing dries at a sensible distance. Guests eat stew, trade road news or sit quietly. A returning party can celebrate without the room producing a new emergency on cue. Akemi Fushida is usually based here, working as proprietor of whitepass caravan lodge.

**Whitepass Caravan Lodge - Route Desk**

A desk holds copied route notes, local notices and contacts for transport. Advice distinguishes maintained paths from uncertain reports. Staff can help arrange a service, but they cannot promise that a high pass is safe in changing weather. Natsume Hibari is usually based here, working as licensed mountain adventurer and expedition guide.

**Whitepass Caravan Lodge - Covered Yard**

Pack animals and carts shelter beneath deep eaves. Tie rails, repair blocks and water access support real travel work. Loads remain in their owners' custody, and animals are handled with permission.

**Whitepass Caravan Lodge - Guest Landing**

Simple private rooms open from a warm upper corridor. Quiet hours and paid arrangements keep tired travelers from disturbing one another. A closed door is not an invitation to inspect someone else's belongings.

**Whitepass Caravan Lodge - Drying Room**

Racks and controlled warmth dry boots, cloaks and ropes without scorching them. Labels prevent accidental swaps. Damaged gear can be noticed here before another journey, creating a practical reason to visit an outfitter.

**Whitepass Caravan Lodge - Whitepass Drying-Loft Room**

A warm private room with a sturdy bed, gear hooks and a shuttered mountain window above the lodge drying loft.

### 05. Rimecrest Trail

A marked highland trail provides the most approachable excursion beyond the capital's immediate roads. It crosses exposed ridges, sheltered turns and places used for route and weather surveys. Prepared travelers can practice navigation, observe local ecology or carry out authorized fieldwork without immediately entering the deepest monster territory. The views are wide, but conditions can change quickly. Marker Ridge, Windbreak Shelter and Survey Shelf are established stops; pushing beyond the maintained route is a separate decision.

**Rimecrest Trail - Marker Ridge**

Weathered poles mark the maintained route along a broad ridge. The city remains visible in clear weather, while cloud can erase it within minutes. Travelers compare markers and footing before continuing instead of following every apparent track.

**Rimecrest Trail - Windbreak Shelter**

A low stone shelter holds a bench, a protected floor and a notice recess. It is a temporary refuge, not a staffed inn or limitless supply cache. A previous traveler may have left a useful dated warning.

**Rimecrest Trail - Survey Shelf**

A stable shelf overlooks slope, snowpack and distant animal routes. It suits careful observation and agreed field measurements. Leaving equipment or collecting specimens requires the same judgment and permissions as any actual expedition.

**Rimecrest Trail - Return Junction**

A marked junction points toward the lower approach and more demanding routes. Signs summarize destinations, not current certainty. Turning back here is a normal choice when visibility, gear or time makes further travel unwise.

### 06. Crescent Crown Fortress

The capital's upper stronghold occupies the higher northeastern shelf, reached by a rising, guarded approach. Thick pale-gray walls, square towers and practical slate roofs enclose courts, stores and offices. An enormous dragon skeleton curves around the stronghold's outer rock, making fortress and remains a single unmistakable landmark. The Jarl's Hall, Clans' Assembly House and Crescent Sanctuary occupy distinct precincts of this larger complex. Visitors come with petitions, official business or permission to see the public courts; the fortress is a working seat of government rather than an extravagant palace.

**Crescent Crown Fortress - Ascending Approach**

A zigzag stone approach climbs from the lower city to the upper shelf, its parapets broken by sheltered passing bays. Couriers, carts and visitors share the route. From below, the ancestral ribs appear beyond the walls like another range of pale cliffs.

**Crescent Crown Fortress - Outer Arch**

A deep archway narrows entry beneath heavy gates. Guards direct public visitors toward the court and examine freight destined for government stores. Their ordinary work is traffic, access and weather readiness; a portal credential is not a fortress appointment.

**Crescent Crown Fortress - Public Court**

Packed snow surrounds a cleared square between administrative buildings. Petitioners wait near a roofed notice wall, while laborers cross with timber and coal. Signs and attendants distinguish the routes toward the Jarl's Hall, assembly precinct and sanctuary.

**Crescent Crown Fortress - Stores Passage**

A vaulted corridor connects locked grain, fuel and equipment stores. Ventilation grilles breathe cold air across numbered doors. Deliveries are weighed here, and access depends on the actual job; scarcity cannot be solved merely by declaring the shelves full.

**Crescent Crown Fortress - Inner Ward**

A smaller guarded court separates offices from private residential rooms. Plain windows and covered walks speak of warmth and security rather than ceremonial grandeur. Authorized messengers wait under the eaves instead of wandering into the Jarl's household.

**Crescent Crown Fortress - Rampart Walk**

An inspected stretch of wall overlooks the lower roofs and neighboring peaks. Public access depends on weather and duty. The sacred skeleton lies outside the masonry; nobody uses its bones as a railing or convenient source of building material.

### 07. The Ancestor's Crescent

The remains of the ancient protector lie in a vast crescent around the northeastern fortress shelf. Weathered ribs rise above stone walkways, the spine follows the mountain's curve, and the skull rests on a broad natural ledge. The founders sheltered under this dragon's protection when they were close to dying on the mountain; their descendants regard the remains as sacred history. The bones define the city's silhouette but do not generate its heat or mana. Keepers maintain respectful viewing paths and support fragile sections without turning the skeleton into a public climbing frame.

People associated with this place include: Keishin Tōdō — Crescent archivist and conservator; Miharu Sakuraba — Sacred-bone conservation craftswoman.

**The Ancestor's Crescent - Pilgrims' Approach**

A broad stair ends at a low stone boundary where visitors brush snow from their boots. The first ribs loom beyond a turn in the path, larger than the buildings below. Keepers explain which sections can be approached safely.

**The Ancestor's Crescent - Rib Walk**

A paved route follows the outside of the rib cage behind a modest rail. Prayer ribbons hang from separate wooden frames, never nailed into the bones. Quiet conversation mixes with wind passing through the enormous spaces between the ribs. Miharu Sakuraba is usually based here, working as sacred-bone conservation craftswoman.

**The Ancestor's Crescent - Skull Overlook**

The skull lies beyond a respectful viewing distance on its rock shelf. Visitors leave lamps at a stone stand, with the fortress rising behind the curve of bone. The overlook offers no ordinary passage into the skull or beneath its unstable edges.

**The Ancestor's Crescent - Spine Terrace**

A sheltered terrace follows a section of vertebrae half-buried in old snow. Carved plaques recount the founders' refuge and later settlement. The path is useful for slow historical walks, remembrance and observation of the surrounding masonry.

**The Ancestor's Crescent - Conservators' Shelter**

Brushes, survey notes and temporary braces fill a low maintenance shelter. Keepers and stoneworkers compare cracks and snow loads before work begins. A visiting researcher can ask to examine the records; study of the remains requires actual permission. Keishin Tōdō is usually based here, working as crescent archivist and conservator.

**The Ancestor's Crescent - Windward Boundary**

A final low wall closes the public route before exposed scree and cliff. Warning markers are practical, not a challenge to prove devotion. Beyond it, the bones continue around the rock where only trained maintenance parties venture.

### 08. The Jarl's Hall

A long, sturdy hall within Crescent Crown Fortress houses the Jarl's public business. Dark beams, clan banners and a broad central hearth provide ceremony without lavish palace ornament. The Jarl is elected through the clan chiefs rather than inheriting an unquestionable throne, and governs a country whose clans retain substantial local influence. Petitioners seek decisions on roads, trade, defense and disputes beyond a single household. Access to a hearing depends on business and availability; being an impressive visitor does not automatically secure an audience.

People associated with this place include: Daizen Iwatsugu — Jarl of Drakenspire and chief of Clan Iwatsugu.

**The Jarl's Hall - Petition Porch**

A sheltered porch holds benches, a public notice board and a table for sorting requests. A clerk distinguishes urgent harm from ordinary appointments. Visitors can learn the procedure without surrendering every detail of a private dispute to the waiting crowd.

**The Jarl's Hall - Hearth Hall**

The audience hall centers on an open hearth and raised working seat, with clan banners overhead. Hearings have witnesses, interruptions and practical limits. The room may be between sessions; an empty seat is not proof of a missing ruler. Daizen Iwatsugu is usually based here, working as jarl of drakenspire and chief of clan iwatsugu.

**The Jarl's Hall - Council Chamber**

A smaller chamber accommodates negotiations that need maps, accounts or difficult compromise. Thick shutters soften the wind against the fortress. Invitations matter here, and a public petition does not confer attendance at every closed discussion.

**The Jarl's Hall - Records Room**

Shelved copies of judgments, commitments and correspondence preserve the government's memory. Clerks retrieve relevant records according to access and condition. A public ruling can be consulted without exposing private testimony or giving visitors ownership of the documents.

### 09. Clans' Assembly House

A broad stone chamber beside the fortress court provides neutral ground for the chiefs' deliberations and the election of the Jarl. Carved seats face an open speaking floor; the room is practical enough for ordinary budgeting as well as solemn decisions. Clan autonomy makes agreement necessary, while merchants and military leaders exert influence through testimony, resources and reputation. The assembly is not always in session. Residents come to hear public business, submit a grievance or learn which authority can actually resolve it.

People associated with this place include: Shūhei Iwatsugu — Clan Iwatsugu works surveyor and aspiring expedition leader; Noboru Ōhira — Clan Assembly recorder.

**Clans' Assembly House - Delegates' Court**

Clan messengers and witnesses wait beside stone benches and a covered water stand. Public notices identify the day's business. The court is a place for conversation and preparation, not an automatic invitation to overhear confidential bargaining. Shūhei Iwatsugu is usually based here, working as clan iwatsugu works surveyor and aspiring expedition leader.

**Clans' Assembly House - Speaking Floor**

A clear central floor faces the chiefs' benches. Speakers present claims, answer objections and refer to actual evidence. Disagreement can end in adjournment or compromise; every debate need not become a duel, election or national crisis.

**Clans' Assembly House - Witness Gallery**

An upper gallery accommodates permitted observers and people awaiting a call to testify. The low ceiling traps the warmth of the chamber below. Whispered commentary travels easily, and a visitor's interpretation is not the same as the assembly's decision.

**Clans' Assembly House - Charter Room**

Bound agreements, route obligations and old settlements are kept in plain cupboards. Custodians help locate a relevant clause while protecting fragile originals. Learning the law is possible here; changing a document does not change every copy or everyone's recollection. Noboru Ōhira is usually based here, working as clan assembly recorder.

### 10. Crescent Sanctuary

This restrained sanctuary faces the ancestral bones from the fortress precinct. Its faith honors dragons as sacred beings and ancestors as people whose shelter, labor and choices made the present possible. Lamps, stone reliefs and woven memorial cloths fill a low, warm interior. Keepers conduct remembrance, receive offerings and teach the founding account. Outsiders may ask questions or sit respectfully without pretending belief. Religious observance shapes public life, but individual residents differ in devotion, interpretation and patience with ceremony.

People associated with this place include: Seika Amatsune — First Keeper of the Crescent.

**Crescent Sanctuary - Lamp Porch**

Sheltered stone niches hold lamps protected from the wind. Visitors pause here to warm their hands and ask about the day's observances. Public notices distinguish memorial gatherings from ordinary visiting hours without treating every outsider as a suspect.

**Crescent Sanctuary - Hall of Refuge**

Reliefs show exhausted founders beneath a protecting dragon, followed by the first shelters and workings. A central aisle leaves space for quiet gatherings. The story is told as the community's foundation, not a prophecy identifying the newest traveler. Seika Amatsune is usually based here, working as first keeper of the crescent.

**Crescent Sanctuary - Offering Chamber**

Covered tables receive food, cloth and other accepted gifts before their proper disposition. Keepers record significant offerings and prevent spoiled provisions from accumulating. Gifts intended for a living dragon do not guarantee that one will arrive to take them.

**Crescent Sanctuary - Memorial Alcove**

Small family tokens rest beside names and dates in a softly lit recess. People speak to their dead, sit silently or share a remembered meal nearby. These objects are personal memorials, not random magical treasure.

**Crescent Sanctuary - Keepers' Workroom**

Repair thread, festival schedules and conservation notes crowd a practical workroom. Volunteers can ask about specific tasks. The sanctuary's care includes sweeping, recordkeeping and coordination with stoneworkers as much as public ritual.

### 11. Ancestor Cairn Terrace

Low cairns and family memorial stones occupy a sheltered terrace below the sacred crescent. Paths remain broad enough for winter funeral processions, with places to rest out of the wind. This is a living place of remembrance, used by clan households and residents without grand lineage alike. Visitors may trace local history through inscriptions or accompany someone paying respects. The terrace is peaceful more often than eventful; grief and ordinary care need no supernatural revelation to matter.

**Ancestor Cairn Terrace - Procession Gate**

A broad stone gate opens onto paths swept after snowfall. Covered benches allow mourners to gather before continuing. Flowers, evergreen sprigs and wool wraps appear according to the season and household, rather than one universal ritual.

**Ancestor Cairn Terrace - Family Cairns**

Low mounds and engraved stones mark generations of residents. Some names are freshly cut; others have almost weathered away. Visitors can read public inscriptions, while private grief belongs to the people present rather than to a compulsory quest.

**Ancestor Cairn Terrace - Common Memorial**

A plain wall remembers people whose families could not maintain a separate monument. Workmates and neighbors leave small lamps here. Its care reflects shared civic responsibility as well as religious custom.

**Ancestor Cairn Terrace - Sheltered Remembrance Bench**

A curved wall shields a line of benches overlooking the lower city. This is a quiet place to talk, eat a small packed meal or sit without speaking. The sound of festival drums can occasionally drift up from far below.

### 12. High Rampart Gate

A fortified gatehouse controls the road from the inhabited lower shelf to the northeastern fortress precinct. It combines a switchback, inspection arch and sheltered waiting yard so winter traffic can be handled without blocking the entire ascent. Guards check appointments, dangerous loads and current restrictions. Ordinary people have business here—deliveries, petitions, repair work and religious visits—while access to military interiors remains separate. The gate's importance comes from its position, not from elaborate magical spectacle.

People associated with this place include: Reina Tachibana — Captain of the Upper Ward and fortress inspections; Hayate Mizusawa — Upper gate guard.

**High Rampart Gate - Lower Switchback**

A winding approach gives carts room to climb the grade without a straight charge at the gate. Snow poles mark the outside edge. People on foot use the inner steps and can see the lower city expanding beneath them.

**High Rampart Gate - Inspection Arch**

Under the heavy arch, guards compare visitors' stated business with the access being requested. There is room to stand aside while a delivery is checked. The inspection concerns the fortress; it does not revoke valid portal travel by implication. Hayate Mizusawa is usually based here, working as upper gate guard.

**High Rampart Gate - Waiting Yard**

A roofed edge of the yard shelters people delayed by weather or appointments. A brazier and posted notices keep waiting tolerable. Conversations here can reveal practical concerns, though nobody knows all government business merely from standing in line.

**High Rampart Gate - Watchroom**

The duty room holds route boards, spare cloaks and current orders. Entry requires a reason to speak privately with the watch. The staffing and readiness of a working gate persist after the player leaves. Reina Tachibana is usually based here, working as captain of the upper ward and fortress inspections.

### 13. Oathward Barracks

Plain stone barracks house the hold's guard detachments and their equipment. A courtyard permits drill without turning the lower streets into a training ground. The force protects gates, patrols public roads and responds to violence; it is answerable to local government, not a branch of Glowhaven's strikeforce. Visitors can report danger, deliver supplies or inquire about lawful work at the duty entrance. Sleeping quarters, weapons stores and operational records remain restricted.

People associated with this place include: Gōki Shishido — Commander of the Drakenspire Hold Guard.

**Oathward Barracks - Duty Entrance**

A small counter receives reports, deliveries and requests to speak with the watch. Wet cloaks steam on hooks behind it. Urgency is assessed from what happened and where, with witnesses and injuries treated as evidence rather than theatrical proof.

**Oathward Barracks - Drill Court**

Packed gravel and snow cleared from the center leave space for formation work and practical exercises. Racks hold training equipment during supervised use. The court is a workplace, not an open arena for challenging every guard. Gōki Shishido is usually based here, working as commander of the drakenspire hold guard.

**Oathward Barracks - Equipment Stores**

Locked cupboards contain winter gear, maintained weapons and repair supplies. Issuing an item requires an actual assignment or authorized transaction. Issued equipment is logged, and shortages can delay an assignment.

**Oathward Barracks - Mess and Rest Wing**

Long tables and adjoining sleeping rooms accommodate off-duty personnel. The atmosphere is tired and ordinary between shifts. Access follows an invitation or official need; uniforms do not remove privacy or the need to rest.

### 14. Dragonwatch Balcony

A broad sheltered lookout on the upper rock faces the high ranges. Residents watch changing clouds, distant wings and the paths by which trouble may approach. The gray guardian is occasionally seen far overhead, and such a sight draws serious attention, but the balcony is usually a quiet place for weather watching and conversation. It gives no view of a confirmed dragon lair. Visitors can compare sighting records, sketch the mountain skyline or simply appreciate the city below.

People associated with this place include: Kotoha Umezaki — Dragonwatch observer and weather diarist.

**Dragonwatch Balcony - Wind Screen**

A thick wall breaks the prevailing wind before the open viewing edge. Hooks hold communal weather notices, and a narrow bench gives tired climbers a rest. Heavy snow can make the exposed part of the balcony unsuitable for lingering.

**Dragonwatch Balcony - Sky Rail**
