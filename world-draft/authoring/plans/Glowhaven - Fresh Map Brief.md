## Purpose

## The place

Glowhaven is an independent, prosperous center of magical education and craft. One great walled city shares its territory with modest villages, working farms, western wooded hills, river country, eastern coastal settlements and offshore islands. It is a lived-in country, with many ordinary homes and workplaces between the named institutions.

## The central landmark

The Evertree rises from the ground beside the northern civic precinct, behind the court approaches. Its living trunk and canopy are the main magical silhouette. The court, Sage's Spire and rootward grounds form a close group, but they are not interchangeable buildings. The portal hub is beneath the rootward structures; distant realms do not occupy extra land on this map.

## Composition

Use a horizontal 5:4 canvas corresponding to five columns and four rows of square regions. North is up. The city occupies much of the upper-left and upper-middle landscape. Working harbor and shoreline open toward the eastern sea; the bottom half becomes increasingly rural. The whole map is one continuous landscape, not twenty unrelated illustrations.

## What makes it large

The city must read as a major settlement containing dense blocks of ordinary roofs, several different residential textures, workshops, institutions and multiple campuses. Rural houses are the same physical size as comparable city houses. Fields, woodland, meadows, water and distance provide scale. Do not make a cottage huge simply to make it recognizable at first glance.

## How to use the catalogue

## Scale and architecture

### View

A high oblique aerial view with a consistent ground-plane registration. Roofs dominate, but enough wall faces remain to distinguish buildings. Avoid a foreground panorama in which the southern cottage appears larger than a northern city block. A shallow camera, huge sky band or strong foreground enlargement defeats the requested scale.

### Ordinary buildings

Proposed master-scale budget: a typical house or shop is about 0.10-0.22% of total canvas width. On a hypothetical 5000-pixel-wide composite, that is approximately 5-11 pixels. This is an art-direction example, not a promised native generation size or a real-world measurement.

### Institutions

A public hall may be about 0.4-0.8% of master width; a multi-building academy precinct about 2-3%; the great arena about 1.5-2.2%. These are aggregate footprints, not a rule to enlarge every roof within a campus. Retain enough space between nearby landmarks.

### The tree

A proposed Evertree canopy width of roughly 4-6% of the master keeps it exceptional without swallowing the city. Its crown and height are above-ground mass; its exact anchor refers to the ground-level base, not the top of the canopy. Its final size should be judged against the adjacent court and city, rather than fixed by pixels alone.

### Rural restraint

Most countryside is landscape. A village is a small group of normal-sized roofs; an outpost is a yard and a few structures. Towers, ruins, mills and the abbey are limited accents. Large meadows and woods are intentional. Do not fill empty space with new stone circles, castles, ruins or identical decorative cabins.

### Road character

City paving ends at the appropriate gates. Country routes become narrow dirt or cart tracks, with lesser paths fading into working land. Bridges reach both banks. Keep the main avenue readable, but avoid a broad paved highway cutting through all the southern plains.

### Detail

Build small structures from coherent footprints, roof planes, walls and access. Do not simulate detail with noise, random greebles, melted roofs or sharpening halos. In later close crops, invent credible construction from the written role instead of reproducing blurry master-image marks.

### Interpretation

Coordinates, names, area membership and described functions are data facts. Unspecified roof pitches, stone colors, timber details and minor yard arrangements are proposed visual interpretations. They may vary consistently across the new design. Do not treat those proposed finishes as newly established world lore.

## Position and interpretation notes

### Positions are graphic anchors

The current world uses regionSize 100 and local coordinates from -50 to +50. Positive y is north. The master percentages below retain the whole 5-by-4 grid; they are not rescaled to the occupied pin cluster. They locate ground-level anchors and do not prescribe physical building size.

### Names that sound similar

Pebbletown is southwest of the city; Pebblewick is the small eastern coastal town; Brookmeadow is far southeast. Clovermead is a northern farming settlement reached by boat. They are four different settlements. West and East Greyfield are the adjoining poor settlement outside the southern city walls.

### Cross-border sites

Seabreeze Inn is in the Crescent Coast region but belongs to Pebblewick across the region boundary. Stargarden Manor is near the boundary beside the civic district. Thistlebank Alchemy Cottage is exactly on a tile edge, Hermit's hut is on a southern tile edge, and Longgrass Crossroads is almost on a northern edge. These are not duplicate sites or reasons to interrupt paths.

### Broad descriptions versus pins

The Winding Lowlands is described as remote rural water country, but its current anchor is in the northwest region. Follow that anchor for this brief; keep the settlement tiny. The current Shadowspire pin is at the northeastern side of Greenfield Farmlands, not in the far southern corner. No old map composition overrides these current values.

### Linear and areal places

The Grand Approach, Grand Ascent, Merchant's Row, Pinewater River, beaches, farms, woods and neighborhoods extend away from their pins. Design a continuous route or area that includes the anchor, rather than shrinking an entire river or district into a circular marker.

### Separate realms

Wraith Island is the coastal landing and approach visible here; the prison interior has its own playable realm. Evertree portals go to distant regions rather than creating another academy portal on the landscape. Shadowspire's private realm lies beyond a cellar gate, not behind the tower as eight more exterior castles.

### Living creatures

Nerathyl sleeps at the fixed Bluecoil reef; Mauruun is the passive giant turtle associated with Stillback; Ghorvath is a roaming kraken. Their habitats and deepwater routes matter. A static background is not proof of their current life, defeat or position, so optional sightings must not create duplicate bosses.

## Master-to-crop workflow

### First image

Generate the complete landscape once. Check coast continuity, the three wall gates, the civic axis, all four campuses, major island separation, open southern country and the tiny building scale before spending effort on individual windows.

### Split from one master

For square region crops, split five across and four down. Core bounds are A-E = 0-20, 20-40, 40-60, 60-80 and 80-100% across; rows 1-4 = 0-25, 25-50, 50-75 and 75-100% down. Use the original master for every crop rather than recursively cropping previous crops.

### Supply context

When refining one part, provide the accepted whole master, the target crop and this PDF's matching region entry. Include adjacent edge context. An overlap around 10-15% of a tile's width is a starting production choice; explicitly record core and overlap bounds.

### Rebuild detail

Reconstruct tiny buildings from their purpose, placement and footprint. Sharpen coherent roofs, entrances, bridges, courtyards and terrain forms. Do not turn blurred pixels into sacred architectural facts, move ground anchors, enlarge rural buildings, add unsolicited landmarks or change the camera.

### Seams

Match roads, riverbanks, wall segments, coastlines and lighting across every shared strip. Generative refinement cannot guarantee pixel-identical edges. Align and blend in Photoshop using masks or soft erasing, preserving the intended shared geography.

### Final pass

Check the assembled result at whole-map scale and at native crop scale. Tiny shops may remain subtle; they should not be inflated to make their identity readable in a thumbnail. High resolution means credible extra structure, not arbitrary texture or an automatically upscaled image.
