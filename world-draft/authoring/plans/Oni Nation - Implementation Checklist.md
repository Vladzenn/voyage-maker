# Portal to the Oni Nation — Implementation Checklist

Planning supplement, 2 November 2026. Requested checklist review plus one portrait style study. **No playable content, world JSON, global portrait rules or platform settings are changed.** The [existing map/content plan](Portal%20to%20the%20Oni%20Nation%20-%20Research%20and%20Map%20Plan.md) holds the approved direction and earlier working estimates; this supplement turns the author's expanded checklist into coverage and acceptance criteria. Counts and unapproved names remain proposals.

## 1. Settle the geography and authority first

- [ ] Reconcile the author's arranged 4-column × 3-row atlas with the earlier landmark sketch. Use the latest actual image/coordinates when supplied; do not force the old proposed anchors onto different buildings. One capital, two castle towns, connected countryside and forests at all outer edges remain the direction.
- [ ] Trace roads, river crossings, mountain passes and useful shelters. Give each province a recognisable landscape, a safe route where appropriate and optional risk beyond it. A region is not one uniform encounter level.
- [ ] Identify the capital castle, sacred mountain, ordinary transit torii, separate Soul Torii, branch guild, towns, shrines, dojos, workplaces and settlement neighborhoods before adding every individual interior as a location. Rooms belong in areas when that produces clearer navigation.
- [ ] Define what the Shogun, territorial houses, magistrates, local watch and independent organizations can actually command. Give each significant faction a resource, constituency, internal disagreement and something it cannot solve alone. Do not create a faction for every shop or extended family.
- [ ] Establish foreign visitors' transit authorization and local legal procedure. Existing Glowhaven wanted-poster and Wraith custody rules do not automatically apply abroad. Determine the treatment of violence, weapons, forbidden workings and disputed land without writing a punishment controller for every offense.

## 2. Make a country people can live in

- [ ] Staff most inhabited establishments with appropriate people; leave wilderness appropriately sparse. Include farmers, millers, boat workers, couriers, cooks, innkeepers, artisans, healers, performers, clerks, shrine attendants and ordinary households alongside fighters.
- [ ] Build samurai and shinobi as occupations and traditions, with varied employers, methods and loyalties. Oni, humans, beastfolk, fae and intelligent yokai should not inherit a profession, moral alignment or political allegiance from ancestry.
- [ ] Distribute selective relationships: a few families, former teachers, old comrades, marriages, debts, petty feuds and friendships across faction lines. Some people can be solitary. Characters need appetites, bad habits, humor, ambitions and opinions beyond the quest they introduce.
- [ ] Distinguish major people through agency, relationships and portrait flair. Officials and masters need not all be exceptional combatants; ordinary civilians do not inherit a high wilderness level minimum.
- [ ] Give public institutions real functions: magistrate hearings, watch assistance, clinics, an ordinary school, archives, shrine services, storehouses, water maintenance and a place to resolve disputes. Institutions need usable services and opening situations, not only architectural descriptions.
- [ ] Include food, lodging, baths, theater, fairs, seasonal customs and quiet places. Rentals and modest ownership can use the existing address-specific key convention after an actual agreement. Avoid compulsory recurring chores or a new household simulator.

## 3. Guild work, roads and discoverability

- [ ] The capital branch recognizes the same international badges. Existing members do not repeat registration or receive an extra badge; an unlicensed visitor does not become licensed by reading the board. Posted rewards remain the amount the player receives.
- [ ] Provide beginner work around levels 1–5, a substantial lower/midgame selection, and clearly advertised optional dangerous contracts. Ordinary offers suit the participating players; a strong NPC companion does not turn a beginner errand into an elite hunt.
- [ ] Mix local-exclusive hunts with escorts, repairs, missing travelers, delivery disputes, field surveys, shrine problems, salvage and questionable employment. A few jobs have complications; straightforward jobs may remain straightforward.
- [ ] Put opportunities on roads and in villages: stranded porters, damaged bridges, rival merchants, pilgrims, traveling troupes and people already dealing with a problem. Not every traveler requests help or conceals an ambush.
- [ ] Offer only a small relevant selection at a time. Overheard news and visible problems reveal circumstances without accepting work, leaking a secret or teleporting the party. Declining one job should not immediately produce the same demand from another NPC.
- [ ] Distinguish lawful criminal capture, a dangerous creature hunt, a missing person and mediation with an intelligent spirit. A bounty needs a client, target identity, basis for the claim, accepted forms of proof and a clear reward owner. Species is not proof of guilt.

## 4. Yokai, wildlife and memorable hunts

- [ ] Research specific folklore accounts before authoring adaptations. Use local ecology, habits and human customs to distinguish creatures rather than turning every legend into a hostile reskin. The setting's version may deliberately differ from its inspiration.
- [ ] Ordinary populations and repeatable encounters use npcTypes; persistent individuals whose history, relationships or defeat matters can receive named NPC cards. Separate appearance, intelligence, disposition and danger.
- [ ] Populate fields, bamboo, cedar woods, marshes, mountain roads, ruins and abandoned works with different encounter pools. Telegraph hazardous routes; maintain safe civic spaces and believable retreat. Do not use travel encounters as a substitute for what inhabits a location.
- [ ] Start with a small set of named roaming quarry, each tied to two or three plausible haunts. Preserve its identity, actual condition and resolved outcome. Rumors can be uncertain, but the target must not change species or become a stronger replacement every time it is found.
- [ ] A hunt can proceed through rumor, evidence, chosen pursuit, tracking and resolution. Combat, traps, relocation or negotiation fit different targets; not every monster is negotiable and not every spirit must die. Pay once for the accepted undertaking.

Possible original hunt pitches, not approved canon: a bell-antlered boar that destroys abandoned ward posts; a reed-masked ferryman whose dangerous toll concerns a broken local agreement; a roof-running ember beast mistaken for an arsonist; and a hollow festival armor that follows a displaced household banner. Their true causes, levels and outcomes require individual design, rather than four copies of the same twist.

Primary reference leads: [Nichibun's folklore database](https://www.nichibun.ac.jp/YoukaiDB/) and [Fukusaki's locally grounded yokai examples](https://fukusakikankou.jp/en/the-yokai-of-tsujikawayama-park/). These support research into variation and specific associations; they are not a monster stat catalogue or authority over our world's lore.

## 5. Dojos, equipment and player builds

- [ ] Give a few institutions genuinely different teaching practices: sword/spear work, archery and fieldcraft, unarmed movement, shinobi reconnaissance and shrine disciplines. Imported magitech has a few specialized practitioners rather than a routine school in every town. Mentorship may involve practice, fees or relationships without a new hard skill-acquisition lock.
- [ ] Include demonstrations, training partners, disagreements and personal goals. Dojos should remain useful without making each one a second Crucible or an academy curriculum state machine.
- [ ] Audit existing weapons, armor, abilities and talents first. Add regional tools and techniques that fill actual playstyle gaps: polearms, bows, hooked/chained weapons, practical armor, travel gear, limited Arcane/Soul devices and useful noncombat craft.
- [ ] Preserve martial and noncaster viability. Spellcasting uses its discipline; invention uses Spellcraft; demanding martial techniques use Stamina. Do not turn shinobi into an unapproved new magic discipline or every resident into a Soul specialist.
- [ ] Give rewards a use: an unusual material, service, technique, contact or piece of equipment. Respect existing item ceilings and currency rules. No broad combat rebalance or compulsory power inflation is authorized by this expansion plan.

## 6. Arcs, starts and the two gates

- [ ] Build a few independent arcs with specific public premises, concealed stakes and several acceptable conclusions: political obligations, a personal/dojo story, a road or village conflict, and the Soul Torii investigation. Avoid making every local problem part of one conspiracy.
- [ ] Use ordinary native quests for accepted tasks, arcs for developing stories, and exact triggers for necessary state changes such as earned gate awakening. Each undertaking has one completion/reward owner. Do not add an offer/completion state machine to every conversation.
- [ ] Prepare the five requested local openings—samurai, shinobi, ronin, shrine apprentice and flexible civilian—with an authored scene, real contacts and one useful immediate choice. A possible sixth guild arrival remains optional. An opening need not determine ancestry, permanent allegiance or all later training.
- [ ] The authorized capital torii belongs to the Evertree network and returns to Evertree Base - Portal Room. The Soul Torii is a different site, unlocked through its own local story/item and returning from spirit wilderness to that same local anchor. Neither should compete with the protected once-only Spider anomaly.
- [ ] Decide whether Soul Torii awakening persists before implementing it. An inventory condition can be an internal progression test without claiming trophies or arbitrary carried objects are magical ingredients. Keep public item descriptions natural.

The community [arc reference](https://wiki.unofficial.voyage/world/arcs) describes a premise-based generated surfacing trigger and runtime quest labeling. That is useful scaffolding, not a guarantee of a fixed sequence or automatic alternate-ending support. Keep a short authored chain where a precise prerequisite/reward is essential; verify actual engine behavior during play.

## 8. Magic presentation and cultural direction — author's revision

The author rejected a separate regional rendering style, prescribed warm lighting and a limited clothing palette. Keep the established colorful 2D anime portrait treatment and existing composition/role guides. Clothing colors and patterns vary richly for ordinary people as well as major characters. Lighting follows the actual scene. No extra global style or palette rule is authorized.

The selected difference is **the visual vocabulary of practiced magic**. In the sample crop, Wind reads as broad, tapered, translucent ribbons with overlapping soft edges and open space. The supplied usual portraits emphasize bright specular water droplets, glassy surfaces, sharp gold fractures, hard luminous lines and many small particles. This comparison concerns these examples, not every existing portrait; soft Wind and forceful Water/Earth are also different disciplines and roles.

Proposed short prompt addition for locally trained magic:

> Favor flowing, softly translucent magical forms with tapered, brushlike edges and graceful directional motion. Let the character's actual discipline determine the substance and color. Keep the effects vivid and readable, with fewer hard glitter points and crystalline highlights where they are not intrinsic to the technique. Large or powerful spells may remain spectacular. Preserve our usual character rendering, varied clothing and scene-appropriate lighting.

Soft describes flow, edge treatment and material highlights, not weak magic, a blur filter or a ban on sharp Lightning/Metal/Ice. Fire can unfurl in flowing tongues, Water form smooth washes and streams, and Soul appear as drifting layered wisps; distinct disciplines must remain recognizable. A fighter's learned technique and personality can justify a harder effect. Foreign visitors retain their own presentation. This remains a preference for future briefs, not an extra native NPC field or an appended global Image Prompt instruction.

### Draft lore for later merge

Many local traditions teach magic through felt mana, breathing, rhythm, movement, visualization and repeated inherited forms. Teachers often ask a pupil to recognize the right sensation and reproduce it before putting an explanation into words. Formal theory and research still exist, and practitioners disagree about the merits of foreign analytical teaching. This is a cultural tendency, not universal anti-intellectualism or a change to the world's four casting approaches, discipline mappings or mechanical requirements.

Magitech is a recent and unevenly distributed import. Some houses buy foreign mechanisms, hire specialists and experiment in guarded workshops; many residents have never handled such equipment. Most buildings, crafts and daily routines remain traditional. Engineered katanas, crossbows and armor can become expensive military assets and points of political contention. Their actual mechanisms, power supplies and maintenance distinguish them from an ordinary enchanted object. **There are no guns or firearms in this direction: magitech crossbows and swords occupy the disruptive imported-weapon role.**

The author reserved **one extraordinary prototype weapon concealed by a major antagonist**, using the supplied Chuunibyou weapon image as a design/scale reference. Here it is a real experimental weapon, not a delusion. Its owner, maker, workings, limits and eventual availability remain to be designed. It is not representative civilian equipment or a standard shop item. The Blue Eye Samurai analogy guides the political significance of imported arms; it does not import that show's plot or assert historical facts about Japan.

The single existing sample is an unnamed adult Oni innkeeper with Wind affinity. She is a style study, not an added NPC. Her warm lantern setting and indigo/vermilion outfit are individual choices, not regional requirements. Her headroom is tighter than the production target; the requested one-pass example is retained without an automatic reroll. No new image was requested by the revision.

## 9. Build order and release checks

1. Approve final national/capital/house names, geographical anchors, transit authorization and legal scope. Reconfirm the Soul Torii persistence choice.
2. Build the map/roads, capital services, guild, small initial cast and five openings from the latest supplied Studio export. Validate before mass population.
3. Expand civilian settlements and dojos, then native quests, local encounters and a few stable roaming hunts. Add independent arcs and the small spirit slice after the basic crossing/return loop works.
4. Fill equipment/technique gaps, generate portraits in curated waves, and illustrate selected important locations. Music follows the actual situation; merely mentioning a fight must not change a peaceful conversation into combat music.
5. Check fresh local and visiting starts, natural short destinations, level-1 solo work, martial builds, declined work, interrupted quests, absent NPCs, lost keys, finished hunts, repeat rewards and multiplayer participation. Verify ordinary returns in structured state. Preserve existing media/coordinates, all other realms and the Spider Realm exactly.

Before announcing release, separate local validation from actual Studio import and live tests. Existing saves may retain their old content; do not promise automatic migration. Keep author/generation instructions out of public lore, cards, item descriptions and quest prose. Current AI fields are near their caps, so regional detail belongs in scoped world content rather than pasted into every global instruction.
