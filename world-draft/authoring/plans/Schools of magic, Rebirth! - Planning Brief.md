# Schools of magic, Rebirth!

## The Wizard of Shadowspire

Planning brief · 4 November 2026

**Release scope.** Feature the new reincarnated opening first in Story Starts and in the announcement. Build the Wizard of Shadowspire as its long-form storyline, with permission-based exploration of the tower and the pocket realm beneath it. Add Beastfae to character creation, expand the general lore, target about 100 additional scrolls with a shared study prototype, and include the approved field-exam trigger improvement, general optimization review and a [general tone audit](Schools%20of%20magic%2C%20Rebirth!%20-%20Tone%20Audit.md). The author is adding a trading port outside Glowhaven beside Brookmeadow. It gives the player somewhere to work, shop and spend time between chapters. This pass drafts the content and leaves it unapplied to WorldAlpha.

The two author prompts are recorded verbatim in [Author proposals](#author-proposals) below.

## The featured start

**Proposed menu title** · Rebirth — A Morning in Brookmeadow

**Menu description** · You wake up in a body that isn’t yours, with your previous life still in your memory. The people who found you unconscious on the shore brought you to Brookmeadow. They know where they found you. What you tell them is up to you.

> You wake up in a body that isn’t yours.
>
> The face in the washbasin is unfamiliar. The last room you remember is clear enough to walk through in your head. You know where its door was and what you could see through the window. This window looks out on a mill yard.
>
> A woman takes a bowl off the stove. There is sand on the floor beneath the chair beside your bed.
>
> “We found you unconscious on the shore. Kenta ran for me. You’ve been here since dawn.”
>
> Outside, someone sets a bucket down.
>
> “I’ll tell Haruho I’ll be late,” Kenta says.
>
> She sets the broth within reach and sits. “When you feel up to it, tell me what to call you.”

The opening ends before the player answers. The bowl and the question about their name give them something immediate to respond to. They can ask where they are, test the new body, explain or conceal their memories, or walk out to meet Kenta. The narration should adapt the bodily cues to the chosen character. The last remembered room in the sample is a replaceable detail. The player owns their name, ancestry, appearance, past-life particulars and response. Keep the previous occupant’s fate open until the author decides what happened.

**Opening circle.** Stage the recovery in a cottage off **Brookmeadow - Mill Lane**. This is a proposed recovery scene within an existing area. Keep the exact shore address open until the author places the port and confirms the rescue site. Use one new recurring cottage resident as the practical caregiver. She wants her spare room back before the next market day and has a batch of lamp covers to finish. Kenta already works around **Brookmeadow - South Road**. His habit of making promises on the spot and his desire for a paid music booking give him reasons to check in and reasons to leave. **Haruho Taneda** already works on Mill Lane and can be the first recurring work contact. Bring him in after the player steps outside or asks about work. **Shiori Kawase** can reappear later at **Pinewater River - Brookmeadow Ford**, where she has her own work and judgment. Establish why each person is present. Keep help, friendship, lodging arrangements, lessons and recruitment voluntary.

**Summoned one** · “You woke in another body with the memories of your previous life intact. So far, you alone know what happened to you.”

Use **Summoned one** as the origin trait, with no stat package, automatic magical mastery or public allegiance. Give it only through the exact Rebirth Story Start. Voyage documents start-based trait eligibility in character creation. For an automatic grant, prototype a one-shot mechanical trigger at tick zero and verify the actual timing in Studio. Keep the trait out of ordinary creation and level-up choices. Use a typed `player-traits` check on the ambush path, together with the exact `story-start`. The start identifies the campaign, the trait identifies the intended character, and the story supplies the court's lead. The trait is private to the player's story. NPCs act on facts they can learn in the world. Test the lobby, player sheet, narration, NPC dialogue and journal. Keep the start first in the list. Default selection remains open. Prototype in solo. In shared play the trait condition can match any party member, so prove recipient targeting and later joins before enabling ambushes there. ## Beastfae character creation

**Beastfae** are a rare playable ancestry with upright, person-shaped bodies and strongly animal features across the face and body. A wolf-like Beastfae can have a full muzzle, a fur coat, and padded hands. A lizard-like Beastfae can have a reptilian head, scales and a tail. They choose names, clothing, professions and affinities as individuals. The attached illustration is a reference for the range of animal heads and silhouettes. Its designs inspire original characters.

**Draft creator copy.** A rare ancestry with animal-shaped faces, upright bodies, and a range of fur and scaled forms. Choose a form and give it your own colors, build and clothes.

| **Fox** | Tapered muzzle, tall ears and a full tail, with distinctive individual markings. |

## The story across a long save

### Act I · A life in Brookmeadow

**1. The shore and the spare bed.** The player wakes in a borrowed bed. The people who carried them in say they found them unconscious on the shore. Recognition comes through the unfamiliar body and intact memories. The rescuers know where they found the player and what condition they were in. The player can decide what to tell them. Once they have had a chance to look around, a local offers two ordinary directions. There is work around town, or the player can head to the **Adventurer's Guild - Glowhaven Branch** in the main city. Introduce the branch through the normal Reception Counter and its actual registration and contract rules.

**2. Something worth coming back for.** Give the quiet stretch its own satisfying end. A suggested village episode is a market delivery on a bad river day. The mill owes a waterside cook a load of flour, Shiori closes an unsafe crossing, and Kenta has promised to arrive in time for a paid set. The player can find a safe route, renegotiate the delivery, support Kenta separately, or make other plans. A city-bound player can reach the same stage by establishing a contact, taking work or settling into guild or academy life. Allow several ordinary scenes and a meaningful foothold before the delegation enters the player's story. A level gain alone advances neither the inquiry nor the hunt.

### Act II · The Sage's visitors

**3. An inquiry without a name.** One day, an Ardelis delegation passes through and locals wonder why it is there. The delegation says it has business with the Great Sage, **Ilyra Stillglass**. A real diplomatic audience is a plausible reason to be in Glowhaven, where cooperation in Ardelis's war has already been refused. Privately, the current Ardelis court mage felt the original summoning interrupt and throw its subject off course. That supplied a broad direction. The later delegation lets the court ask about odd sightings, unusual rescues or shore traffic. Its members can compare a place and a period. They begin with no name, face or certain identification. The inquiry takes place after the player's ordinary first act.

**4. The man they recognize.** Once some of the delegation's soldiers have started asking intrusive questions, **Valkazathoth'dmir**, called **Vladimir**, interrupts in person. One officer who knew the old court recognizes him and abruptly goes quiet. The reaction shows what his name means to that officer. Vladimir asks what business with the Great Sage requires her visitors to question people on the shore. The officer backs off and the soldiers leave the encounter. Vladimir gives the player a look that could mean he has put something together, bids them farewell and goes. He offers no diagnosis, lesson, invitation or explanation in this scene. He spends most of his time at Shadowspire, where he tries to follow what Ardelis is doing. Intercepted correspondence or targeted observation could supply individual facts. His unusual excursions are authored scenes with a reason to be there.

> The officer turns. His hand comes away from the ledger.
>
> “Master Vladimir?”
>
> “The Great Sage receives visitors in the city,” Vladimir says. “What business of hers has brought you to this shore?”
>
> The officer closes the ledger and motions his people back toward the road. Vladimir watches them go. His gaze rests on you a moment longer than courtesy requires.
>
> “Good afternoon,” he says, and takes the other road.

**5. Cloaked hirelings.** The first personal attack happens later, after a real lead gives the people paying for it a place to look. A proposed lead is a shore witness or copied rescue report whose time and place match the court mage's trace, followed by a sighting that identifies the person taken in. The attackers appear to be hired people with concealed faces. The leading design is that an intermediary supplied advanced magitech constructs that pass for people at a glance. They try to seize the target or test their defenses. The intermediary gives them only a local description and a task. Their knowledge ends there. They carry no dispatches or identifiable maker's mark, and have no way to send back the player's identity or location. If genuinely captured alive, the constructs automatically go inert and are treated as dead. A capture attempt, an offer of surrender or a momentary knockdown is insufficient. An inspected shell can reveal a generic fused regulator or a broken command component. It can establish that the attackers were constructs. The connection to Ardelis comes from independent evidence such as Vladimir's surveillance or a witness who actually saw the intermediary meet a delegate. This route also works for a player who kills the attackers, escapes or avoids them. These attacks are exclusive to the Rebirth opening and its **Summoned one** bearer. A small, finite sequence is enough.

**6. An invitation to Shadowspire.** In a later eligible attack, a one-time chance may bring Vladimir into the scene if he has a plausible reason to be close. Treat the chance as a brief appearance after the encounter has resolved. Vladimir comes to see who was using his roads and can offer a meeting on his own terms. The player handles the attack and owns its outcome. If he misses the attack, a one-time invitation can arrive through an established channel once the player has recovered evidence or an investigator has made contact. That route also works if an ambush never happens. Accepting the invitation leads to **Shadowspire - Outer Clearing**. If Vladimir approves the visit, he gives the player the guest key and can invite them upstairs to the study. The key lets the player return and explore the tower and its cellar portal at their own pace.

In his study he finally explains that Ardelis used his old diagram to summon a hero for its war, and why he thinks the first casting went astray. He can say what his own surveillance established and leave the next step to the player. The player owns disclosure of their memories and can take what they learned to Glowhaven, find another source or walk away. The last question is what to do with the discovery that the court is preparing another summoning. **Act III starts when that preparation becomes an actionable threat.** A refusal of Vladimir's invitation leaves an evidence route through a civic or scholarly contact.

### Act III · The weapon they wanted

**7. Stop the next summoning program.** Give the player an achievable objective against Ardelis. A proposed first operation is to find a relay, operator, or record that ties a new field trial to the court. The player can disable part of the apparatus, warn Glowhaven, protect people the court is targeting, or secure proof for the Great Sage. Which route they choose affects the resources, civilian exposure and evidence in the field test. Ardelis can make the breakthrough independently, including in a run where it never identifies or captures the player. A later, level-appropriate confrontation with actual **Ardelis paladins** remains an optional escalation if they have a credible personal lead. Those paladins are people who want the player alive for soul study. Preserve fight, evasion, surrender, custody, escape and negotiated release as distinct outcomes. Any actual extraction would be a further consequence of play.

**8. The second arrival.** Ardelis successfully repeats the ritual and adds command magic. It sends a stronger, controlled woman into Glowhaven as a weapons test. Her behavior under orders is neutral and eerily mechanical. A proposed scene opens near a city gate. She has orders to keep a breach open long enough to measure the city's response, and the fighting spills into a street used by civilians. The player can evacuate people, disrupt a command component, confront her or gather evidence. A late arrival can find witnesses and damage from an attack recorded in that save. The scene establishes the crisis and leaves its resolution in play.

**9. The choice that was yours.** If she survives and breaks free, the player can ask what she wants to be called, echoing the question from the cottage. She may refuse help, seek shelter, leave, or become a recurring rival with her own reasons to act. If control persists, the player can pursue its source. If she dies, witnesses and recovered evidence can still carry the political resolution. Resolve this deployment and the local part of the program. The larger war leaves room for later stories.

The previous blueprint's Vladimir Kujo name gives way to the proposed **Valkazathoth'dmir**, called **Vladimir**. He left the Ardelis court long ago and is comparable in power to a great Glowhaven founder. His wider reputation remains obscure. Older details such as immortality and his original reason for drawing the diagram need explicit reconciliation. The player's previous body, exact war objective and the precise interruption are open.

### Vladimir's role

**How he comes across.** During the delegation encounter and the private discussion, Vladimir has a fatherly gravity. He waits for someone to finish, asks the question everyone else has missed, and speaks quietly enough that the other people stop talking. That is the side the main story usually shows. In private he is goofy, indignant, unserious, bratty and childish. He pouts when contradicted and takes astonishing pains over something petty. Let optional visits and small disagreements show that side gradually. He might listen very patiently to an account of the failed ritual, then spend the rest of the evening sulking because a visitor called one of his prize pebbles ordinary.

**What he does.** He follows his own interests. He sends the soldiers away because they are conducting an inquiry on his doorstep, and talks about the diagram used by the court because it is his work. He keeps his own records. A guest key is permission to visit his home. Requests for a lesson, a rescue, supplies, an escort or help with the court get an evasive answer or a plain refusal. When the player needs help to progress, give them a civic, scholarly or witness route they can pursue. Vladimir stays independent even at high trust. He never joins the player's party.

**Psychic immunity.** His thoughts are inaccessible to mind-reading and telepathy. Psychic intrusion, mental compulsion and mind control fail on him. The cause of the immunity can stay private. The canonical damage list includes `psychic`. Prototype mechanical damage immunity through a dedicated NPC type used for Vladimir, keeping the status and mind-reading behavior in his scoped NPC guidance. Test each separately. Use the native party-membership check and removal effect as a narrowly scoped backstop if a normal recruitment or host action can put him in the roster. Test dialogue recruitment, direct companion addition, re-entry and reload, including his ability to remain in a scene as an independent NPC. [Official NPC state controls](https://voyage.io/whats-new/patch/patch:100001188) · [Community damage-type reference](https://wiki.unofficial.voyage/world/npcTypes) · [Community party trigger reference](https://wiki.unofficial.voyage/mechanics/triggerDefinitions)

**The rival's affinities.** The female rival comes from an alternate Earth without magic. Whether it is the player's Earth remains open. The proposed exception gives her all twenty current magical affinities.

| Magical approach | Affinities |
| --- | --- |
| Logic | Arcane, Metal, Gravity, Enchantment, Space |
| Passion | Fire, Lightning, Earth, Wind, Water |
| Empathy | Healing, Nature, Light, Summoning, Soul |
| Intuition | Divination, Void, Illusion, Ice, Sound |

Time stays excluded. Spellcraft and Magitech are technical skills. Affinity makes learning easier. Her actual training, mana, casting preparation and authored abilities determine what she can do in a fight. Give her a small recognizable repertoire with recoverable tells, plus optional Space only if she has the established Gravity preparation. Her disposition outside coercion can remain neutral while her opinion of the player develops through play. [Character Creation Format](../../../research/Character%20Creation%20Format.md)

**Working explanation.** The first ritual's interruption and the rival's command seal are separate problems. Ardelis could reconstruct a stabilizing line from subsequent experiments and test a separate command mechanism on the second subject. The proposed cloaked constructs use an engineered core that burns out when their custody safeguard trips. Their restricted behavior, finite range and real parts give their disguise and shutdown a common explanation. Keep the physical clue generic. Let a separate human source establish provenance so the investigation survives the loss of an attacker. ## Exploring Shadowspire

Vladimir's invitation opens a fully explorable tower and his private pocket realm for permitted guests. The existing woodland approach still feels like a place trying to send strangers in circles. On the first visit, Vladimir meets the party at **Shadowspire - Outer Clearing**. Once he grants permission, he hands the player a reusable **Shadowspire Guest Key** (working name). Its brass tooth swings into place near a warded path. A permitted bearer can walk through the exterior areas without being teleported by the wards, open the tower door and use the private portal in the cellar. On later visits the item works while Vladimir is elsewhere. Guest access is a permission from Vladimir. The Rebirth origin governs the Ardelis pursuit separately.

**From the forest to the portal.** Keep the existing forest, trail, clearing, pin and artwork as the approach. Plan a small connected tower with a threshold and stair hall, an upper study, and the Cellar Gate beneath the ground floor. The study holds the first private discussion about Ardelis and also works as a place to return later. Entry starts from the safe clearing. The key quiets the exterior ward before a guest moves through its passages. If a visitor approaches a warded crossing without permission, it can fold them back to a safe exterior spot. The existing world describes people losing their way and a sealed door. A reliable ward redirect, the key and the interior are work for the Rebirth build. Sports and hobbies can be ordinary interactions. Add a quest only if someone wants a concrete outcome and the player agrees to help.

**Access prototype.** Give the key through one actual handover after Vladimir approves the visit. ## Draft lore expansions

These are proposals for the Rebirth lore pass. The first seven are material a person can learn through everyday life, study or public records. The last is private to the storyline. Keep the existing national histories, magical foundations and reserved mysteries as anchors. [Current world lore](../../WorldAlpha%20-%20World%20Lore.json)

### Delegations between Ardelis and Glowhaven

An Ardelis envoy can request an audience, negotiate transit and ask a local office for help. What they can inspect, move or hold depends on what Glowhaven agrees to. A clerk who can confirm that a delegation was received may know very little about the questions its soldiers are asking in the countryside. Merchants with friends on both sides tend to care more about which roads and ports will stay open than about which court got the better of a speech.

### The Crown, its provinces and their interests

A royal campaign puts different demands on the kingdom's towns. Coastal offices count vessels and cargo space. Inland estates count teams, fodder and grain. Temple households look after people whose families have been called away. An Ardelis trader may be happy to sell supplies to Glowhaven at the same time that a court official is pressing the city for military cooperation. Give people on either side of a border reasons to disagree with their own government.

### Familiar work and experimental summoning

Ordinary summoners work with a defined call, a prepared form or an established bond. A teacher can demonstrate a small helper, give it a limited task and explain how to end the working safely. A construct following a short instruction, a companion acting by consent and a familiar returning to an established bond can look similar for a moment. Someone familiar with the work can ask how the bond began and what each side can choose.

### Pocket spaces and fixed thresholds

A rare prepared threshold can hold a fold of space around a local anchor. Rooms on its far side might sit above their own ceilings or share a door from different directions. A host can keep a return landmark fixed while the rest of the place is rearranged. Such a work belongs to its maker, its anchor and the conditions under which it was prepared. The Evertree's long-distance transit network has its own civic purpose, exceptional power source and permissions.

### Minds, appearances and evidence

A false image can be shared by everyone looking through a window. A working that reads a memory or compels a decision acts on a person directly. An academy examiner can separate those claims by asking what a witness actually saw, where the effect originated and whether another observer experienced it. Divination can supply a bounded impression, which a careful investigator compares with testimony or a record.

### How construct parts travel

A brass joint may have been cast in one town, fitted to a frame in another and replaced twice by the time an investigator sees it. A careful repairer can often tell how a part worked from its wear. A maker's mark, a distinctive batch defect or a ledger can establish more about its origin when that evidence actually exists. Standard parts circulate between workshops, including ones that have never traded directly with each other.

### Brookmeadow's working coast

The mill times some loads around the state of the road and the port's landing hours. Port cooks buy flour and eggs inland. Village households buy hooks, salt and sailcloth offcuts from returning carriers. On a wet market day the useful person is often the one who knows which wagon has room and whose stove will still be hot after dark. Give this trade enough ordinary business to be worth visiting on its own.
